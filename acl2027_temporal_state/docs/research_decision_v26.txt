V26 decision: do not submit the current method claim
6 October 2026

Decision

The one-day rework did not establish a novel method or a natural empirical advantage.
Do not submit the current work as a validated ACL method paper.
Do not run the prepared query-frame comparison.
Do not spend the projected eight to fifteen CPU hours on that comparison.
Its measurement object differs from the intended source-binding hypothesis.
The corrected source audit also gives insufficient motivation for that campaign.
These are research decisions, not observed failures of a proposed model.

Completed work

We froze sixty new natural questions before reading their contents.
Sampling used pinned TEMPO data and excluded six previously inspected questions.
All 9,959 requested source documents were recovered and hash-checked.
The diagnostic pools contain supplied positive and negative document identifiers.
They are known-document pools, not corpus retrieval results.
Original questions remain unchanged.

We built nested evidence packs using question-only BM25 selection.
All sixty questions remain in the accounting.
Fifty-nine packs fit the frozen native context budget.
Item 027 exceeds that budget without evidence for the revision arms.
Its execution failure remains separate from question eligibility and source availability.
No question or source was silently truncated.
An independent byte audit verified 1,426 rendered unit occurrences across both stages.

The pinned four-billion-parameter interpreter completed one authored transport test.
No natural model prediction, training run, or answer-level comparison occurred.
Real-data runtime operations only counted tokens.
Thirty-three focused software tests pass.
Those tests establish implementation checks, not reader competence.

The original experiment audit is complete.
Nine of thirty-five selected reference claims lack explicit current-role support in their released text.
Only five date-baseline contexts contain the requested subject-relation key.
The published accuracy margins cannot reach p<.05 under any compatible exact two-sided McNemar table.
The timestamp parser loses day precision in 151 complete Wikidata dates.
Correct ordering changes two selected current references.
These findings qualify the old claims; they do not create a new algorithm.
The original package remains unchanged.

The measurement failure

The drafted schema described the subject, scope, and time requested by a question.
The intended hypothesis concerns bindings of assertions inside source passages.
Those are different objects.
Additional passages can change a supported answer while leaving the question's interpretation unchanged.
Running the query-frame comparison would therefore be uninformative about source-claim revision.

Draft query references cover all sixty questions.
They contain forty resolved frames, two insufficient frames, and eighteen exclusions.
Their C0 and C1 frame judgments are equal.
These are assistant-authored draft references, without completed independent source-reference review.
They are not human gold or a validated reader set.
Their equality does not falsify the source-binding hypothesis.
The original protocol, amendments, annotations, and runtime receipts remain preserved.

The source audit

Reviewers then inspected the same frozen packs for source-binding witnesses.
No new questions, evidence crops, or favorable replacement samples were introduced.
A qualifying witness must concern the same source assertion before and after evidence expansion.
Adding an unrelated answer fact does not qualify.
Unknown-to-known information does not itself demonstrate an initially wrong assertion.

The first pass identified two limited candidates, items 026 and 032.
It preserved uncertain and rejected cases separately.
Case 026 adds information about a judicial decision's scope.
Its initial pack already distinguishes passing remarks from necessary reasoning.
Case 032 adds population details for maritime registers.
Its relation to the requested Isle of Man certificate fields remains unsupported.
Case 038 restates a distinction already present in the initial evidence.
Case 016 does not securely establish an unchanged focal assertion across the proposed clarification.
The second review checks eight flagged cases and confirms no clear witness requiring a new, task-relevant binding premise.

This audit does not estimate a population failure rate.
It does not prove that models cannot misread these passages.
It does not establish that source-binding revision is impossible or unimportant.
It says this fixed diagnostic does not presently justify the intended method campaign.
The prospective twelve-error and six-residual model gates remain untested and closed.
No source judgment can substitute for those model outcomes.

It establishes no method novelty, stronger reader, or held-out improvement.
A self-audit of one package does not automatically support a strong general analysis paper.
Compilation and page compliance do not establish submission readiness.

What would justify restarting

Start from a concrete source-supported phenomenon, not another architecture name.
Obtain unchanged source assertions whose entity, scope, or temporal bindings need additional evidence.
Require task relevance and exact source identities.
Keep publication time, event time, and validity time distinct.
Show that ordinary full-context extraction leaves useful, correctable errors.
Compare against a stronger extractor before attributing small-model errors to a missing method.

The fixed-anchor design in docs/source_binding_repair_v26.txt isolates one possible component.
It explicitly uses assisted anchor selection and cannot establish automatic proposal.
Its schema and references must be newly frozen before predictions.
The current query-frame references cannot be relabeled for that purpose.
No empirical result here justifies training this repair.

If a suitable phenomenon cannot be established cheaply, stop this method direction.
Do not fill the remaining day with low-value inference or call a polished draft a breakthrough.
