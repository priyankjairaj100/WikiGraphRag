Proposed distinction and falsifier
Do not claim that HC-RAG cannot resolve scope ambiguity: its structure or
generator may already do so. Our distinct empirical question is whether an
explicit incompatible-binding witness policy adds value when the candidate
evidence, structural representation, reader and all resource limits are shared.
Reject that claim if an intent-aware structural baseline or a robust generic
sufficiency policy matches it. This is a proposal, not a reproduced comparison,
proof of a new method, or claim of absence from other literature.
