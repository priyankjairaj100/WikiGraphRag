CANDIDATE AMBIGUITY AUDIT DESIGN v0.7

Purpose and frozen boundary
---------------------------
The existing Nike development graph has seven atomic mentions, one resolved
reading each, and an adapter-added unresolved choice for each. This pass asks
whether the graph omitted genuine rival interpretations of those same source
statements. It does not presume such interpretations exist. The audit protocol
and prompt were written before a fresh audit output or any v0.7 scores. They
are development protocol artifacts, not a preregistered benchmark experiment.

The audit designer read only the source request, repaired candidate graph,
repaired prompt/schema and source-protocol documentation. No questions, answer
labels, prior numerical scores or decoder-result files were consulted for this
design. The designer is not the fresh audit generator. This document must not
be included in the fresh auditor's context: it contains case-specific design
observations, whereas the frozen prompt states generic acceptance rules.

Invoke once in an isolated context restricted to those paths. Save the exact
raw JSON plus a receipt listing input hashes, visible task, model identity if
available, revision if known, and output hash. An unversioned conversational
audit remains unversioned; a fresh context is not proof of model-family
independence, lack of training exposure or annotation correctness. The source
request supplies full eligible context, including text outside the CEO scope.
Operational cutoff remains the existing development eligibility convention,
not independently authenticated historical first-public availability.

The audit output is a report and proposals, not a silently rewritten graph.
It must cover all existing mentions and may legitimately propose no candidates.
No alternative is admitted solely because its JSON differs from a base reading.
If any reading is proposed, or any existing representation defect is flagged,
a single fresh source-only adjudication is required before graph augmentation.
Its decisions and unresolved findings must be preserved; it sees no scores,
answers or decoder choices. The protocol specifies this conditional stage;
it does not claim it has already happened or that it constitutes human gold.

Key distinctions
----------------
1. Semantic equivalence. A role abbreviation, alternate evidence span or
   paraphrase does not form a new factual hypothesis. Exact core-semantic
   duplicates are collapsed within each mention and recorded in a lineage log.
2. Weaker information. An unbounded end includes a supported exact end; it is
   not a mutually exclusive alternative to that end. Likewise deleting an
   observation or replacing a specific modality with an unmotivated hedge
   does not create a genuinely rival reading. Bound uncertainty should not be
   discretized into arbitrary point candidates merely to enlarge search.
3. Separate assertions. Current incumbency and planned future departure can
   both be asserted. Their required separation into compatible modality
   records is representation work, not evidence of identity ambiguity.
4. Conflicting source assertions. The filing explicitly uses an October 14
   effective date for the CEO retirement; the press exhibit says effective
   October 13. These are separately attributed claims. Without additional
   linguistic support, the disagreement is not permission to offer both dates
   as readings of either statement, or to infer that either corrects the other.
5. Genuine alternatives. A candidate must specify both its supporting evidence
   and why its meaning cannot coexist with a particular base reading under the
   same interpretation convention. Full primary-source context must leave both
   readings defensible. An isolated phrase is insufficient if another clause
   resolves its referent or effective-time convention.
6. Representation defects. An erroneous old reading, unsupported relation or
   missing independent fact is reported separately. A needed correction blocks
   automatic augmentation; it needs an explicit new repair protocol rather
   than being added as a convenient competitor to the defective old record.

Case-specific source-only assessment to motivate caution
-------------------------------------------------------
Nike's organization, outgoing/incoming people and CEO role are stated explicitly
in these two documents. They do not obviously offer a factual identity ambiguity.
The filing's later paragraph says Donahoe will resign his positions and become
an adviser as of the defined effective date. That contextual clause constrains
an isolated inclusive-versus-exclusive interpretation of the preceding word
"until". A generic last-day shift should not override such surrounding text.
The statement thanking Donahoe for his CEO service supports a historical service
assertion; reducing it to an unknown end or adding a future endpoint would need
a separate semantic justification. The announced succession does not show that
the departure or appointment actually occurred by the report date.

These observations are design cautions, not the fresh auditor's result. No
source-grounded rival reading was inserted by the designer. A fresh audit can
still report an overlooked reading or a defect, but a zero-addition outcome is
plausible and must remain an acceptable terminal outcome for this pass.

Compute and union rules
-----------------------
The inherited bounded decoder interface supports at most eight mentions and
four resolved readings plus one unresolved option per mention. This audit holds
the seven existing mention anchors fixed and creates no new mentions. The upper
bound is therefore 28 resolved readings. Counts are caps, not required quotas.
An overflow fails, with omitted descriptions retained; no score, answer label,
or decoder preference can choose which supported reading to retain.

The protocol freezes exact semantic fields, canonical hashing, representative
choice, source-only adjudication, append order and temporary-to-canonical ID
mapping. Existing graph records remain unchanged. New links are never cloned
from an old reading; each must receive its own source-supported proposal and
admission decision. Unresolved is added by the deterministic adapter. Hash and
schema validity establish artifact integrity and representation compatibility,
not source entailment or semantic completeness.

If no alternatives are admitted, reuse the existing graph identity for the
pinned-scoring pilot. Report that this selected development case does not yet
exercise competing resolved readings. Do not call one resolved option versus
unresolved a multiple-identity candidate evaluation. Do not keep regenerating
until a candidate produces a favorable decoding contrast.

Consequence for the ACL research route
-------------------------------------
A zero-addition audit would identify a data-selection limitation, not refute
joint inference in general. The next corpus step should prospectively specify
source-only discovery criteria for naturally ambiguous roles, entities,
references and update types before inspecting answers or method comparisons.
Retain cases with and without ambiguity, record exclusions and uncertainty,
and separate source conflict from within-assertion ambiguity. A sample chosen
only after it produces a joint-decoding gain cannot support a general empirical
claim. Any observed algorithmic comparison still needs independently assessed
interpretations and updates, realistic candidate recall, multiple histories,
strong matched-budget baselines and uncertainty reporting. None of those are
supplied by the audit alone.
