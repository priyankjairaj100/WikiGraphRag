1. Source check and next probe

All twelve previously exposed filings were recovered with exact byte counts
and SHA256 identities. The 41 historical representative conflicts and all 87
broader full-binding conflicts were reconstructed. All 87 have compatible signed
rounding intervals, table/prose occurrences, a single context/unit ID per group,
and a uniquely most precise value. None has unequal values at equal accuracy,
mixed signs, or zeros. Recomputing 191 lexical/sign/scale values found no mismatch.
An assistant source review covered the 41 primary cases. This is not independent
human adjudication or full XBRL/DTS conformance. These counts do not establish a
conflict-resolution task. The portable replay reproduced the substantive audit.

A separately frozen six-history cross-filing probe asked for the earlier year's
revenue as displayed in both primary statements. All six reporting questions
were resolvable using typed facts plus assistant-reviewed statement structure.
Two displayed amounts changed: PFE and BRK-B. This is not an automatic baseline
accuracy measurement, and no cross-taxonomy semantic equivalence was presumed.

A second frozen probe checked those two explanations. PFE's statement footnote
and Note 1A explain the royalty presentation change. The displayed difference
is 1,057 million, versus a 1,058-million royalty row; the one-million residual
is preserved as compatible with reported rounding, not forced into equality.
BRK's arithmetic changes balance, but the bounded passage review did not establish
the particular causal explanation. Absence of an explanation in reviewed passages
does not establish its absence from the whole filing. The coverage gate stays
open/unresolved for that question. Neither case demonstrates a retrieval win.

Decision: keep these as source-grounded development controls, including a bounded
abstention case. Do not promote a dimension split or rounding duplicate to the
paper's mechanism. The current probe does not justify launching a comparative
text/table study. A task-matched natural reader gate still requires an admitted
residual task and independently reviewed evidence. The next source work must be
a separately bounded task-discovery protocol, with competent typed, precision,
structural and ordinary footnote baselines retained. Do not search until a win
and discard intervening failures.

2. Reader preparation, not reader competence

Prepared 100 complete and 100 designed-insufficient source-backed typed-record
controls, in 100 paired families. Fifty families test a scoped reported amount;
fifty test an explicitly requested same-filing subtraction. They span all twelve
filings and six exposed issuers. They are authored/program-derived controls,
not 200 natural text/table questions or human gold. The generator, strict scorer,
public selector/hash manifest and external reconstruction recipe are available.

The immutable large-reader model target, exact prompt, greedy decoding, arithmetic
and development thresholds were fixed before candidate generation. Runtime,
hardware, source/reference review and tokenized rendering still need a preflight
freeze. No inference ran. Passing these controls cannot open the natural reader
or retrieval-comparison gate. The old 2/8 score is not carried forward as the new
gate. See docs/reader_gate_v23.txt for the exact thresholds and handoff contract.

3. Proposer/verifier pilot and coverage

The new semantic module takes a question and observed evidence text, requires
complete scoped joint proposals, binds model outputs to the precise inputs,
validates observed-premise judgments, and provides class-recall, false-elimination
and emission-risk scoring contracts. Its 200-ID queue is aligned with the reader
control roster but explicitly lacks natural text and reviewed class references.
There are no actual proposer/verifier predictions or measured pilot metrics.

Coverage is a separate, structured source-scope attestation. Beam scores, candidate
counts and finite-map optima cannot clear it. The implemented authorization arm
requires an independently declared human audit; it is a bounded human-gated
diagnostic, not automatic coverage discovery. Audit identity and semantic truth
remain external validation obligations. Gold reference class inventories are
evaluation-only, and operational review cost must be charged. An automatic
coverage policy remains unimplemented and unvalidated.

4. Confirmatory design

Prepared prospective choices: 200 new issuer histories, six questions each,
an observed supported-answer gain of at least five percentage points plus a
positive one-sided 95% paired-history t lower bound, and a one-sided 95% exact
upper bound of at most 5% for any unsupported emission within a history in both
primary arms. The latter is history risk, not risk conditional on answering.
The t inference is approximate; this is not a claim of power for a true five-point
effect. One comparator/operating point, budgets, failures, missing outputs and
history-level analysis are specified. All earlier exposed issuers are excluded.

The cohort, human references and final methods are not frozen or executed. The
existing 89-ticker metadata pool cannot furnish 200 fresh issuers. A new source
frame and an independent reference process are required. Confirmation cannot
start on the strength of the typed controls or a schema-valid coverage record.
No registration or confirmatory finding is claimed.
