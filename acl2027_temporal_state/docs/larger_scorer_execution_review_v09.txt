Coverage and numerical checks

The audit independently checked the full Cartesian coverage of two models,
four fixed statements and two fixed label orders: sixteen records from
sixteen distinct research process IDs. Both models had all eight inputs
preflighted before scoring. Inputs contained 2,520–2,546 tokens including
the native template; every input retained a generation slot within 4096.
Every response reported cache_n=0, prompt_n equal to the full input count,
one predicted token and no truncation.

For each record the audit recomputed Yes-minus-No native log odds, sign
decision, literal-label mass and returned-top-256 mass. It recomputed the
order comparisons independently and matched all collector-derived values.
Every raw log probability remained finite, nonpositive and unmodified.
The fixed 1e-4 mass-accounting tolerance was checked for both probability
sums. Returned mass ranged from 1.0000000003291845 to 1.0000013969365011.
These small finite-precision excesses are retained and disclosed, not
clamped or renormalized. They do not justify treating the scores as
calibrated probabilities.

Native request/response settings establish the unconstrained pre-sampling
probability route: no grammar, logit bias or adapter; neutral repetition,
presence and frequency penalties; post_sampling_probs=false; backend
sampling disabled; and only the temperature sampler. The request uses -1
temperature and this runtime echoes its normalized value as 0.0. Returned
probabilities are from the separately documented full-vocabulary route,
before top-256 output slicing; they are not normalized over Yes/No alone.
