NEXT CANDIDATE SCHEMA: REQUIREMENTS FROM THE FIRST NATURAL PILOT
Status: design requirements for v0.3, not implemented or empirically validated.

The v0.2 natural pilot contains five retrieved SEC filing representations and
two selected histories. Independent early-prefix passes produced 13 and 14
assertions. A separate later-prefix pass produced 10. Their raw outputs remain
unchanged. This is model-assisted development, not a frozen benchmark.

1. Separate polarity from modality.
The existing enum reported/planned/negative/uncertain is not a partition: a
future departure is both planned and negative. Pass B put such negation in its
qualifier. The replay adapter explicitly excludes a qualifier beginning
"Negative assertion:" from positive occupancy, logs the loss, and does not
pretend that this phrase rule is a general negation parser.

Use separate candidate dimensions:
- polarity: positive, negative, unresolved;
- modality: reported_actual, announced_future, conditional, uncertain;
- observation_kind: effective_boundary, state_observation, report_only;
- temporal_precision: exact_day, bounded_days, year_only, unknown.
Status of an announced plan must survive answer generation. A future plan alone
is not evidence that the event subsequently occurred. Pilot questions explicitly
ask what the supplied records and their announced schedule support.

2. Distinguish report observation from state observation.
The current extraction field observed_on was used for the document/report date
even when the assertion concerned a future appointment. Do not pass that field
directly into decoder.Reading.observed_at: the latter means the factual state
holds at that date, not that someone reported an assertion then.

Use separate reported_at and state_observed_at. Both remain distinct from source
publication and operational source availability. An observation that X is CFO
on day t constrains an interval to contain t; it does not assert the appointment
occurred on t. A year-only appointment permits a bounded onset, not January 1.
Keep effective-start and effective-end bounds, exactness, inclusive/exclusive
conventions, source spans and derivation provenance separately.

3. Localize uncertainty.
The v0.1 memory treats unknown starts as key-wide uncertainty before an optional
end, and a later unknown-start observation can spoil an earlier answer. Preserve
that baseline and implement a separate bounded interpretation materializer.
Uncertainty over an October 13/14 retirement boundary should not create global
uncertainty about every date or automatically invalidate a subsequent occupant.
Do not infer a precise boundary just to eliminate uncertainty.

4. Extract aliases from the eligible source prefix.
The natural replay exposes sensitivity to NIKE, Inc. versus Nike. Alias and
legal-name descriptions must be extracted/cached from the same eligible prefix,
with source support. Questions are ordinary text and must never supply a gold
entity/key ID. Keep the old lexical matcher as a transparent baseline.

5. Preserve alternatives before scoring.
Each source mention should retain multiple supported readings or an unresolved
option, including first-day-out versus last-day-of-service where the text does
not settle the convention. An assertion of cessation and an observation of the
preceding occupancy are distinct mentions or readings, not an alignment error.
Machine agreement does not resolve an ambiguity absent in the source evidence.

6. Freeze and evaluate the complete pipeline.
Pin a reproducible extractor backend and cache input/prompt/model/config hashes.
Measure candidate recall and schema failures before training a scorer. Cache
identical unary, pair and null scores for independent, iterative/restarted and
joint decoding. The v0.2 exact decoder is an enumeration reference over small
components; no scalable solver, trained scores or natural joint comparison is
present. Its observed_at constraints already distinguish actual observations,
but polarity/modality and bounded endpoints still need candidate-level support.

Acceptance for the next bounded sprint:
- Round-trip planned negation and year-only or observation-only timing without
  invented days or changed polarity.
- Keep later source text out of every early prefix alias/candidate cache.
- Reproduce initial 37 raw extractions unchanged while retaining a versioned
  derived candidate representation, with no gold fields in inference.
- Query an observed state while retaining unknown appointment time; retain
  uncertainty only where the permitted interpretations disagree.
- Add source-supported product-lifecycle histories and explicit correction
  pairs before treating the first two leadership histories as representative.

Research decision remains open: these cases reveal representation and routing
failures, not evidence that global joint inference is necessary. The principal
ACL claim requires natural gains beyond contextual and restarted iterative
baselines with identical candidate information and measured computation.
