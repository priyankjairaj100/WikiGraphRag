Novelty boundary update, 4 October 2026
Targeted primary-source check; research interpretation, not an empirical result.
This supplements the v16 proposal without changing its frozen history.

1. Pair separation needs explicit attribution.
Golovin, Krause and Ray (2010), sections3–4, already formulate identifying an answer-equivalence class through costly tests. EC2 cuts weighted edges between hypotheses assigned to different classes. Their framework also reduces suitable noisy decision problems to classes over complete outcomes; they discuss the potentially exponential representation. Therefore our answer-changing-pair objective is not, by itself, a novel acquisition principle. The paper's logarithmic guarantees concern its specified hypothesis/prior/test model. They do not automatically apply to our incomplete observed proposals, learned utility estimates or jointly required source premises.
Primary source: https://arxiv.org/html/1010.3091v2
Conference source: https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2010/hash/1e6e0a04d20f50967c64dac2d639a577-Abstract.html

2. Correlation alone is not a theoretical gap.
Chen, Hassani and Krause (2017) study correlated, noisy tests and propose ECED, a surrogate-based policy with guarantees under their model. Merely adding correlated witness errors or saying that information gain can fail does not establish an advance over that literature.
Primary sources: https://proceedings.mlr.press/v54/chen17b.html
https://arxiv.org/html/1605.07334v2

3. Budgeted adaptive evidence acquisition is already a RAG baseline.
Wu et al. (2026), sections3.4 and5, compare static cost-aware selectors and a ReAct-style acquisition controller using a shared corpus/retriever. The controller chooses a tier or stops. Their limitations identify fixed candidates, original-query retrieval and no source verification. Thus the proposed work must isolate any benefit of source-relation actions and measured controller behavior; budget awareness or adaptive stopping alone is insufficient. Their simulated access prices are distinct from our measured token/runtime accounting; neither should substitute silently for the other.
Primary source: https://arxiv.org/html/2606.02245v1

Consequence for this project (our inference and prospective design)
Keep the source-reader pilot first. The relevant unresolved empirical question is whether natural retrieved fragments leave answer-changing attachments that a competent generic structural/sufficiency baseline fails to resolve efficiently. No current result establishes that residual need.

If that gate passes, compare three acquisition criteria on identical proposals, actions, verifier and reader: generic sufficiency, an explicitly EC2-inspired pair-separation adaptation, and dependency-aware lookahead. Charge proposal and scoring access, not only final evidence. Name the adaptation honestly and state where its probabilistic observation model is unavailable. A two-action lookahead is an engineering heuristic until a separate guarantee or decisive controlled result is established.

Keep positive support and proposal-coverage uncertainty separate from pair reduction. A finite beam becoming unanimous is not evidence that it contains the correct interpretation. Evaluate true-binding omission and false elimination over the full admitted denominator. If the conventional complete-evidence baseline solves the task, retain the tooling/diagnostic findings and stop the specialized retrieval claim.

No new algorithmic theorem, approximation bound, natural QA gain, independent human annotation or exhaustive novelty review is claimed by this update. The six existing issuer pairs remain development material.
