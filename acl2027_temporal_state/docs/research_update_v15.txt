WikiGraphRAG research update v15
4 October 2026

What is now implemented

The new component is a conventional typed reader of declared Inline XBRL numeric
facts. It keeps exact source anchors and reported concept, entity, period,
dimension, unit and physical-source aspects. Numerical conversion, binding
resolution and overall fact usability have separate statuses. A structured
lookup accepts explicitly supplied source/aspect requests. It does not interpret
natural-language questions or establish financial scope.

The twelve v14 filing objects were recovered after workspace maintenance with
all original content hashes and byte lengths intact. The fixed population is
23,651 nonFraction occurrences. No new questions, references, paid jobs or model
completions were introduced. These are inspected development sources, not a
held-out evaluation. The Berkshire filename/report-year discrepancy is retained.

Both execution outcomes are preserved

The initial reader rejected six ASCII-declared source files under its original
encoding profile. Its result retains the full denominator, including 14,778
missing reader occurrences. The remaining six files produce 8,873 observations,
8,838 exact agreements on the common numeric subset, eight nil pairs and 27
unsupported numeric cases. A successful comparison on that subset would not
justify claiming a successful all-source reader.

The separate v15.1 amendment accepts declared ASCII/US-ASCII only when every
original byte is ASCII. It never transcodes or replaces the original bytes. The
old reader, protocol and unfavorable result remain unchanged. The amendment
changes no transforms, reported-aspect rules, sources, lookup policy or numerical
outputs based on observed values. It is explicitly a post-inspection engineering
correction, not a pristine confirmatory run.

The amended run retains all 23,651 occurrences from all twelve sources. Reader
and reference normalize the same 23,519 occurrences and agree exactly on every
one. Both retain 16 nil occurrences and 116 unsupported transformations outside
the numeric comparison. The reference invokes pinned Arelle 2.46.0 transformation
functions through a separate XML/normalization adapter; it does not run full
Arelle DTS validation and is not independent financial truth.

Reported bindings resolve for 23,641 occurrences; ten remain unresolved because
of typed-dimension semantics. Combining numerical and binding requirements gives
23,509 locally usable normalized facts, 16 nil facts and 126 unresolved facts.
The 126 comprise 116 unsupported transformations plus ten normalized values with
unresolved bindings. These partitions must not be collapsed into a single
reader-accuracy percentage.

The fixed integration sample takes the first eight distinct eligible reported
keys per source. All 96 probes retain their originating occurrence and reject a
wrong physical source hash. Eighty return a unique reported value and sixteen
retain multiple equal-valued occurrences. Because requests derive from the same
records being queried, this establishes integration self-consistency only.

A post-execution descriptive comparison finds all 23,651 reference records and
all 8,873 previously observed typed records unchanged; 14,778 records are newly
processed. This is not a new prespecified endpoint or proof of general semantic
equivalence. Public analysis includes aggregate categories and hashes only.

Verification and its boundaries

The amended execution checks fact/context/unit anchors for all occurrences and
records 94,895 successful evidence-byte checks, including repeated dependency
checks. This is not a count of distinct spans. Separate-agent replay completes
763,240 checks with no failures for the amended attempt and 343,357 checks with
no failures for the faithful record of the initial technical failure. Replay
uses recorded locators and aspect masks, so it does not independently certify
all parser or binding semantics.

The amendment receipt records 610 passing retained/authored unit tests and a
separate replay of 68 previous reader controls against the new module. Those
68 are repeated controls, not 68 new tests. There are no natural QA predictions.

Taxonomy concept types, defaults, domains, period compatibility, unit restrictions,
financial correctness, rendered visibility and source-defined scope remain
unvalidated. Exact agreement and anchors do not clear these uncertainties. The
component therefore does not establish a generally strong reader, natural QA
gains, novelty or submission readiness. The v14 failed reader/dense upgrades
remain the recorded development outcomes.

Next algorithmic route

The v16 proposal in docs/algorithmic_next_stage_v16_proposal.txt focuses on
retrieving evidence that separates automatically proposed answer-changing source
bindings. It combines a question-to-aspect proposer, an anchored three-valued
relation verifier and dependency-aware two-bundle lookahead. These are prospective
components; the current witness selector still receives supplied judgments.

The decisive experiment gives conventional source-aspect filtering, generic
sufficiency control and the proposed selector the same source metadata, reader,
actions and total costs. It first measures complete-evidence reader competence,
candidate recall and false elimination on independently reviewed cases. A
separately frozen history-level evaluation must follow. Machine-tag assistance
and reader-visible text/table evidence remain separate tracks. If the strong
standard baselines remove the residual need, the specialized method claim stops.

The six-page working addendum is paper/typed_reader_study_v15.pdf, with canonical
JSON, editable generic TeX, a claim ledger and rendering/visual-QA receipts.
Reproduction commands and external-data requirements are in docs/reproduce_v15.txt.
The PDF is a ReportLab development document, not an ACL-template compilation.
