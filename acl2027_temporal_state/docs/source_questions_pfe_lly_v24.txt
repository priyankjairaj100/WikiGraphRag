Blind source reference pass: PFE and LLY, v24
==========================================

Role and boundary
The author inspected only the four immutable source reports and the frozen v24
question protocol. No automatic retrieval rankings, selected packs, prediction

Outcome: five supported; one partial; two unresolved; zero replacements.

PFE currency growth: supported. The 2024 report says total revenue and
operational revenue each grew 7%, at rounded precision. It separately gives a
$349 million unfavorable currency effect, approximately 1%. Operational means
excluding foreign exchange. This exposes a real definition/rounding requirement:
the equal rounded growth figures cannot justify a zero FX effect. The current
comparative revenue basis includes the explicitly recast royalty presentation.

PFE segment presentation: supported. Biopharma remains the only reportable
segment in both reports. Three operating segments in 2024 replace two in 2023,
and PC1/Pfizer Ignite had previously been managed together. More substantively,
Note 17A says Biopharma's earnings basis changed from January 2024 by including
manufacturing overhead and the described global ORD/PRD R&D and medical/safety
costs formerly in Other business activities. It explicitly says prior-period
segment information was reclassified to the current presentation. This source
statement, rather than differences between amounts, supports the relation.

PFE free cash flow: unresolved. Searches and cash-flow/liquidity/non-GAAP review
did not locate a company-defined FCF measure in the bounded report pair.
Disclosed operating cash and capital purchases do not authorize inventing one.
This search outcome is not a globally insufficient label or coverage clearance.

PFE stock compensation: supported. The table separately supplies unrecognized
pre-tax cost and expected recognition years for TSRUs ($270m, 2.1), RSUs ($214m,
1.8), PPSs ($107m, 1.9), PSAs ($40m, 1.7), and options ($4m, 1.7). No BPAs were
outstanding. The references use the 2024 cell for each award with the specific
award/year headers, row labels and unit label; the whole table is not required.

LLY currency growth: partial. Consolidated revenue grew 32% in 2024. The revenue
change table decomposes this into volume 27%, price 5% and an FX dash at displayed
precision; the source notes rounding. No explicitly defined constant-currency
or operational-growth metric was located. A volume-plus-price calculation must
not be presented as a separately disclosed issuer metric or exactly zero FX.

LLY segment presentation: supported, with a qualified negative. Both reports
retain a single worldwide pharmaceutical segment. The 2024 report adopts the
expanded ASU 2023-07 disclosures; its 2023 report described the prospective
standard's requirement for retrospective application. These statements do not
establish a segment reorganization or a substantive remeasurement of comparatives.

LLY free cash flow: unresolved. The report pair search and ordinary liquidity
discussion did not supply an issuer-defined FCF reconciliation. No FCF amount is
admitted. This does not prove that the company never disclosed FCF elsewhere.

LLY stock compensation: supported. The final sentences in the award discussions
give RSUs $485.1m over 23 months, SVAs $61.4m over 21 months, and RVAs $27.5m over
22 months; no remaining cost for the discontinued PA program. References retain
the date, award type, amount, units and recognition period in each sentence.

Both stock-compensation questions are typed controls: their amounts and periods
are Inline XBRL facts. A failed query parser would not constitute new research.
The supported definition/presentation relations may justify source-evidence
