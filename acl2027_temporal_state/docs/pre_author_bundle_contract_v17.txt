Occurrence registry and interface boundary

The registry retains EVERY fact wholly contained in any retained block, including
nil/unsupported and all duplicates. Same source occurrence appearing in overlapping
blocks is counted once with all block links; different occurrences are retained.
Wrong source hashes and conflicting copies of one ordinal fail. Typed contexts,
numbers and labels are not sent to authors. Visible row-label candidates are the
alphabetic source text in nonnumeric cells of the SAME row. Numeric cells are
identified by contained fact anchors across the whole bundle. Do not invent
"total" for blank rows, use taxonomy synonym expansion or generate question maps.
Facts outside table rows may have no row label; no semantic label is guessed.

Declared concept/entity/period/unit values are copied only when the frozen
decoder says that aspect resolved. The interface-shape witness text explicitly
labels them DECLARED SOURCE METADATA, not visible semantic support. Source hash
and neutral physical source ID are provenance. These copied aspects are retained
in the common machine-assisted track; they do not establish financial meaning,
legal identity, correct column attachment or taxonomy/default semantics.

consolidation, until separate question-free source annotation/review is complete.

never used to infer a missing population or default. All visible period/unit/
population verification flags remain false. This candidate is not an evaluable
complete pack and cannot bypass the pending reference/admission gates.
