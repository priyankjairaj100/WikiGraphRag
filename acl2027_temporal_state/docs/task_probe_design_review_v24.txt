V24 prospective task-discovery design review
==========================================
2026-10-06

Review role and timing
----------------------
This is an independent assistant-role design review, not human adjudication.
It was written after reading AGENTS.md and the v23 research update, cross-filing
probe, reader-gate description and prior-art update. No v24 source outcomes,
rankings, references or model predictions were inspected for this review.
Recommendations are prospective. The root investigator subsequently specified
the four families and budgets below, before this reviewer inspected any source
outcomes. The executed protocol is authoritative about final details.

Question roster
---------------
Freeze four question families across each of the six exposed issuer histories:
24 questions total. Preserve every planned question and its outcome, including
nonapplicability, non-disclosure, invalid premise, source-audit uncertainty and
baseline success. No replacement questions or extra issuers after observing
results. All twelve existing filings remain development material.

The four selected families are:

1. Reported versus company-reported currency-adjusted revenue growth. Ask for
   the latest year's reported growth, the company's disclosed constant-currency
   or operational growth if any, and the actual adjustment definition. Do not
   infer that operational growth removes only currency, or compute a missing
   adjusted figure from an unsupported formula. The question must distinguish
   a published adjustment from an analyst's constructed counterfactual.

2. Comparative segment presentation. Ask whether the reported segment
   presentation changed between the two filings, retaining explicit old/new
   definitions and source versions, and ask how comparative figures were recast
   only if the source supports that premise. No change is a valid result.
   Differing labels or arithmetic patterns alone do not establish a recast,
   business transfer or causal reason. If a numerical segment exemplar is added,
   fix its selection rule prospectively; never select the largest discrepancy
   after inspecting values.

3. Company-reported free cash flow and its reconciliation to operating cash
   flow for the latest fiscal year. The measure is fixed as free cash flow;
   do not switch to adjusted earnings or another better-behaved measure after
   inspection. Preserve the issuer's actual definition and every disclosed
   reconciliation component; do not assume a universal cash-flow-minus-capex
   formula. If the source does not report the measure, retain nonapplicability
   rather than constructing a reference from a generic analyst formula.

4. Unrecognized stock-compensation cost and the weighted recognition period
   at fiscal year end, preserving award type. This is a numerical typed positive
   control. Retain each disclosed award category, amount unit, time unit and
   the exact meaning of the reported average; do not combine distinct award
   types or treat a missing aggregate as zero. Baseline success is expected to
   be informative. An automatic baseline failure is initially a parser/query
   implementation diagnostic, not evidence of a new text/table mechanism.

These are authored natural-language research questions, not naturally collected
user questions. General financial plausibility does not establish applicability
to any particular issuer, answerability, difficulty or novelty.

What must be measured
---------------------
The v23 source-assisted statement and footnote review is an annotation-assisted
upper-bound diagnostic. An annotator finding an ordinary footnote neither proves
that automatic retrieval succeeds nor proves a new method is required.

Run an automatic baseline from the frozen question, source pair and permitted
metadata only. The selected comparison is BM25 with full tables, headings and
one-hop explicit links, versus a stronger query-decomposition variant with the
same evidence representation and budget. Competent typed filtering/precision
handling remains a necessary control, particularly for family 4. Disclosed query
expansion may cover financial synonyms and company-specific labels learned
without reference selectors. Report variants separately and retain their exact
returned evidence. Query decomposition alone is a standard baseline refinement,
not a claimed novel acquisition method.

The baseline must receive the same document access, preprocessing, runtime tools
and acquisition/rendering budget as the candidate policy. Charge expansions and
table/footnote closure; do not supply free answer-selected footnotes to one arm.
Use a shared atomic evidence representation and record both raw acquisition cost
and final rendered size. The selected primary budget is 4,096 rendered
whitespace-separated words, with 1,024 and 16,384 as frozen sensitivities.
These are engineering word budgets, not model tokens or full operational cost.
Do not select a favorable sensitivity after outcomes and call it the primary
result. Fix treatment of oversized tables and deduplication before execution;
any table splitting or header propagation must be available to both arms.

The primary screen measures required-premise coverage in retrieved evidence,
not model QA accuracy. A residual needs all of the following:

  - a valid, unambiguous question within the prespecified family;
  - a source-supported reference answer with exact source version and scope;
  - explicit, source-anchored premises sufficient for that answer;
  - an independently role-reviewed judgment that the baseline evidence misses
    at least one necessary premise and contains no adequate alternative;
  - an observed automatic retrieval failure under the frozen budget, not merely
    failure of a typed-only parser or a manually selected baseline excerpt.

Sufficiency and necessity judgments can be wrong. Preserve dissent and label
them assistant-reviewed, never human gold. Alternative valid evidence must count
as success even if it does not match the preferred reference span. A support set
is not automatically a unique minimum set or a proof that every span is needed.

Reference reviewers should receive full permitted source material and question,
but not method identity, rankings or model outputs while writing the reference.
A separate alignment review may subsequently examine retrieved evidence. The
source annotation itself has a fixed search/review budget and logs all passages
inspected. An exhausted annotation budget yields unresolved reference status;
it cannot establish globally insufficient sources or certify semantic coverage.

Stop/go interpretation
----------------------
Publish all 24 outcomes. The roster is a bounded discovery census, not a powered
comparison or an estimate of the prevalence of difficult financial questions.
If no answerable residual survives the competent baseline and reference review,
stop this track. Preserve baseline successes and unresolved cases; do not keep
searching within the same nominal probe until a favorable case appears.

A single reviewed residual establishes a concrete development target, not a
general retrieval advantage. Repetition across independent histories and a
shared failure mechanism would strengthen the case for a method. Any numerical
go threshold must be frozen before observing outcomes. A candidate acquisition
policy may be developed on these cases, with every earlier attempt retained;
gold span locators, reference answers and review labels cannot be policy inputs.

Before a large comparison, the candidate must recover required premises on
fresh development cases from natural questions, at matched total cost, and the
reader must pass a task-matched frozen competence gate. An annotation-conditioned
oracle can diagnose achievable performance but is not the candidate method.

Reader, coverage and novelty boundaries
--------------------------------------
The v23 100 complete/100 insufficient typed controls are useful software/reader
controls but cannot become the natural-evidence gate by changing their label.
An actual natural gate must use the admitted task, source-rendered text/tables,
reviewed joint answers and reviewed alternative-evidence checks. A deliberately
withheld premise can create a packet-insufficiency control if the removal and
remaining alternatives are disclosed and audited; it cannot establish that the
full source lacks the answer. References and input hashes must precede reader
predictions, with model, prompt, decoding, arithmetic and rendering frozen.

Candidate-class recall and false elimination must be measured against these
reviewed natural items; pre-parsed typed fact grouping does not test a natural
question proposer. Coverage remains an external source-scope decision. Scores,
beam exhaustion, a finite candidate list and failure to find another answer
cannot authorize emission. A bounded human/assistant audit must be described
as such and its operational cost charged; it is not automatic coverage.

The existing prior-art ledger already constrains novelty in cross-filing mapping,
complementary evidence sets and certificate-style stopping. Finding a residual
would motivate an acquisition problem, not establish a new algorithmic result.
A plausible contribution would need an explicit algorithm that discovers missing
source premises from observed evidence, an appropriately strong implementable
baseline, and supported-answer yield/cost/risk improvements after the reader
gate. No new theorem, automatic coverage guarantee or ACL readiness is asserted
by this design review.
