LLY primary-budget semantic evidence review v24
==============================================
2026-10-06

Scope and method
----------------
Reviewed the two source-supported LLY questions (segment presentation and
stock compensation), each under typed, lexical, structural and decomposition
retrieval at the frozen 4,096-whitespace-word cap. The eight exact pack hashes,
costs, block inventories and 28 claim-level judgments are recorded in
results/semantic_retrieval_review_lly_v24.json.

The reviewer verified the LLY source identities and all eight frozen reference
witness spans, then inspected every block of the complete rendered packs.
Typed cards were inspected in a compact view retaining all their values and
bindings, with shared namespaces/metadata displayed once. Repeated blocks across
packs were equality-checked rather than treated as new evidence. Neither keyword
search alone nor preferred-anchor inclusion determined the judgments. Exact
pack hashes, costs and ordered block IDs match the public retrieval result.

This is a separate assistant-role review of source authors' and source reviewers'
references, not independent human gold. Policy labels were visible. Necessity
adjudication was performed after retrieval and is explicitly supplementary;
the frozen reference file was not edited. These are evidence judgments, not
predictions produced by the proposed reader or retrieval method.

Segment presentation
--------------------
All three text-capable packs include the 2023 description of the retrospective
disclosure rule and the 2024 adoption/expanded-disclosure paragraph. Those passages
support the ASU disclosure change. They do not supply the company's earlier and
later pharmaceutical segment bases, which the question expressly requests.

The pack's generic rule about single-segment companies cannot by itself establish
that Lilly operates that way. Similarly, a numeric card's ReportableSegmentMember
label does not establish a single worldwide pharmaceutical segment. The remaining
pack material contained no adequate alternative for those missing premises.
The typed pack lacks the adoption/rule explanation as well.

This identifies a bounded question-level retrieval gap for the tested packs.
The absent facts are ordinary organizational/segment descriptions. This is not
proof that a stronger conventional query reformulation or section retriever
would fail, and it does not establish a new algorithm's advantage.

Stock compensation
------------------
The three text-capable packs each provide the 2024 RSU, SVA and RVA amount-period
pairs and the PA no-remaining-cost statement in ordinary program paragraphs.
Earlier-year material in the lexical pack is explicitly dated; it does not make
the 2024 evidence insufficient. Structural and decomposition packs also include
program headings and the stock-compensation note's award inventory.

The typed pack retrieves the correctly scoped three cost cards. Exact source
inspection shows that the corresponding 23-, 21- and 22-month durations are
ix:nonNumeric facts using ixt-sec:durmonth. They fall outside the bounded normalized
numeric-card extraction profile. This is an ordinary parser coverage limitation,
not evidence that typed information cannot answer the duration request.

The frozen PA claim combines a useful no-remaining-cost inventory statement with
the program-discontinuation explanation. The latter is extra reference detail:
the cost/period question does not require a causal explanation. Its absence alone
would not justify a question-level failure. Here all text-capable packs include
both components, and the typed pack independently fails on the explicitly
requested recognition periods. No outcome depends on demanding the extra cause.

Interpretation
--------------
The stock question is already answered by ordinary retrieved prose. The segment
question is a development case for missing organizational scope under these
particular ranks and budget. Neither result proves a certificate-policy win,
reader competence, natural QA accuracy, automatic coverage, or novelty. No
retrieval-policy rerun, policy change or model inference was performed for this
review, and all eight original packs remain unchanged.
