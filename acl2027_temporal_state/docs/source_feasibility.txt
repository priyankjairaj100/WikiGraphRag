An archival anchor does not mean the bytes have already been captured locally.

1. Starbucks, 2024. The announcement, archived press exhibit, 8-K, and later certification provide a future CEO start, an interim CEO, simultaneous CFO service, a board-role change, and a later restatement. The clocks differ naturally: the release is dated August 13; the filing was accepted August 14; the report cover references August 11; and the departure was effective August 12. The archived release cannot be used as evidence available August 13 solely because its enclosed dateline says so. This is the best first pilot history.

2. Nike, 2024. An archived 8-K and press exhibit announce Hill's future appointment and Donahoe's continuing adviser service. A later interview supplies a natural role restatement. The press account refers to an October 13 CEO departure, whereas the contractual description uses October 14 as the transition date. Preserve this difference: it may be a boundary convention or inconsistency, and it is not an explicit correction. Role departure and eventual employment retirement also must not be conflated.

That is an explicit editorial clarification candidate, not yet a verified before/after correction example. Obtain both historical versions before any correction annotation.
