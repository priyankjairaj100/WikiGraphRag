Decision: recover and retain the complete v1/v4 source pair, but do not admit it to the unchanged same-scope correction core. This is one history, not four independent examples. No propagation gain or benchmark result is established.

The four predeclared source attempts all returned HTTP 200 with no redirect. Their raw and extracted-text hashes were verified. The original paper is a 24-page PDF, the revised paper a 37-page PDF; each abstract is a separate bounded document. The arXiv v4 page's 34-page comment differs from the physical page count (the PDF ends at printed page 34 after front matter). All pages were examined through page-separated text. No old text was reconstructed. Both documents assert at the OPERA Collaboration level, while the individual author lists differ; the repository host is not merged with the asserting authors.

The decisive semantic blocker is directly observed. The original analysis has 16,111 events, consisting of 7,586 internal and 8,525 external interactions (v1 PDF pages 8 and 11). The revision has 15,223 events, consisting of 7,235 internal and 7,988 external interactions, and explicitly discards about 5% of preselected events with isolated earliest hits that could bias the result (v4 PDF pages 9 and 13). Instrument corrections, revised event selection and reanalysis coexist. Identical nominal years and an explicit document replacement cannot certify an unchanged observation scope.

Direct downstream corrections must also remain visible. v1 reports delta t 60.7 +/-6.9 statistical +/-7.4 systematic ns, corresponding to a relative-velocity excess of (2.48 +/-0.28 statistical +/-0.30 systematic) x10^-5 with 6.0 sigma significance (PDF pages 19 and 22). v4 directly supplies delta t 6.5 +/-7.4 statistical +8.3/-8.0 systematic ns and relative-velocity difference (2.7 +/-3.1 statistical +3.4/-3.3 systematic) x10^-6, explicitly describing compatibility with light speed within errors (PDF pages 28 and 33). The relative-velocity formula is printed in both versions. It grounds a dependency but does not create a non-direct correction case or authorize recomputation by the current model.

The unchanged 731278.0 +/-0.2 m BCT-to-OPERA geodetic baseline is an independently grounded retention example in the source text (v1 PDF pages 9-10; v4 pages 11-12). It comes from geodesy and terrestrial survey, not from the disputed arrival-time result. This baseline differs from the 730.085 km effective baseline used in the velocity conversion. Retaining it does not supply alternative support for the old significant velocity excess. The v4 alternative waveform analysis and late-2011 short-bunch TT/RPC results are distinct support routes for revised compatibility; they are not hidden or relabeled as support for the old exact result.
