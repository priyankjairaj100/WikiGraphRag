ACL 2027 REWORK: THE NEXT DISCRIMINATING STUDY
3 October 2026. Planning proposal, not an executed result or preregistration.

Research objective
------------------
Establish whether comparing assertions across document versions has a residual
scope-compatibility problem after adequate retrieval structure and competent
reading. A new solver is not the next priority. The current two histories are
development material, and the old nondirect-propagation route remains stopped.
Version routing, metadata predicates, table preservation and abstention have
substantial prior art. A combination of those components is not sufficient by
itself to justify a novel algorithm claim.

Official planning target
-----------------------
The ACL 2027 main call lists the final ARR submission deadline as 4 January
2027 at 23:59 UTC-12, followed by a separate venue commitment stage. The call
permits multiple contribution types, including negative findings and resources;
that does not make the present small pilot submission-ready. Venue facts were
checked on 3 October 2026 at https://2027.aclweb.org/calls/main/ and
https://aclrollingreview.org/dates. Commitment and acceptance dates were TBA.
No paper has been submitted and no commitment action is authorized by this plan.

Gate A: establish a usable reader and representation
--------------------------------------------------
Target: first completed technical gate by 17 October, subject to actual compute
availability. This is a planning date, not a promised runtime reservation.

1. Establish an executable stronger-reader path with a pinned model revision,
   tokenizer, runtime, prompt, output budget, price ceiling and durable result
   destination. The fresh Hugging Face Jobs read-only probe remains UNAVAILABLE;
   an advertised connector is not a working endpoint. No paid job was launched.
   Do not hide this by labelling an unversioned conversational annotation as a
   pinned strong-model result or by silently increasing the local memory guard.
2. Use source-sufficient development controls spanning monetary multipliers,
   fiscal versus publication years, old versus revised findings, alternative
   baselines, changed samples and genuinely insufficient context. Each requires
   reviewed evidence. Passing two tiny authored controls is insufficient.
3. Preserve the existing normalized and layout runs. Do not change their
   references, questions, scores or failed answers when constructing new tests.
4. Audit a conventional structural retrieval baseline before adding a new
   method. The detailed proposal is docs/next_retrieval_gate_v13.txt.

The result of this gate is a documented capability decision. If an adequate
reader with source-faithful structure resolves the observed failure, do not
continue presenting that failure as evidence for a new reasoning algorithm.

Gate B: source-faithful structured retrieval
------------------------------------------
Target: freeze a limited implementation and source-binding audit by 24 October.

Represent candidate evidence as original spans with explicit source relations:
table title, row label, column year, unit declaration, footnote and cross-page
continuation. An attachment must have source evidence; a nearby numeric string
or a convention in a different table is not enough. Record unknown attachment
when geometry/text cannot establish it. Keep extraction confidence distinct
from truth or answerability. Never fill missing cells by recomputation.

The primary comparison should hold reader, questions, candidate corpus and
actual input-token budget fixed. Count header/footnote/continuation text against
that budget. Include ordinary retrieval, version quotas, standard table/header
preservation and metadata filtering as explicit baselines. A proposed scope
compatibility mechanism must be compared against those simpler components.
Disclose whether history/lineage metadata is given or predicted. The v0.13
within-known-history setting is not a large-corpus document-discovery result.

Use source-sufficient, reference-assisted contexts only as a separately marked
diagnostic. They cannot become retrieval candidates selected using answer keys,
or be pooled with deployable primary results. A method that needs more context
must be evaluated as a budget increase, not a free inference improvement.

Gate C: fresh data and a contribution decision
---------------------------------------------
Target: decision by 31 October, then a frozen new evaluation plan by 15 November.

First review naturally occurring histories without model answers. Include
same-scope revisions, explicit retention, changed population/procedure and
unresolved comparability. Distinguish assertion time from document time, and
changed measurement definitions from changed values. Do not select only cases
where the candidate method wins or manufacture correction dependencies after
reading source outputs. Record source-access and annotation shortfalls.

Choose the final scale after the capability and source-binding gates. Increasing
the number of questions from the same two histories does not create independent
test data. Split by connected history/entity, freeze the split and question
rubric before prediction, and reserve a held-out set that is not used to tune
chunking, prompts, thresholds or scopes. Independent human reference review and
disagreement handling remain needed; model-assisted labels must stay labelled
as such until that review actually occurs.

The candidate contribution survives only if a comparison-specific residual is
replicated across histories under the adequate reader, faithful structure and
matched budgets. Reject or narrow it when rendering alone removes the failure,
ordinary metadata/structure matches the proposed mechanism, extra evidence or
oracle labels explain the improvement, or the effect does not survive the
held-out evaluation. A null result can motivate a broader analysis paper only
after credible scale and strong baselines; two selected histories do not suffice.

Gate D: evaluation, writing and release
-------------------------------------
Target windows: final experiments by 1 December, full draft by 15 December,
independent claim/reproducibility review by 22 December, submission package
ready by 3 January. These are dependencies, not reasons to relax evidence gates.

Report factual content, scope, grounding, completeness and abstention separately.
Preserve numeric sign, units and asymmetric uncertainties. Give both unsupported
assertion risk and answer coverage; a partial abstention does not erase an
unsupported volunteered fact. Use history-aware uncertainty only after there
are enough independently evaluated histories; do not bootstrap sixteen cells
from two histories into a generalization claim.

Separate retrieval coverage, representation fidelity, reader error, and proposed
inference value. Keep every runtime failure and output-budget failure in the
declared accounting. Report source-assisted and predicted-structure settings
separately. Perform causal ablations only where one component changes at a time.

Maintain editable paper source, a claim-to-artifact ledger, versioned protocols,
source/runtime fingerprints, tests, descriptive replay and a data-availability
statement. The v0.13 readable PDF is a working manuscript, not an ACL-template
compilation. Final submission needs the actual official template, authorship
and assistance disclosures, responsible NLP statement and user-authorized
submission action. Check current venue instructions again before submission.

Active-session working rule
---------------------------
Treat GitHub checkpoints as save points. Continue through preparation, actual
execution, error analysis and justified follow-ups when authorized and feasible.
Do not ask for another go-ahead after every small stage. Stop only at a material
external dependency, a genuinely unapproved action, or a completed substantive
work increment, and identify the exact next constraint. Do not imply that work
continues unattended after the active session ends.
