RESEARCH ROUTE REVIEW — v0.9

Status: independent recommendation, not a performed experiment. Reviewed only
next_method_gate_v09.txt, source_annotations_v09.txt, methods_draft_v07.tex,
results_update_v08.tex, challenge_selection_protocol.json and
claim_ledger_v07.json. No live v0.9 scores were inspected.

Decision

The immediate scientific gate should be a paired natural-source replay in which
an explicit correction changes the support of a downstream answer. Do not wait
for additional routine leadership/schedule annotations or a more elaborate
solver before testing this mechanism. The larger-scorer challenge is useful
execution diagnosis, but its four authored statements from two first prefixes
cannot establish correction handling, dependency withdrawal or joint inference.

The current evidence does not exercise the proposed mechanism end to end.
The v0.9 extension has zero explicit corrections. Its changes, planned dates,
co-holders and unsupported start dates test important semantic distinctions,
but not correction-dependent inference. The v0.8 HS2 correction establishes
a reported 81%-to-41% correction with narrower scope; the records do not provide
the original erroneous version or demonstrate a downstream dependent claim.
Docker's purported correction was removed on semantic review. The v0.6 natural
pilot supplied only one resolved reading per mention and selected no correction
or essential-support inactivity. Source annotations also remain uncompiled
semantic records. These are different deficiencies; a better scorer alone
cannot repair them.

One recommended experiment: correction-dependent support replay

Within the existing twenty-history development gate, freeze a bounded enriched
slice targeting eight independently grouped correction histories. Record the
admission rule and every exclusion before scoring; if fewer qualify, report the
shortfall rather than replacing missing dependencies with authored ones. Admit
only histories with explicit erroneous-assertion/correction evidence and a
source-grounded downstream question whose support changes because the corrected
assertion was essential. Admit an independently supported unaffected claim as
a retention control. Ordinary succession and schedule revisions are separate
negative controls for over-withdrawal, not correction positives.

Use frozen supplied-document prefixes. A claimed pre-correction information
state requires the actual earlier document version; an already updated page
cannot supply that state by deleting its correction. A current correction notice
can instead support a clearly labelled current-document test. Keep those designs
separate. Independently adjudicate the pre/post answer, correction target,
essential dependency and unaffected control before model-output inspection.
An inferential dependency must be justified explicitly; topical similarity,
chronology and RESTATES alone do not establish it.

Freeze source-only proposals, including unresolved readings and plausible target,
scope and modality alternatives, without questions or reference answers. Audit
coverage separately. Do not remove false alternatives using answer labels.
Map the proposals into executable candidates before claiming an inference run.
Unsupported bare retractions, partial-field edits and shared-role cases must be
reported as representation exclusions, not silently coerced into existing
whole-assertion/single-valued semantics.

Necessary same-source comparisons

Give every method the identical complete eligible prefix and proposal inventory;
the full-prefix reader may answer directly from the text. Pin its model, prompt
and budget and require supporting spans and abstention. Compare it with a
contextual candidate scorer followed by independent readings plus exact
conditional links, ordinary iterative revision, restarted revision, and exact
joint inference within the common finite limit. Share scores across structured
decoders. A direct reader need not inherit the decoder's scoring errors.

Cross the structured comparison with three explicit update policies: no
withdrawal; direct-target whole-assertion withdrawal only; and direct withdrawal
plus essential-dependency propagation. Keep temporal semantics and source access
fixed. This separates correction recognition, dependency policy and search.
Use the historical objective as declared; retain active-support suppression as
a known failed ablation, not a method success. Score objective improvement
separately from answer/evidence improvement.

Falsification and route decisions

At history level, a mechanism success requires the correct pre/post answer,
valid retained support, removal of unsupported dependent support, and preservation
of the unaffected control. Report paired wins/losses against each baseline,
abstention, proposal coverage, exclusions, cost and runtime. Repeated prefixes
and questions are not independent samples.

If no qualifying history survives source adjudication, this collection does not
test the hypothesis; stop presenting it as a correction study. If dependency
propagation produces no net additional history successes over direct withdrawal
and the strongest full-prefix/iterative baseline on covered cases, the slice
fails the correction-method continuation gate. A small negative development
result is a route decision, not proof that the mechanism never helps.

If policies help but exact joint inference supplies no validated additional
history successes over restarted revision under identical scores, there is no
observed motivation for a new search algorithm. If exact search raises the
objective while worsening answers, investigate scoring/objective failure rather
than optimize that objective faster. A positive result only motivates a larger,
independently adjudicated held-out study; eight histories cannot establish a
publishable effect.

Contribution limit

The project records already acknowledge precedents for temporal constraints,
joint labels/links and dependency withdrawal. Their combination is not yet an
algorithmic advance. Any surviving residual failure must identify the new
methodological claim. Verification task, only if that claim survives: compare its
precise assumptions and operations with the closest constrained-prediction,
truth-maintenance and belief-revision methods before asserting novelty.
