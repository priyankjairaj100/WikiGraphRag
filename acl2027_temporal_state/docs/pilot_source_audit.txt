PILOT SOURCE ACQUISITION AND ADMISSION AUDIT
Audit date: 2026-10-02
Scope: SEC archive metadata for two candidate leadership histories. This is a
model-assisted source audit, not an annotation of answers or human adjudication.

DECISION
The five SEC documents across three accessions are useful developmental sources.
Their archive identities and acceptance metadata are inspectable. Admission as
strictly verified historical source prefixes is not established by that alone.
Use a visibly labelled acceptance-based day convention for development, preserve
the remaining uncertainty, and do not count them as a completed benchmark gate.

OFFICIAL GUIDANCE AND ITS CONSEQUENCES
The SEC Webmaster FAQ identifies Acceptance Time as EST. It separately warns
that SEC.gov availability has no recorded first-publication timestamp and that
the often short dissemination delay has no guaranteed duration. The same FAQ
distinguishes the reporting period, official filing date and post-acceptance
change date. It also states that public EDGAR filing content can be accessed and
reused. These statements support retaining archive metadata and source text;
they do not certify a precise public-availability instant.
https://www.sec.gov/about/webmaster-frequently-asked-questions
Audit reference: turn61view0; relevant page lines 77-83, 332-337, 355-372.

The SEC data-access guidance says certain filings after 17:30 ET are disseminated
the following business day. It also documents post-acceptance corrections and
deletions. An accession is an archival identifier, not a cryptographic promise
that currently retrieved bytes exactly match the bytes served at acceptance.
The Nike and November Starbucks headers show no later change date, which reduces
one concern without proving complete byte history. The three inspected acceptance
times are before 17:30 in the displayed local clock.
https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-search-assistance/accessing-edgar-data
Audit reference: turn59view0; relevant page lines 46-51, 85-110.

The SEC website dissemination policy permits copying/distribution with source
citation and treats logos and artwork separately. Store textual evidence and
attribution; no SEC seal or artwork is needed for this research.
https://www.sec.gov/about/privacy-information
Audit reference: turn69view1; relevant page lines 147-150.

UTC DAY RESOLUTION
Do not interpret a bare acceptance date as availability at 00:00 UTC that date.
For these specific records, the next UTC midnight is later than acceptance under
both plausible readings of the FAQ's EST label:

Accession                 DST-aware US/Eastern UTC    Literal EST UTC       Next UTC midnight
0001193125-24-200724       Aug 14 21:10:59            Aug 14 22:10:59       Aug 15 00:00
0000320187-24-000053       Sep 19 20:16:42            Sep 19 21:16:42       Sep 20 00:00
0000829224-24-000057       Nov 20 21:08:06            Nov 20 21:08:06       Nov 21 00:00
All dates in the table are in 2024. These UTC conversions are calculations, not
timestamps retrieved from a contemporaneous dissemination log.

Next-day midnight therefore resolves the acceptance-clock day ambiguity for
these three times. It does not prove dissemination occurred before midnight:
the minimum margin is only 1:49:01 for the August record under literal EST, and
SEC gives no strict upper bound on delay. Also, a general next-UTC-day rule would
fail for filings accepted sufficiently late in Eastern time or held overnight.
Use source-specific evidence, not a blanket date increment.

RECOMMENDED DEVELOPMENT METADATA
Keep reported_publication_date, sec_filing_date, sec_accepted_local,
sec_time_basis, retrieved_at, source_uri, accession, representation and hash
separate. Assign a field such as development_available_on to Aug 15, Sep 20 or
Nov 21, with availability_basis = sec_acceptance_next_utc_day_proxy. Preserve
historical_availability_verified = false unless stronger evidence is acquired.
Do not relabel the proxy as measured availability or silently overwrite the
original public dateline. A copied exhibit and its parent filing share origin;
their agreement is not independent corroboration.

For strict admission, acquire archived dissemination/capture evidence bounding
availability before the selected cutoff, record any post-acceptance changes,
and acquire/hash the exact representation consumed by the annotator. A current
local hash fixes our observed representation; it cannot retrospectively prove
that the representation existed in 2024. If tools return normalized page text,
label it as a retrieved-text representation rather than original HTML bytes.
Quote offsets must point into that stored representation.

Preserve ambiguity in event-date interpretation separately from uncertainty in
source availability. An event date, report-period end, press dateline, acceptance
time and our retrieval time serve different purposes. More precise wording in
one source does not by itself establish that another source was corrected.

AUDIT LIMITS
This audit opened primary SEC index, header and guidance pages. It did not
download filing bytes, hash source snapshots, inspect a contemporaneous archive
capture, or establish a dissemination timestamp. Search results from third-party
sites were not used as authority. The research team must separately preserve
the source material actually given to the blind annotation passes.
