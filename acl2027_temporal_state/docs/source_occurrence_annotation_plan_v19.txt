Purpose and accounting

Review the planned fixed 326 retained bundle-occurrence entries without question
conditioning. This is an entry count across bundles, not a claim that there are
326 distinct physical facts. The frozen projection must provide the exact entry
set and per-bundle counts. Every entry requires an explicit disposition, including
entries whose locator, visibility or semantic attachment cannot be resolved.
No entry may be dropped to improve completeness or apparent support.

Review-complete coverage means that every frozen entry has been processed and
accounted for. It does NOT mean that all aspects of all entries are known or
verified. Unknown bindings remain null. Nil, unsupported, empty, dash and duplicate
presentations must not disappear from the complete population. The producer may
verify private category retention; the source-only reviewer must not receive or
infer hidden decoder categories from a visible empty or dash presentation.

Future question admission requires verification of the relevant requested
bindings under its separate protocol. This plan imposes no universal requirement
that every retained fact have every binding resolved. Source-visible currency or
exact-period unknowns concern the fixed visible projection only; declared typed
metadata is handled separately by the producer and reference workflow and is
outside this reviewer's access. No conclusion about future machine-assisted pack
answerability follows merely from a source-visible unknown.

Input and access boundary

Only after the projection freeze, receive the physically separated source-only
projection: neutral bundle and source handles, opaque occurrence/block/row/cell
handles, immutable entry and projection digests, original byte/DOM/cell anchors,
raw source text-node lexical strings, unchanged whole retained source blocks,
qualified source/raster locators, and separately hash-bound admitted literal
identity cards. Safe visibility cues and prior whole-view status do not establish
per-occurrence visibility; that remains a review task.

Do not open combined privileged bundles, typed records, canonical values, concept
QNames, decoder/context/unit/scale/sign helpers, hidden identity values, native
code or outputs, baseline prompts/scores, reference files, or prediction files.
Do not receive preassigned semantic row labels, question targets, rankings or
answer-oriented hints. Do not follow proprietary references to hidden facts.
Record actual role identity, input hashes and prior context exposure privately.
The source-semantic model role is not a fresh question-author role, independent
human annotation, or human gold. Do not claim blindness to prior public candidate
metadata when it was available in the role's context.

No new source search, dependency expansion, replacement table, question-specific
evidence selection, or registry mutation is authorized by this plan. A missing
required relation stays unresolved in the fixed context; it does not authorize
retrieval of a more convenient source block. All original source blocks, competing
observations, duplicate presentations and failed-attempt provenance remain intact.

Compact annotation schema

Batch header:
  schema_version
  plan_sha256
  frozen_projection_sha256
  frozen_entry_set_sha256
  expected_entry_count
  source_view_receipt_bindings
  admitted_literal_identity_card_bindings
  reviewer_role_and_private_exposure_record
  processing_counts
  semantic_support_counts
  independent_reconciliation_status

Shared witness record, scoped to one physical source and retained bundle context:
  witness_id
  physical_source_sha256
  opaque_block_handle
  original_whole_block_anchor
  original_dom_locator
  selector: whole block, original row, original cell, or exact local text span
  selector_text_sha256
  exact_source_literal
  source_and_actual_raster_artifact_bindings
  qualified_view_receipt_binding

An optional local text span uses offsets in the immutable projection text with
its digest and retains the original whole-block context. It is not represented
as an original HTML byte span unless that mapping is actually supplied and
verified. Shared witnesses reduce repetition; they do not imply shared meaning.

One entry record for every frozen bundle-occurrence entry:
  key:
    opaque_bundle_handle
    opaque_occurrence_handle
    immutable_entry_sha256
  disposition: reviewed, reviewed_with_unknowns, or unreviewable
  presentation:
    status: visible, not_visibly_exposed, ambiguous, or unresolved_locator
    witness_ids
    rationale
  attachments:
    aspect
    state
    literal_or_null
    witness_ids
    rationale
    alternatives_if_ambiguous
  unknown_reason_codes

Attachment aspects distinguish source row label or local prose clause, column
heading, reported entity, population and explicit qualifications, visible period
wording and temporal role, visible unit wording, and applicable definitions.
Do not collapse these into one blanket bundle scope. An entry can have several
explicitly sourced population qualifications without inventing a normalized
financial population or taxonomy dimension.

Each attachment state is one of:
  supported
  ambiguous
  not_stated_in_fixed_projection
  not_applicable
  unreviewable

Every state needs a short rationale. Supported relations need exact witnesses
for both the wording and its attachment to this entry. Ambiguous alternatives
must describe source-compatible attachments, not fabricated source facts.
Unknown values remain null. Not-applicable requires an explicit reason; it must
not conceal missing evidence. An unresolved locator cannot receive invented
source support. A visible word or nearby heading alone does not establish its
scope relation to every occurrence in the block.

Source interpretation rules

1. Inspect the presentation in its actual qualified view and complete retained
   source context. Preserve split punctuation, parentheses, dashes, empty strings
   and continuations as observed. Cite all needed presentation components without
   computing a sign, value, decimal normalization, scaling conversion or tolerance.

2. Bind table rows and columns through actual source structure and inspected
   alignment. Preserve original labels, column-heading spans, row/cell spans,
   heading groups, definitions and qualifications. Leave an unlabeled row
   unlabeled; do not manufacture a total or infer it through arithmetic.

3. For prose, bind each occurrence to its own clause and explicit referents.
   Ordered phrases such as respectively require the complete ordering witnesses.
   A paragraph containing several entities, dates or quantity kinds cannot receive
   one automatic scope for all occurrences. Split statements require all retained
   continuation witnesses that support the relation.

4. Separate the cover issuer from the reported subject. An admitted literal
   identity card identifies the physical report; it is not a default entity or
   population for its tables. Preserve explicitly stated subjects, business
   subsets, hypothetical qualifications, ownership relationships and reporting
   treatments. No empty-dimension-to-consolidation inference, legal-name alias
   conversion, financial default, or cross-namespace concept inference is allowed.

5. Separate temporal roles: observation duration or instant, report-end wording,
   hypothetical assumption date, recognition lag, disclosure date and other
   explicitly stated roles. A shared heading may require different readings for
   different rows. Preserve literal dates and duration wording; do not calculate
   new start dates, infer fiscal labels from calendar dates, shift an observation
   period by a reporting lag, or infer chronology from filenames.

6. Cite visible unit wording and its scope, including a symbol, named currency,
   scale or per-unit exception only when explicitly supported. Do not treat an
   accounting framework, cover jurisdiction, familiar issuer or dollar symbol as
   proof of an otherwise unstated currency. Do not inspect private unit helpers.

7. Preserve repeated occurrences and all bundle memberships. Physical identity
   may be checked through frozen handles and exact anchors, never through equal
   numbers or similar labels. Do not transfer meanings across bundles merely
   because they contain the same occurrence. Each bundle must expose the required
   witnesses for its own attachment. Cross-source concept correspondence is a
   separate unperformed task unless explicitly assigned under another frozen plan.

Workflow after freeze and root approval

Verify the exact frozen entry set and all required input digests before starting.
If the projection population differs from the planned frozen population, report
the discrepancy without silently changing the denominator or dropping entries.
For every entry, inspect its presentation, retained context and applicable
witnesses; record supported relations or explicit unknowns. Preserve unresolved
locator and visibility cases in the ledger. Reconcile exact physical duplicates
mechanically while retaining every per-bundle disposition and context boundary.

Freeze the initial annotation ledger before any independent reconciliation.
Retain initial disagreements, corrections and unresolved relations as separate
records; never silently overwrite an earlier favorable or unfavorable judgment.
No question, reference answer, evaluated prediction or baseline performance may
condition the initial review or reconciliation. A later author receives only the
separately approved author projection, not an unreviewed semantic ledger or
privileged occurrence projection through convenience or shared file access.

Completion and reporting

Check exact key-set equality: every expected entry appears once in the annotation
ledger, with no extra, missing or substituted entry. Validate that every witness
resolves inside the unchanged fixed context and matches its bound source/text
digest. Report processed/expected entries and dispositions separately from the
number of supported, ambiguous, missing, inapplicable and unreviewable relations.
These counts do not by themselves admit any fact, bundle, evidence pack or task.

Keep source text, lexical quantities, identity values, detailed scope annotations
and reconciliation material external in newly versioned private review artifacts.
Public receipts contain only source-free counts, statuses, hashes, locators and
bounded methodological explanations. Preserve previous artifacts and report
model-role dependence and available annotation cost separately. This draft
authorizes no annotation execution before the reviewed projection is frozen.
