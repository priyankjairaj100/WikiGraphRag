ACL 2027 REWORK: CHECKPOINT v0.3
Work date: 2026-10-02
Focus: source-supported bounded temporal representation and controlled repair.

Outcome
The revised bounded materializer handles all nine known development questions
under their announced-schedule interpretation. A and B repeat seven early-prefix
questions independently; L covers two later-prefix questions. These are repairs
of inspected examples, not a held-out test or proof of joint-inference benefit.

Implementation
- Separate positive/negative polarity from actual/planned/conditional/uncertain
  modality. An announced departure no longer becomes positive occupancy.
- Distinguish report dates from actual state observations. An observation
  constrains a continuous interval to contain that date without inventing the
  appointment day. Exact, bounded, year-only and unknown endpoints are retained.
- Compute possible/necessary interval membership analytically. Endpoints use
  inclusive possible-date bounds; the occupancy interval itself is half-open.
- Keep factual queries separate from announced-schedule queries. Actual mode
  excludes plans and projections beyond the report horizon. Invalid future-report
  metadata cannot bypass source eligibility. No indefinite forward persistence.
- Route through source-supported aliases from the eligible prefix. Colliding
  aliases stay ambiguous instead of silently merging organizations.
- Preserve raw extractions, old memory/evaluation code and unchanged questions
  and expectations. An archive comparison verifies 24 prior input/legacy files.

Source-only semantic migration
Fresh model contexts separately received the early A, early B and late L source
prefixes and their own raw extraction. No questions, labels, predictions or other
passes were supplied. Migration created 39 claims from all 37 raw assertions;
A split two occupancy/departure readings. There are 20 source-supported alias
records and 89 claim evidence spans. Exact input/prompt/output hashes, change
logs and unedited outputs are retained. This is additional semantic model work,
not merely renaming fields, and not human adjudication.

The observations ablation shows why a reported current role cannot simply be
represented as an unknown appointment. The endpoint ablation loses announced
transition timing. Alias effects differ across A/B because their subject labels
differ. These are diagnostic observations on known cases, not generalization.

The exact-date baseline also assumes open-end persistence, while the bounded
method assumes a connected selected interval without forward persistence. Its
projection necessarily loses non-exact endpoints, observations and negative
claims. This contrast measures the implemented representation/materialization
bundle. It does not isolate a new decoder or provide a fair comparison to every
possible strong temporal baseline. The within-v0.3 controls share semantic
migration; v0.2-v0.3 differences also include that added model interpretation.

Verification and review
140 unit tests pass, including 43 bounded-materializer and 12 migration/routing
tests, plus the existing 48 decoder diagnostic checks. A separate code reviewer
checked 4,896 finite interval configurations without finding a membership-rule
mismatch. Review found and fixed lost context citations, future-report metadata
leakage, understated episode-continuity assumptions and conflation of unresolved
update links with uncertain factual occupancy. See semantic_review_v03.txt.

Remaining methodological limits
- No joint propagation across all claims' temporal constraints. The per-claim
  conservative engine may abstain where a global consistency model could resolve
  a later competing unknown-start observation.
- Unresolved polarity and per-endpoint modality remain unsupported. Whole-claim
  modality plus factual projection guards are a partial implementation.
- Multiple observations in one claim assume a continuous episode. Do not merge
  separated observations across a possible reversion without evidence.
- Corrections are explicitly rejected by the new materializer. The existing
  small decoder's correction links still need compatible downstream semantics.
- No learned shared candidate scores, full candidate-recall study, scalable
  joint solver or natural joint-versus-iterative comparison exists yet.
- Five current source representations from two selected histories remain
  development-only; strict historical version/availability admission is pending.
- Perfect repairs of examples used to design the method cannot establish novelty,
  statistical significance, robustness or ACL acceptance prospects.
