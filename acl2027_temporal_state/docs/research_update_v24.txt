V24: bounded question probe and automatic retrieval audit
2026-10-06

Decision
--------
This probe does not establish a publishable method advantage. Conventional
structural retrieval and query decomposition each supply question-sufficient
evidence for seven of the nine source-supported complete questions at the
primary 4,096-word cap. The Lilly segment question is missing evidence under
all four frozen policies. Pfizer's segment question is missing under the two
structural policies but uncertain under lexical retrieval. Conservatively,
only one history is a confirmed residual against all four implementations.
The two-history expansion rule therefore is not cleared. Stop this bounded
probe at its declared 24 questions; do not add cases until a desired count wins.

The frozen protocol said two independently reviewed, source-supported residual
candidates across two histories before expansion. Its candidate language did
not predefine a full all-policy semantic adjudication algorithm. The present
decision deliberately uses a conservative interpretation: an uncertain outcome
under one conventional policy is not a successful residual admission. This
interpretation is recorded after retrieval, not represented as a prospectively
registered statistical test. The PFE case remains a useful unresolved candidate.

The next engineering priorities are ordinary question planning/source-time
coverage and duration representation. They are controls to repair before any
new-method claim. No certificate acquisition policy was run in this probe.
Dense/hybrid retrieval, semantic query rewriting and a strong LLM query planner
were not compared; the observed misses do not establish their failure. A task
expansion or different family needs a new bounded protocol and explicit rationale.

What was completed
------------------
Before source-question outcomes, four question templates were fixed across six
issuer histories: reported versus currency-adjusted growth, segment presentation
and comparative recasting, issuer-defined free cash flow, and unrecognized stock
compensation. All 24 questions were retained. These are prospectively authored
natural-language templates, not organically collected user questions. The same
twelve already-exposed filings remain development material, with exact source
byte identities; Berkshire filename years are not the actual filing fiscal years.

Separate assistant roles authored source references blind to retrieval outcomes,
then cross-reviewed them. Nine questions are supported, ten partial and five
unresolved. No status changed in source review. One wording repair and one
alternative-witness repair were applied in a separate projection, together with
eleven alternative witness sets and one explicit derived claim already present
in an answer summary. The original annotations/reviews remain intact. The final
projection has 67 claims and 265 verified witness-anchor occurrences. It is not
independent human gold. Partial/unresolved questions are not certified globally
insufficient questions and cannot fill the negative half of a reader gate.

The retrieval author had no access to reference answers or gold selectors. Four
conventional policies ran over each item's two admitted filings at 1,024, 4,096
and 16,384 rendered whitespace-word caps: typed-card lexical ranking, BM25,
BM25 plus structural context, and generic clause-query fusion plus the same
structural context. All headers, repeated text and cards are charged. Indexing
accesses all 62,730,877 source bytes; output-word limits are neither LLM-token
counts nor complete acquisition/computational cost. Source-date routing differs
between lexical and the other policies, so this is not a clean closure ablation.

The full run completed all 288 cells. Thirty-six primary-budget packs, covering
all nine complete questions and all four policies, received a full semantic
evidence review in separate assistant roles. Equivalent typed bindings, visible
table fragments and alternative source passages were credited. Method identities
were visible during this review. These are evidence judgments, not model answers.

Primary results: denominators and distinct measurements
-------------------------------------------------------
Policy          Exact preferred       Strict reference       Actual-question
                witness coverage      semantic sufficiency   sufficiency
typed           0/9*                  0/9                    0/9
lexical         3/9                   3/9                    4/9 (+1 uncertain)
structural      4/9                   5/9                    7/9
decomposition   4/9                   5/9                    7/9

* Typed cards intentionally have no raw-text coverage intervals, so their zero
  exact-witness score is an interface property and is not a semantic score.
  The separate semantic review checked the cards' actual fields and limitations.

The nine-question denominator is the supported subset of the complete 24-question
source probe. This table is descriptive, with repeated questions within issuer
histories; it is not a held-out accuracy estimate, an independent-question
significance test, or a comparison with the proposed method.

The distinction between the last two columns matters. The Pfizer currency
reference included extra royalty-reclassification history; the stock-compensation
reference included a zero-outstanding BPA detail. Requiring those extra details
would create failures even where the actual questions could be answered. Both
the frozen conjunction and the post-retrieval question-necessity judgments are
released. No reference was silently weakened or overwritten. SLB's reconciliation
also has a valid alternative evidence chain through the cash-flow statement,
which an exact preferred-anchor match alone missed.

Source findings and ordinary baseline limitations
-------------------------------------------------
Pfizer genuinely changed the allocation of costs to segment earnings and
explicitly reclassified comparative segment information while keeping the
Biopharma reportable-segment name. Lilly's source describes the single-segment
business and adoption of expanded segment disclosures. These relations are
source-backed. Automatic retrieval of all needed premises remains incomplete
in some packs, but retrieving their ordinary organization/note prose is not
itself evidence for a new certificate algorithm.

Both supported FCF questions and all four supported stock-compensation questions
are answered by the evidence in the structural/decomposition packs. The Pfizer
currency question also has sufficient evidence under all three text-capable
policies. Conventional text retrieval therefore solves seven of nine cases.

The typed policy is deliberately bounded: normalized numeric cards, exact-binding
duplicate/precision handling, source-year routing and lexical concept ranking.
It is not a complete natural-question-to-aspect interpreter. In Lilly's stock
question, the correct costs were retrieved but durations are ix:nonNumeric
durmonth facts outside the numeric-card profile. That representation omission
and weak concept ranking are engineering limitations, not semantic impossibility
or novel-task evidence. Relevant prose already resolves the duration question.

Reproducibility and preserved attempts
--------------------------------------
A coordination race launched an initial frozen attempt just before a requested
blind implementation review. It was interrupted after four pack/decision pairs,
before any output contents were inspected by the retrieval author. The original
freeze, code, tests, hashes and interruption record are retained. Independent
authored fixtures exposed visibility, link expansion, table-header and precision
errors. The amended full run followed a new freeze and passed the review.

A code-only portability fixture exposed last-bit floating-score differences from
Python set iteration. The completed run was preserved. A separate environment
freeze preceded an otherwise unchanged PYTHONHASHSEED=0 replay. All 288 ordered
selection lists, pack hashes, source-interval unions and word counts matched
exactly. All 576 actual packs were validated. This does not establish arbitrary
cross-version/platform/seed invariance. Use the documented fixed seed for replay.

Indexing took 32.88 seconds; original retrieval 293.95 seconds; seed-zero retrieval
287.03 seconds in this environment. These are observed engineering timings, not
a controlled system-performance study. Twenty focused authored software tests
pass. Metadata and arithmetic replay do not re-adjudicate source semantics.

The two large result JSONs are released losslessly compressed, with compressed
and exact uncompressed SHA256 identities. The included unpacker restores their
original bytes and refuses to overwrite a different file. Full source/index/pack
text stays external; selectors, hashes, references, reviews, code and results
are included in Git. See docs/reproduce_v24.txt.

ACL 2027 position and next work
-------------------------------
The v21 conditional theory remains complete, but a new-method advantage, novelty
and a held-out natural result remain unestablished. This checkpoint supplies an
auditable empirical diagnosis and prevents overclaiming a gap from weak controls
or unnecessary reference details. It does not finish the research paper.

The natural 100-complete/100-insufficient reader gate, actual proposer/verifier
class-recall and false-elimination pilot, externally validated automatic coverage,
and new-issuer confirmatory study remain pending. The v23 typed controls remain
prepared, not a substitute for the natural gate. No new model predictions ran.
The reader/comparison gates remain closed, and a large-model campaign is not
authorized by these results. Future work must first repair the ordinary baseline
limitations and resolve or replace the task hypothesis under a new bounded plan.
