V19 initial source-only annotation coordination schema
Frozen plan: docs/source_occurrence_annotation_plan_v19.txt SHA a0aa078de05d7e6d163dee2f67fcc1630cd5f9527de39e369b88b418db08815d.
This schema only coordinates serialization. It does not supply semantic answers.

Write each assigned bundle as <bundle_handle>.initial.json in your assigned role directory.
Top-level required keys:
 schema_version: source_occurrence_initial_annotations_v19
 bundle_handle, source_handle, source_sha256
 plan_sha256, projection_sha256, reviewer_role
 status: initial_review_frozen_pending_reconciliation
 author_release_allowed: false
 entries: list, exactly one per packet occurrence in packet order
 witnesses: list of exact source witness records
 view_inspections: list of actual raster-page inspection records

Entry required keys:
 key: {bundle_handle, occurrence_handle, entry_sha256}
 disposition: reviewed | reviewed_with_unknowns | unreviewable
 presentation: {status: visible | not_visibly_exposed | ambiguous | unresolved_locator,
                literal: original decoded lexical string unchanged,
                witness_ids: [...], rationale: ...}
 attachments: list of relations below
 unknown_reason_codes: list

Required relation aspects: row_or_clause, column, reported_entity, population, period, unit.
Add currency_identity and period_exactness when helpful to separate explicitly visible
unit/period wording from an unknown national currency or exact period boundaries.
Other optional aspects: classification, definition, temporal_role, qualifier.
Each relation: {aspect, state, literal: exact source wording or null,
               witness_ids: [...], rationale, alternatives: []}
States: supported | ambiguous | not_stated_in_fixed_projection | not_applicable | unreviewable.
All states require a rationale. Supported requires exact source witnesses. Unknown literal
is null, not a guessed label. Not-applicable needs a source-grounded reason. For prose,
column is not_applicable if the exact quantity is not in a table; bind local clause instead.
Unit records literal displayed unit/scale only; currency_identity separately retains any
unknown national currency. Period records reported wording/role; period_exactness may remain
unknown without claiming a future declared-aspect pack is unanswerable.
Do not add canonical values, signs, normalized currencies/dates, concept QNames, helpers or
new financial labels. Do not invent total for unlabeled rows, group by equal values, inherit
cover issuer as every reported entity, or transfer meanings across bundles.

Witness record: {witness_id, source_sha256, block_handle, block_id, anchor, dom_path,
 selector: {kind: whole_block | row | cell | text_span, row_index?, cell_index?,
            text_start?, text_stop?}, exact_text, text_sha256,
 raster_refs: [{path,bytes,sha256,page_index}], ...optional safe occurrence locator...}
Whole block anchor/DOM and full unchanged context stay available through the packet. Text
span offsets are in packet text, not HTML byte offsets. Row/cell witnesses may cite their
parent whole-block anchor plus original row/cell indexes. Identity witnesses can separately
reference only packet admitted-card path/hash and its literal source witnesses.

Inspect all packet closed qualified pages for meaningful source context, reusing a previously
personally inspected identical physical raster within your assignment only if logged exactly.
Log each unique actual page/path/hash and which bundle/block links use it. Do not claim an
image was inspected from a hash/readback alone. All literal cells/clauses and numbers must be
checked against actual pages. Keep raw text/quantities and semantic annotations private.

Per-role exposure.json: exact safe inputs/hashes, role, prior context, image pages actually
inspected, no forbidden artifact reads, no questions/references/predictions/normalization,
no registry mutation, scope limitations. Per-role receipt.json: only counts/status/hashes/
locators. Freeze ledger before reconciliation, preserving unknowns. Reviewer coverage is all
assigned entries disposed, not all bindings resolved. No admission or author release.

Allowed inputs only: frozen plan; safe projection inventory/terminal; assigned new projection
packets; direct closed PNG/PDF locators and separate admitted-card references inside packets;
this schema. Do not navigate into referenced full inventories/receipts, combined bundles,
typed records, producer joins, prior source-semantic annotations or native/baseline outputs.
One packet/view at a time; no whole-source parsing, search or context expansion.
