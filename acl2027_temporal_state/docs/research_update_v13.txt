What actually ran
-----------------
Two authored native reading controls passed. All sixteen primary and sixteen
source-layout follow-up requests then completed, with a fresh guarded process
per response. There were 34 completions, 37 processes including preflights, zero
native output-budget failures and no response repairs. Main and layout batches
took 24.44 and 26.97 minutes respectively, including setup and verification;
these are descriptive timings, not an efficiency comparison.

Four existing PDF representations were indexed: 550 pages and 2,306 chunks across
two histories. Two missing OPERA v1 raster tables were recovered with visibly
checked model-assisted transcriptions. The corpus is not certified globally
OCR-complete. Eight question/reference pairs were frozen before predictions.
History identity and version membership are supplied. No new corpus discovery,
historical availability certification or independent benchmark admission occurs.

Why the errors matter
---------------------
Context audits find complete support for only 2/8 questions in each condition.
Ordinary contexts have 4 partial and 2 absent cases; paired contexts have 5 partial
and 1 absent. Layout makes one financial period binding clearer but does not change
case-level completeness. Missing year/unit headers and omitted required passages
are retrieval/representation failures. On the completely supported OPERA revised
result, the small reader still gives wrong version/quantity associations or a
wrong compatibility conclusion. This is a reader failure under available facts.

References and grading are model-assisted with shared development familiarity.
Method labels are masked, not guaranteed statistically blind. One explicit
adjudication applies the frozen rule requiring identified approximate rounding:
a layout answer asserting 22.0 million without qualification changes from content
correct to incomplete/ambiguous. Its original lenient grade remains in
paired_study_initial_v13.json; final overlay and counts are separate. Under the
lenient sensitivity layout-paired content is 3/8; joint remains 1/8 either way.
No native answer, initial grade or reference is overwritten.

Decision
--------
No paired-version retrieval or layout joint-success benefit is demonstrated in
these selected cases. Do not treat this as proof either technique cannot help
elsewhere. The pilot does not justify a new solver, a strong-reader failure
claim, generalization or ACL readiness. The earlier nondirect-propagation route
remains stopped under its prior source-admission rule.

The next gate is source-bound structural retrieval plus an adequate pinned
reader, including conventional header/table and metadata baselines. Only then
should fresh histories and independent evaluation test a residual scope-comparison
contribution. Eight bounded literature searches across the initial and follow-up
audits find substantial prior work on version routing, scope constraints, tables
and abstention. TIDE is verified only through author project documentation.
Novelty remains unestablished. See next_retrieval_gate_v13.txt and
acl2027_execution_plan_v13.txt for the concrete dependency plan.
