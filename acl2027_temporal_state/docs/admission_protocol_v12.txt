Admission protocol for the v0.12 bounded access pilot

Status and purpose

This is a prospective source-admission protocol for the eight-URL access pilot fixed in the v0.11 route decision. It is frozen after earlier discovery and before these new downloads or any v0.12 model predictions. Prior discovery informed the selected cases; the pilot is developmental and neither random nor held out. Protocol authorship is model assisted, with no claim of human adjudication. A new complete PDF, a correct title, or an apparently successful HTTP response does not itself admit a correction history.

The unchanged feasibility target is eight qualifying histories, not eight URLs and not a quota. This pilot contains three histories and eight representations: two Plug Power annual reports; OPERA v1 and v4 abstracts and PDFs; and the current JAMA article and correction notice. These are the exact URLs in the accompanying JSON. No new search, alternate URL, sign-in, challenge bypass or hidden retry is allowed. One attempt per URL retains the existing 30-second network and 8-MiB payload bounds. Failed, oversized, partial, blocked and unattempted outcomes must be recorded. A later transport amendment must be separately frozen before another attempt and cannot retrospectively erase an outcome or change eligibility to obtain a positive.

Admission is assessed on separate axes

1. Transport: record the attempted and resolved URLs, retrieval time, tool/transport identity, response status, bytes, hash and failure or truncation conditions. Transport failure is an access limitation, not evidence that a history is semantically ineligible or rare. A challenge or error page can be completely downloaded while failing to provide the intended source.

2. Representation completeness: bind the exact retrieved representation and full extraction with hashes and format/page metadata. Verify that the response is the intended document and that its body and relevant tables are available. Record unavailable figures, tables, supplements, extraction failures and uncertain reading order. A successful extraction is not an assertion that every pixel was visually checked. An abstract is a complete bounded abstract when captured in full, but it is not a complete paper. A selected web passage or tool-truncated result cannot be relabelled a full source. Missing a supplement is recorded separately; a claim requiring that missing supplement remains unresolved.

3. Source identity: verify document identifier, title, issuer/authors or collaboration, version and relationship to the other source from the captured contents and available primary metadata. Matching URLs or names alone do not certify content identity. Keep alternative representations of one version separate from distinct authored documents.

4. Historical version: require the actual earlier representation with the original assertion and exact locators, together with a later corrective representation and their documented order. A later notice quoting old wording does not create a captured historical original. Repository version and publication metadata remain separate from independently established first-public-availability times. Unknown times remain unknown; retrieval time never fills them. Do not remove updates, reverse numerical swaps or reconstruct old prose to simulate history.

5. Authority: separately record original assertion authorship, corrective assertion authorship, publication revising authority and evidence for the relationship. The existing core requires the same asserting authority. A publisher's official link to a named-author article establishes documentary association, not that premise. Ambiguous attribution remains unresolved. Any authority extension needs its own source-only review and preserved old-core comparison, not an implicit merge of authors and publisher.

6. Correction and scope: locate an explicit replacement of an independently meaningful whole assertion about the same subject, measure, population, observation period, modality and relevant conditions. Preserve units, uncertainty and qualifiers. Distinguish correction from succession, forecast revision, changed population, new measurement, methodological reanalysis, retraction and partial-field edits. A changed numerical value does not establish same-scope replacement. Where the source has multiple changes, certify the selected scope rather than treating the document or table as one proposition. Unsupported semantics are recorded without coercing them into the core.

7. Directly corrected dependents: inspect the complete available correcting source for an explicit correction of every proposed dependent assertion. Record yes, no, or unresolved with locators and the inspected extent. Preserve every observed direct edge. A directly corrected dependent is a useful competent-direct-baseline control and cannot demonstrate added propagation value. Absence from an excerpt cannot establish absence from the full source.

8. Essential dependency: require a distinct source-present dependent assertion and a text-grounded necessary support relation or source-specified computation. Topical links, duplicated paraphrases, invented thresholds and guessed causation do not qualify. Record each AND prerequisite and its scope, with exact evidence. The core withdraws support; it does not calculate replacement estimates, confidence intervals, rankings or causal conclusions. Changed support is not proof the dependent is false. An unresolved essential relation remains unresolved even when two numbers happen to move together.

9. Independent retention: distinguish an independently grounded unaffected assertion from an alternative support route for the affected conclusion. Locate the unaffected control and its independent basis; shared wording or a broad statement that conclusions survive does not automatically certify that basis. Inspect alternative support for each proposed withdrawal and record the scope reviewed and uncertainty. One lost premise does not justify withdrawal of a conclusion that retains independent support. The unchanged core has AND-only dependencies; natural alternative branches must be preserved as evidence and cannot be silently discarded or encoded as extra necessary prerequisites. An unsupported OR case is a retention challenge, not a clean core propagation positive.

Case-specific cautions fixed before access

Plug Power: retain the actual FY2019 and FY2020 reports as their observed representations. The FY2019 report's FY2018 comparative is not automatically FY2018's first original publication. Earlier discovery describes direct replacement of both the loss numerator and EPS; retain those direct edges if verified. This candidate is a potential redundant-propagation control. Do not calculate a new EPS or treat rounded numbers as exact reproducible arithmetic.

OPERA: version labels and a replacement declaration do not establish that the selected observations and analysis scope are identical. Prior discovery reports changed event selection as well as instrumental corrections. Independently inspect whether the selected assertion is a same-scope correction, reanalysis with changed data, or unresolved mixture. Do not substitute the separate 2012 experiment. Keep explicit downstream velocity and conclusion replacements; a formula alone does not imply residual value beyond direct correction. The unchanged geodetic measurement is only a proposed control until its independent basis is confirmed.

JAMA: these two URLs provide present-document evidence only. They cannot supply an observed pre-correction article. The v0.11 publisher/author authority ambiguity and missing historical essential edge remain open. A complete current article could support a separately designed full-document consistency study, but cannot be promoted into historical replay. Preserve outcome, exposure scale and methodological terminology. A reported point-estimate ordering is neither a significance test nor a causal ranking, and the core does not recompute it.

Decision and review procedure

For every axis, record supported, contradicted, unresolved, not assessed, or not applicable, with rationale and exact source evidence or an explicit missing-evidence explanation. An unknown is never converted to false or to pass. Keep access state, source stratum, core compatibility and experimental role as separate decisions. Pairing actual versions is necessary for version-stream replay but insufficient for semantic admission. Complete controlled document-version streams can support a direct-correction control or other compatible development case even when first-public-availability times are unknown. Such a case is not a historically clock-certified replay; leave unknown historical times unfilled and report the narrower controlled-stream use. Eligible directly corrected cases may be admitted as controls; only a certified essential nondirect relation without surviving alternative support can supply a candidate incremental-propagation test. Neither role establishes an algorithmic result.

Freeze captures before source-only adjudication. A reviewer separate from discovery/capture must inspect the source evidence before questions, references or predictions are used for selection. Disclose model assistance, prior familiarity and any shared inputs; do not call this human gold or blind review. Freeze admission decisions, all direct and essential edges, controls, exclusions, and answer/evidence references before a subsequent matched experiment. Keep extraction and routing failures separate from state-update behavior. An assertion-ID oracle cannot be compared with unrestricted reading as an end-to-end gain.

Stop and pivot rules

Stop after the eight listed attempts, or earlier when remaining attempts cannot add a qualifying representation; record why any URL was not attempted. Do not add histories or relax criteria to meet a count. If complete historical sources remain unavailable, keep the longitudinal gate closed. If accessible histories have no residual beyond direct correction, stop expanding the current longitudinal method claim and retain them as controls. A separate full-document inconsistency study requires complete present documents, independently reviewed scope and references, and a newly frozen design; one selected passage case is not a benchmark.

In any later matched comparison, reader/direct/dependency success establishes no answer-level advantage. Reader failure fixed by direct updates supports no propagation claim. A dependency advantage on a certified nondirect development case is only a signal for held-out replication, ablations and uncertainty analysis. Over-withdrawal requires recording surviving independent support. Unresolved scope, authority, retrieval or extraction is reported upstream. Objective-only or annotated-support gains do not establish QA value, novelty, generalization or ACL readiness.
