Smallest bridge

reconciliation is recorded, produce one separately versioned question-free
registry overlay and one separately reviewed author-packet release. Reuse the
fixed v18 bundles and original source-only staging projections. No source search,
dependency expansion, replacement table, question-conditioned label or interface
redesign is proposed.

1. Account for every fixed bundle-occurrence entry: planned 326 across 21 bundles,
   with exact frozen key-set equality and all bundle memberships retained. This
   is a coverage denominator, not a new unique-physical-fact count. Unknown,
   ambiguous, unsupported, nil, duplicate and unreviewable entries remain present;
   review completion does not require all six aspects resolved for every entry.

2. Reconcile source-only annotations with the existing typed registry by frozen
   source identity and exact occurrence anchors, never equal values or labels.
   Preserve canonical values, statuses and copied reported dimensions; corrections
   require an explicit new record, never a silent overwrite. For each aspect,
   distinguish reviewed visible support from copied declared metadata. Retain
   unavailable relations as null. Record disagreements and unresolved mappings.
   Do not convert a report cover into a default table entity or population;
   empty dimensions do not establish consolidation. Literal subject wording is
   distinct from the context entity identifier, visible period wording from exact
   typed boundaries, and visible unit wording from typed measures/normalization.
   Any accepted correspondence needs its own supported attachment and provenance.

3. Keep source/byte/DOM/cell/raster anchors, annotation states and reconciled
   aspect-to-witness relations in a private provenance sidecar. The unchanged v18
   pack accepts witnesses only as handle/aspect/text; extra provenance fields are
   invalid, and its constructor does not prove witness-to-block source support.
   Emit only reviewed aspect-specific witness texts and mapped values into that
   schema. Existing synthetic 'Declared source metadata' witnesses do not become
   visible semantic evidence by changing an admission flag. Preserve the metadata
   distinction and verify every emitted witness against the sidecar.

4. Reuse temporal_state.reader_binding_v18.EvidencePack, its canonical serialization,
   prompt_view() and from_prompt_view() exact round trip. Retain all original
   blocks and occurrences, including exact empty/whitespace blocks. Reuse existing
   interface/finalizer validators; they check syntax and exposed support, not
   financial meaning or natural-language correctness. Population remains the
   existing {label, dimensions} representation. No automatic scope defaults or
   invented composite financial labels are permitted; inexpressible relations
   remain unresolved rather than escaping into a new free-text answer schema.

Bundle release versus question admission

Before author release, close all 21 source-attempt records as admitted or rejected.
An admitted bundle contains its complete fixed table, neighbors and dependencies,
qualified actual source/view inspection, essential admitted literal identity card,
and the question-free registry with all entries accounted for. Missing or uncertain
REQUIRED dependencies block that bundle. Retain rejected bundle IDs and reasons.
Unknown auxiliary aspect mappings alone do not require rejecting the entire
bundle: the v17 workflow explicitly permits unknown aspects to stay null.

Build author packets from the original source-only staging projection, adding
admitted literal cards and qualified source/view admission bindings. Reuse its
allowlist checks and review the released packet structure. Authors receive complete
visible context and neutral anchors, not the occurrence projection, semantic
ledger, typed registry, canonical values, reviewer conclusions or target hints.
Record fresh role/context exposure and freeze the workflow, instructions, packet
and registry hashes before author access. All six issuer candidate sets close
before either independent reference reviewer starts.

For each later answerable candidate, R1/R2 and adjudication require all six
REQUESTED bindings, a supported normalized value, sufficient citation relations,
schema expressibility and unique support despite retained competitors. S requires
one scoped claim; P requires two separately supported physical-source claims and
supported common-concept correspondence. A null/unresolved non-target can defeat
unique support, but it is never removed to make the candidate pass. Retain fixed
candidate order, at most two per slot, and no reference-driven repair. Construct
and review controls only by the existing fixed aspect priority and two-phase
independent workflow. Preserve planned 12 answerables, 6 controls and 54 cells.

Independent timelines and remaining concrete work

Source-only authoring may proceed once source/registry/packet release is frozen,
independently of the authored native batch's outcome. Passing the separate eight
authored requests is required before NATURAL PREDICTIONS, not before authoring.
Neither annotation coverage nor authored native success establishes natural
answerability, token fit, reader adequacy or a research advance.

Whole-pack limits still apply without truncation: 64 facts, 64 blocks, 512
witnesses, 2,000,000 serialized bytes and 6,144 native input tokens for each arm.
Public v18 counts show that pairing the two large PFE bundles would contain
43 + 42 = 85 facts and exceed the fact cap. Other fixed choices may fit; selection
still follows the authored first-admissible rule. Both exact natural arm prompts
must tokenize after the reviewed registry and chosen complete bundle union exist.
The eight small authored prompts provide no evidence of natural token feasibility.

The v19 authored native host is fixed to eight requests and the v16 interface.
Natural execution needs a narrow separately reviewed specialization for frozen
v18 packs and the planned schedule, preserving closed inference-only inputs,
reference separation, resource guards, failure retention and offline grading.
No natural request or reference may be used to tune that specialization.

The natural joint scorer must compare action, every requested canonical value,
all six scopes and sufficient citations; reuse existing validators but do not
mistake authored assessment for this complete scorer. Freeze the common final
action/quantity/scope/citation projection using authored controls, excluding arm
names and diagnostic traces; score B1 proposal diagnostics separately. This
source-free scorer/host work may proceed alongside annotation and authoring.
Natural prediction freeze still waits for immutable references/packs/control
reviews, both-arm token admission, passed authored native gate, complete planned
schedule and the existing independent execution review. No optional new gate is
introduced here.
