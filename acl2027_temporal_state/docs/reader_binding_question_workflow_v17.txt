Reader binding pilot: question/reference workflow v17
Preparation from checkpoint d793b01d12d8e516a433bd659cccb3eb3ca9a25b.
Status: REVIEWABLE PLAN, not an authoring freeze or an execution protocol.
No natural questions, references, source quantities or reader outputs were

Purpose and scope

Prepare a bounded, model-assisted development diagnostic of question-to-reported-
aspect binding, with complete evidence supplied. This is not retrieval accuracy,
raw table-reading accuracy, human gold, an independent test set or ACL novelty.
All six issuer pairs have already been used during project development. The
revenue/duration table pool was purpose-selected and amended after inspecting
visibility failures; retain the original 13/24 and amended 21/24 slot outcomes.

This v17 workflow covers ONLY stage A: twelve planned answerable questions and
six constructed missing-binding conditions. It does not authorize the inherited
stage B, instant questions, ambiguity study, larger competence gate, new model
selection or retrieval experiment. Nothing here is a completed observation.

Binding choices made before any question

1. Issuer order: PFE, NFLX, NVDA, LLY, BRK-B, SLB. The first/second physical source
   for each pair follows the amended pool manifest order. Those ordinals are not
   fiscal chronology. Retain filenames only in private provenance; a filename
   suffix does not establish reporting year, including for Berkshire.
2. Each issuer has S: one source-reported duration quantity from the first
   physical source, and P: two separately scoped reported quantities, one from
   each physical source. P is one question with two required claims. Its common
   financial concept needs explicit source support; preserve separate namespaces,
   reporting periods and populations. It need not imply equivalent populations,
   comparable periods, growth, correction or restatement. No arithmetic task.
3. Reserve S1,S2 and P1,P2 candidates in that order, at most two per slot. The
   author fixes every candidate in all twelve slots before either reference
   reviewer begins. A candidate may be explicitly unavailable. No third candidate,
   reference-driven rewrite or substitution after predictions is allowed.
4. Candidate ordering is the author's recorded pre-review order, not reference
   ease, B0 lexical score, answer agreement or reader performance. Select the first
   candidate admitted by all required gates. Keep every rejected/reserve record.
   Do not force a vocabulary or syntax that the known lexical baseline can parse.
5. For each issuer construct M from its selected S question, with identical
   question text. This supplies six planned controls, never six new independent
   questions. No control exists when its S parent is unadmitted.

Source admission and complete context BEFORE author access

The source-admission team must finish attempted rendering and actual inspection
for ALL 21 amended selected tables and existing neighbor blocks, recording every
failure. No attractive-case replacement from the original 13 or a third table.
Conversion success alone is insufficient. Inspect original source/CSS alongside
the actual pages for row/column alignment, visibility, unit, caption, heading,
footnote and external dependency completeness. Record source identity and each
numeric column relation. A converter is an inspection aid, not browser fidelity.

An admitted bundle contains one complete selected table, all original attached
neighbor blocks, and all required external heading/caption/unit/footnote/identity
dependencies identified before question authoring. Dependencies may be copied as
whole original source blocks but may not introduce a replacement answer table.
Any dependency that cannot be rendered/inspected or is uncertain blocks admission
of that bundle. Dedupe overlapping blocks by source identity plus exact locator;
retain source order and every row/cell/text/visibility record. The source team
must close this fixed bundle manifest before author packets are released. Later
discovery of a required dependency rejects the candidate in this attempt; it does
not trigger question-specific evidence expansion.

Identity-card prerequisite: the frozen v16 source_card() copies DEI identity
values without retaining their visibility flags. The source-free v17 provenance
audit finds syntactically hidden fiscal-year focus in8/12 cards, fiscal-period
focus and CIK in12/12, and registrant name in2/12. None of the other fields has
received visual certification merely by lacking a hiding cue. Therefore the
old card is mechanical reviewer metadata, NOT a reviewed visible author card.
For each of12 physical sources, bind visible source issuer/name, report type
and reporting-end/date wording to inspected original cover/heading blocks and
their render/source anchors. Fiscal-year labels may be included only with their
own visible support; do not infer them from an end-date's calendar year. Omit
optional hidden-only CIK/fiscal-period/fiscal-year fields from the author card.
Keep physical hash/version identity as provenance, not an independently verified
legal identity or SEC-byte-equivalence claim. If essential visible identity is
missing, its source/bundles remain unavailable. Source period/population/unit
relations still need separate numeric-column/source review.

The current101 table/neighbor render attempts do not silently include cover
witnesses. Identify exact required additional whole blocks, bind their hashes/
locators in a separate pre-author supplement, and render/inspect them if outside
that frozen population. Preserve the101 denominator and report any supplementary
blocks plus12 identity-card admission outcomes separately. Rendering a hidden
DEI node with hiding removed is not visible-source corroboration. No such
supplementary identity rendering or card admission is claimed by this plan.

At the same pre-author boundary, a separate pack producer builds a QUESTION-FREE
machine registry for every admitted bundle. It includes every numeric occurrence,
including null/nil/unsupported statuses, all retained duplicates, source-local
visible labels and every available reported aspect with its actual witnesses.
Every label/aspect needs source/decoder provenance. Do not add question synonyms,
answer-target labels, occurrence rankings or selective evidence after reading
questions. Unknown aspects stay null. No empty-dimension-to-consolidation rule.
Recovered taxonomy labels can be used only under a frozen all-occurrence rule.
Source-visible quantity strings in author packets are expected; canonical typed
numbers and context/QName helpers are not. Model-assisted metadata construction
and any semantic annotations remain explicitly disclosed.

Full-DTS success cannot be inferred from Session launch, exit code or captured
schemas. Record completed validation, missing dependencies and errors separately.
If the actual gate permits a source-supported fallback, label it 'visible source-
reported values with unvalidated taxonomy semantics'; admit only explicit source
relations. No default dimension, consolidation, cross-namespace equivalence or
unverified financial interpretation is supplied by familiarity with a label.

S selects one admitted bundle from the first source; P selects one from each.
Authors may choose among the fixed admitted bundles while writing their two
candidates. This is PURPOSEFUL, SOURCE-CONDITIONED ORACLE CONTEXT SELECTION, not
retrieval. Whole bundle unions and their question-free registries are the evidence;
no answer-only row selection. Both P sources are required even if the numerical
values coincide. If a union exceeds 64 numeric occurrences, 64 blocks, 512
witnesses, the serialized interface cap or either arm's native prompt-token cap,
reject it. Never truncate rows, competitors or dependencies to fit. A question-
specific candidate choice is recorded as oracle evidence preparation, not hidden.

Roles and access boundaries

Use the companion source-author and reference-review instructions verbatim after
their pre-author freeze. Role identities, model versions when available, input
hashes, prompts, outputs and context exposure must be recorded privately.

Author: a fresh model role sees only admitted renders, whole visible DOM tables/
neighbors/dependencies, source identity cards and opaque source/bundle anchors.
It cannot see typed joins, canonical numeric values, decoder/context/QName IDs,
references, baseline code/prompts/scores or reader outputs. It receives the S/P
intent but no per-question template and no answer-bearing example from these
sources. It produces candidate questions, intent and chosen bundle anchors;
it does not supply numeric reference answers.

R1 and R2: two fresh source-review roles independently receive the same question,
complete source bundles/renders and question-free registry. Each performs its own
source reading and reference derivation. Neither sees the author's rationale,
other review, reconciliation, system identity or evaluated prediction. The roles
are models, not independent humans or independent training data. Use fresh
contexts that have not served as author, evaluated reader or interface developer.
Current paper/native reviewers remain on protocol-only material; do not send
them later actual questions, source quantities or reference files.

Adjudicator: after R1/R2 outputs are immutable, a separate source-review role
reconciles discrepancies with source spans. No answer is selected by majority or
numerical agreement alone. Its logged decision cannot rewrite the question or
bundle. Unresolved support or reference disagreement makes the candidate
unadmitted. Preserve initial reviews, disagreements and final rationale.

Prediction workers: only frozen question plus exposed pack and fixed prompt;
no author rationale, reference files, adjudication, omitted-aspect labels or
hidden original document. Disable external tools, network retrieval and previous
question/chat context. Audit the trusted host so hidden labels cannot affect
requests, order, prompt cache or early stopping. The v16 native runner reads a
references file in its host process: its natural path must either be separately
reviewed to establish this strict noninterference, or use a new reference-free
prediction path and separate grader. No implication that this fix already exists.

Reference admission and exact answer target

R1/R2 record independent admission decisions for every candidate, then adjudicate.
For an answerable case require all six reported bindings (concept, entity, exact
period, unit, explicitly sourced population and physical source version), a
supported normalized number, and complete citation relations in the exposed
registry. Null/unresolved facts remain in the pack and may block unique support;
they must not be silently filtered out. An answerable S has exactly one requested
scoped quantity; P has exactly two. Equal numbers or duplicate aliases do not
reduce the number of requested physical-source claims.

Freeze exact canonical base-unit decimal targets and acceptable equivalent
occurrence groups. Output must conform to the unchanged interface's canonical
decimal schema. Display scaling is for human references only; no new output
tolerance or conversion is added after predictions. Freeze necessary/sufficient
citation witness sets, acceptable duplicate aliases, exact scope and whether
the finalizer's whole compatible-duplicate group is sound. Row/column proximity
alone does not prove a concept/aspect relation. Admissibility also requires
that all requested claim bindings are expressible under the frozen interface;
unrepresentable semantics are a source/task limitation, not a free-text escape.

Two-source concept correspondence must be supported by visible definitions/labels
and, if needed, a reviewed mapping with provenance; the common concept may be
rejected when semantics remain uncertain. Do not strip namespaces or infer
equivalence from matching numbers. Each source's own scope stays separate.

Missing-binding construction and independent review

For each admitted S, attempt withholding in the fixed order source, period,
unit, population. At most one construction per aspect, four attempts per issuer,
with no evaluated reader calls. Select the first independently admitted control;
keep all preceding failures. If none works, M remains unadmitted. Never substitute
a P control or change the question to fill that slot.

The builder copies the complete pack and removes ONLY access to one essential
binding relation and every duplicate route that discloses it. Preserve all
quantity lexemes, canonical values, all fact handles/occurrences and irrelevant
content. Retained IDs must not encode the omitted relation. Delete witnesses and
set corresponding bindings null wherever needed; retain all unrelated bindings.
Masked source text is an explicitly constructed access view, not the original
source or a faithfully rendered report. Keep original bytes immutable and a
private exact before/after manifest. Quantity lexemes means the exact source
number strings of EVERY numeric occurrence, including non-target, unsupported
and nil presentations; it does not forbid removing a date/year token used only
as a binding witness. Record each removed numeric-looking token's source role.
A token that is both a protected quantity and the withheld witness cannot be
removed; if keeping it discloses the binding, reject the control. This definition
is fixed before authoring, never relaxed after observing outputs. If clean
relation-level masking cannot preserve quantities and unrelated information,
reject this attempt.

Audit visible row/column strings, captions, footnotes, neighboring prose, source
cards, filenames, path names, source hashes, namespace/version strings, unit
normalization, registry values, duplicate facts and question text. Pack/request
identifiers shown to the model must be neutral fixed-length opaque IDs; do not
show M, masked, parent IDs, omitted aspect or reference status. Common prompt
format must not label which cases are controls. Null support can signal absence;
the control is explicitly mechanistic, not a natural-prevalence estimate.

The question may name its requested year/unit/source; that does not automatically
bind an available number to it. Reviewers must explain why that relation cannot
be established from ALL supplied information, including the question. If the
relation follows from remaining metadata, singleton pack/source conventions,
namespace strings or canonical normalization, the control is invalid. Zero hidden
numeric facts or removal of one heading is insufficient evidence of withholding.

Each control attempt receives TWO fresh, independent control-review contexts.
Phase 1 sees only the question and exposed control view and records any supported
answer or unresolved relation before any hidden reference. Phase 2 then audits
the complete original bundle, proposed omission and reference. Record a concrete
nonidentifiability argument, such as two possible assignments of retained numbers
to the requested binding consistent with all exposed information; these are
logical access countermodels, not fabricated claims about the actual filing.
Also verify that no unconditional scoped answer is supported, the underlying
question remains specified, the parent complete pack is answerable, and only
the intended witness relation was lost. Prior complete-reference reviewers may
be reused as role names only in genuinely fresh contexts; retain that dependence
as model-role annotation. Both initial checks and reconciled admission are frozen.

Correct M behavior is 'insufficient' with no material quantity. 'Clarify' is not
correct for a fully specified question whose evidence is missing, although it
can be a safe abstention; report that distinction. Unsupported numbers inside
user-facing refusals still count. B1 hypothesis traces are diagnostic proposals,
not user-facing answers; score their mapping/support separately. If any material
unscoped numeric answer escapes the schema, retain invalid output and unsupported
claim flags; never excuse it as ungraded prose.

Denominators and freeze states

Maintain one immutable ledger row for every planned slot, candidate, condition
and arm, including unavailable and unattempted rows. Explicit stages are:
source_bundle_pending/rejected/admitted; candidate_unavailable/authored;
review_pending/rejected/admitted; pack_pending/rejected/admitted;
tokenization_pending/rejected/admitted; reference_frozen;
execution_unattempted/started/completed/failed/blocked_after_stop.
No missing or failed record becomes a missing denominator. Metadata preparation,
source admission, semantic admission, token feasibility and execution are distinct.

Planned denominators are always: six issuers, twelve answerable slots (six S,
six P), six controls, eighteen conditions, eighteen requested answerable quantity
claims, and fifty-four system-condition cells (eighteen each for B0/B1/B2).
Report candidates authored /24 maximum separately; two candidates are not two
evaluation questions. Report admitted answers /12, controls /6 and conditions
/18, then completed outputs /54 and per-arm /18. Report every rejection reason
by stage and issuer. All coverage figures use both planned and admitted counts.

Natural execution freeze occurs only after authoring is closed, both source
reviews/adjudication and control checks finish, all selected references/packs are
immutable, BOTH arm prompts tokenize within caps, the native authored gate
passes, and a source-free independent freeze review passes. An unadmitted slot
remains unfilled; do not pick a new candidate after this freeze. If tokenization
needs loading the native reader, it is still not a natural prediction and occurs
only under that separately approved reader guard. Its resource failure is retained.

If fewer than all twelve plus six are admitted, an execution of the admitted
subset is labeled INCOMPLETE DEVELOPMENT DIAGNOSTIC before launch, with the same
planned denominator and no adequacy/advance claim. No per-issuer denominator is
silently shrunk. Record intended-but-unattempted cells for all excluded conditions.
References freeze before predictions; tokenizer-only failures may reject a
candidate under the declared first-admissible rule, never an observed completion.

Matched systems, order and budget

Inherit frozen B0 lexical rules and B1/B2 interface from v16; any code/prompt
revision based on these natural references requires a new development version.
B0, B1 and B2 receive identical question, pack, source access and metadata.
B0 may ignore fields by its fixed algorithm; B1 uses one model proposal plus
deterministic finalization; B2 uses one direct model answer with the same model.
B0 is a narrow exact-token/four-digit-year lexical baseline, not a strong
semantic parser. B1 is the stronger same-model structured mapping comparison.
All include support/citation checks and separate external semantic grading.
Canonical values appear in the common pack. This measures selecting quantities
and scopes from machine-assisted evidence, not decoding raw table numerals.
Charge B1's deterministic work and all pack/tokenization costs; common caps do
not imply equal actual FLOPs, tokens, latency or algorithmic complexity.

Condition order is S, P, M for each issuer in the fixed issuer order. Skip only
predeclared unadmitted slots, retaining their ledger cells. Natural arm order is
always adjacent B1 then B2, consistent with the current native validator. This
explicitly supersedes the v16 draft's parity language. Record the fixed-order
latency confound; make no arm timing superiority claim. B0 executes exactly once
per admitted condition against the identical frozen inputs, outside annotation.

No natural repair/retry completions. A prelaunch block, timeout, partial output,
output limit, malformed schema or resource stop retains its scheduled cell.
After a global resource stop, record all remaining cells as blocked/unattempted;
do not call those semantic model errors or selectively resume favorable cases.
A later retry batch is a separately versioned development attempt. There is no
best-of sampling, confidence tuning, model tournament or paid/API budget here.

Maximum natural generation: 36 scheduled requests, 6,144 input tokens including
chat/template/instructions per arm, 768 output tokens, 8,192 context, 300 seconds
per completion, greedy pinned seed0/thinking-off recipe. Totals: 221,184 input
tokens, 27,648 output tokens, 10,800 request-seconds (three serial hours maximum),
plus startup/tokenization/annotation/packing/grading. At an unverified 1-2 minutes
per request, generation alone would be 36-72 minutes. Not a measured runtime.
The distinct eight authored-interface requests precede natural execution; if
one preflight plus full stage A ran, ceilings are 44 requests, 270,336 input and
33,792 output tokens, 13,200 request-seconds. Existing failed preflight attempts
remain extra historical costs and are not erased by this planned budget.

Annotation is not free or included in the 36 reader calls. Bound this workflow
to at most 24 authored candidates, 48 independent candidate reviews, 24 candidate
adjudications, 24 control constructions, 48 independent control reviews and 24
control adjudications. Count actual role sessions/turns and available token/time
usage separately; these are work-item caps, not claims about provider calls or
zero cost. No annotation or natural reader call is executed by this preparation.

Scoring, diagnostics and no-go rules

Primary joint correctness is correct action AND all requested exact quantities,
unit, entity/population, period, physical source and sufficient citations, with
no extra unsupported claim. Score S and P separately and pooled /12 planned and
/admitted answerable cases; score exact claim-level correctness /18 planned and
/admitted claims separately. Two-source partial credit never makes a jointly
correct question. Answerable-case abstention is incorrect, even if safely scoped.

For M report correct insufficient /6 planned and /admitted controls; safe but
incorrect clarification, unsupported answer, invalid output and not executed
are separate. Report per-arm coverage and unsupported-material-answer counts
both /18 planned and /executed conditions; do not report a zero conditional risk
when no material answers were produced. Publish actual numerator/denominator.

B1 diagnostics include all-required-claim binding recall at its first hypothesis
and anywhere in its at-most-eight hypotheses, per-required-claim recall, number
of hypotheses, unresolved declarations and correctly proposed bindings rejected
by finalization. Missing/malformed predictions are zero recall in the planned
denominator and separately identified. Recall means one joint hypothesis covers
ALL requested claims, not unioning incompatible hypotheses into a false success.
Eight is an upper bound, not a promise to fit eight citation-heavy hypotheses
inside 768 output tokens. Report actual returned beam sizes and output-limit
failures; do not enlarge the cap or silently shorten citations after outputs.
Only actually proposed correct bindings can enter the false-elimination rate;
zero proposals is not proof of verifier recall. Duplicate aliases follow the
pre-frozen acceptable groups. Automated syntax/support checks do not grade NL
semantics. Feed the residual semantic grader the SAME canonical final action,
quantity, expanded scope and citation projection for every arm. Omit method
names, raw arm-specific schemas, hypotheses, finalizer traces and diagnostic
reason codes; grade B1 proposal diagnostics separately. Freeze this common
projection using authored controls before natural grading. Removing method names
alone does not blind distinct output structures. Retain raw decisions and any
remaining distinguishability (e.g., abstention style/duplicate citation patterns)
as a limitation, not proof of perfect blinding. References are not rewritten
after seeing outputs.

The engineering adequacy gate requires all12 answerables and all6 controls
admitted; one model arm (B1 or B2) must get >=11/12 joint answers, >=1/2 per issuer,
all6 correct insufficient actions and zero unsupported material control answers.
This explicitly closes the inherited draft's loophole where invalid outputs
could satisfy a zero-unsupported-answer count. B1 advancement additionally requires joint
beam recall >=11/12 and zero observed false eliminations among proposed correct
bindings. Report refusal correctness separately: silence/invalid output cannot
be called successful control competence. Clearing this gate only motivates a
larger separately frozen evaluation, not stage B authorization or statistical
certification. Six correlated issuer clusters cannot establish risk <5%, held-
out generalization, a strong general reader, or method superiority.

If only B0 meets those task counts, that is lexical-baseline competence on this
purpose-selected domain, not a passed model-reader gate. Establish residual need
and diagnose B1/B2 before proposing retrieval machinery; no automatic expansion.
If B0 succeeds alongside a model arm, still establish that residual need.
If B2 succeeds and B1 fails, diagnose the interface/proposer. If all fail with
complete evidence, suspend retrieval claims and diagnose reader/binding errors.
If apparent gains depend on leaked mappings, extra source access or different
admission sets, invalidate the comparison and preserve the failed attempt.
