Decision and evidence boundary

The next useful step is natural source capture and annotation plus a controlled scorer study, before extending exact search. Actual pinned likelihood execution is now established. On this single development graph, however, the scorer is sensitive to the two recorded conditions and exact search offers no advantage over ordinary iteration. This is an exploratory diagnostic, not an ACL-ready empirical contribution, accuracy result or novelty claim.

The working research question is correction-dependent reconstruction under uncertain interpretations: when a reading changes the active correction target or essential support, can a method improve grounded answers beyond strong full-prefix extraction and iterative inference? Current natural evidence does not yet test that question. Temporal memory, joint extraction/temporal-link inference, truth maintenance and temporal constraint networks all have direct predecessors. The implemented machinery alone is not an algorithmic advance.
