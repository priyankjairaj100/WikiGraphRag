NEXT EMPIRICAL GATE — v0.7
Declared 2026-10-02 before root inspects v0.7 scores or ambiguity audit output.

Decision
Keep joint factual identity and temporal update inference as the working research
question, with an empirical gate rather than a promised positive contribution.
The implementation and scoring infrastructure are sufficient to start collecting
actual comparative evidence. Further engineering should answer a measured failure.

The next unit of progress is a batch of independent natural histories, not more
permutations of the single Nike graph. A tiny pinned scorer establishes execution;
it is not a substitute for a strong contextual model or for independent evaluation.

First batch and separation
1. Register twenty development histories across leadership transitions and public
   versioned plans/corrections. Choose histories by a documented source sampling
   rule before extracting candidates or observing decoder differences. Log all
   excluded histories and why. Previously inspected Nike/OpenAI/Lyft histories
   remain development demonstrations and do not count as untouched evaluation.
2. Distinguish routine sampled histories from a deliberately enriched ambiguity or
   correction challenge slice. Report the slices separately. Do not estimate the
   natural frequency of ambiguity from the enriched slice.
3. Retain source text/version hashes, operational availability definitions, exact
   evidence spans, and unresolved historical-availability gaps. A current-record
   experiment may proceed with an explicitly current-record question; it must not
   silently become a historically faithful prefix benchmark.
4. Extract and freeze candidates without questions, answer labels or method
   outputs. Audit both missing alternatives and unsupported alternatives. Do not
   generate a quota of rival readings. Candidate identity, episode assignment,
   temporal boundaries, and correction targets are separate ambiguity categories.
   Competing episode links can matter even when entity names are unambiguous.
5. Build evaluation questions and evidence judgments in a separate source-only
   role with a recorded provenance and disagreement procedure. Model-assisted
   labels remain model-assisted; neither isolated roles nor schema checks make
   them human annotations or gold. Freeze the evaluation set before method output
   inspection and reserve connected histories together in subsequent splits.

Matched comparison
Use exactly the same candidate graph, source access, score cache, constraints,
null conventions and objective for independent, iterative, restarted and exact
small-component inference. The independent baseline already optimizes its links
exactly conditional on its chosen readings. Include a strong contextual/listwise
baseline with matched source access when its runtime is available. Record every
model call, input/output budget, failure, wall time and measured memory.

Report separately: candidate coverage; reading/link evidence validity; scoring
errors; feasibility/representation exclusions; search objective gaps; factual
answer/evidence outcomes; abstention; and cost. An exact-objective gain is not
an accuracy gain. Model-size, prompt, scale and objective changes are separate
experiments. A stronger scorer may eliminate an apparent search advantage.

Proceed / narrow decision
After the first twenty histories, inspect whether nontrivial natural coupling
survives source adjudication and strong contextual/restarted baselines. If it
does, implement a scalable solver only for the measured problem structure and
benchmark accuracy-cost tradeoffs on a subsequently frozen held-out set.
If it does not, narrow the research contribution to the supported problem
(e.g. correction-aware representation or uncertainty evaluation), after a fresh
related-work novelty audit. Do not select a positive subset and present it as a
general benchmark result. There is no guaranteed publishable outcome at this gate.

Still needed for a paper-level claim
Independent natural evaluation, defensible reference judgments and candidate
recall, a strong learned baseline, a demonstrable inference/representation
contribution, robust cost and uncertainty analysis, and a claim-by-claim
manuscript tied to actual measurements. Classical temporal closure, exhaustive
search, support withdrawal, and reproducible caching alone are not novelty.

Source-stream design addendum (same day, before root reads v0.7 likelihoods)
The primary new benchmark will use explicitly controlled document-prefix streams.
The experimental delivery order and frozen current-retrieved versions define the
input, independently of any claim about past public availability. Keep delivery,
retrieval, publication and event clocks separate. A historically faithful slice
requires additional version/availability evidence and is reported separately.
This changes the next collection protocol, not any prior result or cutoff.
See configs/source_stream_protocol_v07.json for the prospective admission rules.
No histories have yet been collected or evaluated under that new protocol.

The Nike audit concerns residual full-context ambiguity. General candidate
proposal coverage is broader: locally plausible hypotheses may correctly be
falsified by full eligible context or coupled constraints. The next batch must
measure those proposal errors without oracle filtering, while comparing against
a strong full-prefix contextual baseline. Zero full-context alternatives in one
case neither demonstrates complete candidate recall nor refutes joint inference.
