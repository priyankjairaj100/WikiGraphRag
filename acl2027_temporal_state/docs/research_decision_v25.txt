Research decision v25: rebuild the claim before rebuilding the paper
6 October 2026

Decision

The current project has no demonstrated novel method with a natural, held-out advantage.
It is not ready for an ACL submission.
Stop expanding the financial certificate route as the intended main contribution.
Keep its theory, software, negative results, and source audits as a preserved research record.

The search found no verified breakthrough in generic temporal graphs, evidence certificates, safe caching, or reasoning-chain repair.
This is a finding about the inspected candidates, not proof that the entire research area is exhausted.
Do not begin another large model campaign or rewrite the manuscript around an untested name.

One conditional hypothesis deserves a bounded feasibility check:
Learn to revise temporal interpretations when retrieved passages change their evidential support.
This remains a research hypothesis, not a selected, novel algorithm.
The canonical next-step limit appears below.
It supersedes the alternative 24-question and 32-history suggestions in the companion audits.

Why the present direction is insufficient

The original mechanism removes predicted obsolete claims before context selection.
That remains a useful baseline and a clear practical motivation.
However, the original high scores largely concern constructed claim collections and supplied fields.
The historical evaluator selects a component through a gold claim identifier.
Its interval answers use stored claim values.
Those experiments cannot establish natural historical question answering.

The strongest original genuine-prose QA result covers 35 questions.
Its released comparison reports 0.8571 against 0.7143, with p=.076.
The paired confidence interval includes zero.
This result motivates further work but does not establish reliable superiority.
The original A-B-B interval defect also remains a necessary repair, rather than a research contribution.

V24 does not rescue the method claim.
Conventional structural retrieval supplies question-sufficient evidence for seven of nine complete development questions.
One remaining history has a confirmed gap across the tested policies.
Another remains uncertain.
These are assistant evidence judgments, not reader predictions.
Dense retrieval, strong semantic planning, and natural reader competence remain untested in that probe.

The project accumulated validation machinery before establishing a difficult, distinct research target.
More validation alone cannot close that gap.

What the literature rules out

Re3 already extracts facts jointly, groups matching entities and relations, and suppresses older conflicting versions.
Its publication-time assumption leaves important limitations.
Those limitations do not make another recency filter novel.
https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.1180.pdf, section 3.4

IA-RAG already represents intervals and refines uncertain temporal bounds.
Its primary abstract and official repository were inspected.
Full-method access remained incomplete.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06044
https://github.com/xiaoAugenstern/IA-RAG

Q-TIE already learns query intervals and combines semantic and temporal scores.
Adding a trained temporal parser therefore cannot support our main novelty claim.
https://arxiv.org/html/2609.23880v1, section 3

EvoTrustRAG already compares grounded explanations of temporal conflicts and preserves unresolved outcomes.
Its inspected formulation starts from extracted facts within a retrieved context.
Simply adding retrieval to that design does not establish a new learning principle.

Joint event extraction and temporal relation learning also predate this project.

The useful correction to our earlier reasoning

Source ambiguity differs from uncertainty in a model.
An initial interpretation can be wrong even when the complete source clearly supports one answer.
Another passage may resolve the event date, entity, population, or meaning of a comparison.
The original v07 gate sometimes required competing readings to remain defensible after reading complete context.
That requirement can exclude ordinary errors which joint interpretation could correct.

The new hypothesis concerns such correctable errors.
It does not require genuine sources to remain ambiguous.
It does not require a publisher to issue a correction.
It does not revive the failed financial task under a different label.
The v07 record remains unchanged.

Three outcomes must stay separate:
1. Additional source evidence resolves a model's wrong interpretation.
2. Even the full admitted evidence leaves the answer unresolved.
3. The candidate generator never proposes the correct interpretation.

Only the first outcome supports the proposed revision mechanism.
The second requires uncertainty handling.
The third requires better proposals before comparing selectors.

Potential paper claim

Working question: Can evidence-driven interpretation revision prevent harmful supersession in temporal retrieval?

Retain candidate entity, scope, and time assignments before additional retrieval.
Record which exact passages support each assignment.
After retrieving another passage, revise the assignments and their replacement relations together.
Preserve valid historical evidence when a supposed replacement concerns another period or scope.
Measure both corrected errors and previously correct interpretations that become wrong.

A possible learned component scores which assignments should change after each new passage.
Training would use source-supported interpretation changes and unchanged controls from training data only.
It would compare alternative assignments jointly, rather than permanently trusting an initial extraction.
This describes a candidate mechanism, not an implemented contribution.
Ordinary structured learning, latent QA learning, and evidence utility learning remain close precedents.
No new theorem follows from this description.

The decisive controls

Use the same candidate passages, extractor, reader, context budget, and total inference accounting.
Compare a full-context reader, ordinary query decomposition, and a second extraction pass over the expanded context.
Compare standard constrained prediction using the same candidates and compatibility rules.
Include suitable temporal baselines, particularly Re3, Q-TIE, and an accessible interval-based method.
Use their reference implementations where practical.
Label adaptations and missing implementations explicitly.

If ordinary re-extraction matches the proposed revision, reject the method claim.
If the same representation with a standard decoder matches it, reject the specialized decoder claim.
If gains come only from more evidence or a stronger reader, report that cause.
Do not call a generic active-discrimination objective a new temporal algorithm.

Bounded next step

Allow at most 60 newly inspected natural questions for the initial feasibility stage.
This is a spending limit, not a powered confirmatory study.
Do not replace excluded questions until a desired difficult subset appears.
Keep all exclusions and all ordinary questions in the accounting.

TEMPO is a candidate source because it contains existing Stack Exchange questions and real documents.
It is not yet accepted as the main benchmark.
Its reported oracle answer score is 80.5, against 77.3 without retrieval.
Those scores measure graded reference coverage, not exact-match accuracy.
The small reported gap makes an answer-level headroom check essential.
https://arxiv.org/html/2601.09523v1, section 5 and Table 6

The six inspected records in the companion feasibility audit are already exposed development material.
Only one exact gold passage was retrieved; other source lookups failed or timed out.
No retrieval baseline ran on those six records.
Exclude them from the new 60-question roster.
Freeze the dataset revision, roster, sampling rule, and evidence access policy before model predictions.
Use original questions without generated reformulations as the task input.
Generated reformulations remain valid baseline outputs when their cost is charged.
Retain query-specific negative documents in our main corpus.
Report the published exclusion protocol separately when reproducing its scores.

Stage A checks whether exact source evidence can support interpretation references.
Missing question dates must remain unknown unless a source supplies them.
The inspected TEMPO document schema lacks source URLs, revision identifiers, and publication dates.
Recover required provenance before using it to claim lifecycle or historical-source performance.
Without this recovery, restrict the task to source-relative interpretation within the released text.
Retrieval failure alone does not establish insufficient evidence.
Do not train or select a method using reference fields at inference time.

Stage B compares frozen baseline interpretations with source-assisted oracle corrections.
Oracle corrections must use the same delivered evidence pack and budget as ordinary re-extraction.
Evidence outside that pack cannot support an oracle correction in this diagnostic.
Oracle corrections measure potential improvement, not method performance.
Seek at least 12 source-resolvable interpretation errors among the fixed 60 questions.
Require at least six additional correct interpretations beyond ordinary re-extraction under the fixed budget.
This is an interpretation diagnostic, not an answer-level comparison.
These thresholds are prospective practical choices, not statistical significance requirements.
Freeze their exact scoring implementation before the run.
If the task or reader cannot support this diagnostic, stop before training.

If feasibility survives, the requested natural reader gate remains necessary before an answer-level method comparison.
Prepare about 100 complete-evidence items and 100 explicitly scoped insufficient-evidence items.
Freeze model revision, prompt, decoding, arithmetic, and scoring first.
The old 2/8 result and the typed-record controls cannot satisfy this gate.
No large-model handoff is ready at this checkpoint.

Only after these checks should we define a distinct training rule and run a pilot.
Before training, write a one-page mechanism comparison against the closest methods.
If it only renames existing operations, stop this candidate.
If the pilot fails, preserve it and permit changes only on development data.
Never tune against the confirmatory set, including after a development win.

A later confirmation needs disjoint source families, pre-prediction references, and a frozen effect size and risk limit.
Group analysis by source history or connected source family, rather than treating related questions as independent.
The twelve financial filings remain development material.
The financial confirmatory design is parked; it does not transfer automatically to this task.

Positioning for ARR

ARR accepts methods, resources, and analyses with appropriately supported claims.
A completely new primitive is not mandatory.
However, our intended method paper still needs a clear contribution and an honest empirical advantage.
https://aclrollingreview.org/reviewerguidelines

The intended revised paper would retain the original motivation and use WikiGraphRAG as a baseline.
Its core would concern revisable temporal interpretation, supported by natural evidence and decisive component controls.
Its theory would state only necessary assumptions or nontrivial consequences.
Its evaluation would measure grounded answers, harmful changes, and total cost.
An analysis paper remains a separate option if a substantial general finding emerges.
It is not an automatic fallback for another small unsuccessful pilot.
