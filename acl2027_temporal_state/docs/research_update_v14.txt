Research update v0.14: completed development evidence and next decision

The attempted reader upgrade did not improve the matched development comparison.
The dense and hybrid retrieval follow-up also did not improve complete source
support on these eight questions. These are useful negative development findings,
not evidence of a novel algorithm, a strong reader, or ACL-ready generalization.
The defensible next investment is source-faithful typed binding and a standard
structured/Inline XBRL baseline before a specialized witness-selection method.

Completed reader study

The corpus remains four PDF versions from two familiar histories, 550 augmented
pages and 2,306 page-bounded chunks. Chunks contain at most 160 whitespace words
with 30-word overlap. Ordinary BM25 selects six history-wide seeds; paired BM25
selects three per document. Parent expansion and source-context closure share
that paired shortlist. No gold page selector or answer-driven retrieval repair
was used. Every displayed span and full native prompt is charged under the
960-source-word, 3500-input-token, 256-output-token and 4096-context limits.

The pinned Qwen3.5-4B Unsloth Q5_K_M recipe passed 8/8 authored short controls, then
completed all 32 natural development responses. Final counts are:

| Condition | Content | Scope | Citation-grounded | Joint | Output-limit failures |
|-----------|---------|-------|-------------------|-------|-----------------------|
| ordinary  | 1/8     | 2/8   | 2/8               | 1/8   | 0/8                   |
| paired    | 1/8     | 3/8   | 1/8               | 0/8   | 1/8                   |
| parent    | 2/8     | 2/8   | 2/8               | 1/8   | 0/8                   |
| closure   | 2/8     | 3/8   | 2/8               | 1/8   | 1/8                   |

Joint means the conjunction of reference-content correctness, scope correctness
and citation grounding. Format, appropriate abstention and native output-limit
failure remain separate axes; joint is not shorthand for passing every gate.
Both output-limit responses remain in the denominator. The 32 cells contain 30
unique native input sequences, and they are not 32 independent questions.

For the 16 ordinary/paired cells with exactly the same context bytes as the prior
v13 layout study, joint correctness is 1/16 for the new recipe versus 2/16 for the
old one. This failed reader-upgrade attempt does not establish a checkpoint-only
causal effect: model, quantization, chat template and nonthinking control changed
together. Eight short synthetic controls did not predict adequate natural-context
performance. Shared model-assisted grading is not independent human gold.

Source support is distinct from answer correctness. Complete received-context
support is 2/8 ordinary, 2/8 paired, 2/8 parent and 3/8 closure. Parent packing drops
19 of 48 seeds and loses one version in two cases. Closure drops none, but also
deduplicates overlapping spans; this experiment does not isolate attachment gain
from that deduplication. Equal limits do not imply equal evidence or actual tokens.

One source-exact whitespace repair was explicitly frozen before main predictions:
the initial parent/closure renderer removed endpoint whitespace, which could
destroy first-line indentation in multiline headers. The amended renderer keeps
every character of the original source slice. Initial code/results/audits remain
preserved; this is disclosed development repair. The parser, seed scores and
selection did not change. Its audit recovered only 7/17 required structural links;
ten remained missing. The no-false-link finding applies only to its limited
context-attachment semantics, not certified row/year/unit binding. Two v1 tables
still use separately tagged model-assisted whole-table transcription, and math/
figure extraction is incomplete.

Completed dense source-support follow-up

After the native main batch ended, the separately frozen source-only follow-up
used official pinned BGE-small-en-v1.5 ONNX CLS/L2 embeddings and equal-weight
BM25/dense RRF with k=60. This is an established compact baseline, not 2026 SOTA.
There was no question/reference-driven tuning, query expansion, or additional
natural QA generation. All 48 contexts were retained. Sixteen BM25 controls were
byte-identical to active paired/closure contexts; 32 new contexts received masked
source-support review against the unchanged required facts.

| Condition     | Complete contexts | Fully supported required facts |
|---------------|-------------------|--------------------------------|
| BM25 paired   | 2/8               | 10/21                          |
| BM25 closure  | 3/8               | 12/21                          |
| dense paired  | 1/8               | 3/21                           |
| dense closure | 1/8               | 3/21                           |
| RRF paired    | 1/8               | 9/21                           |
| RRF closure   | 1/8               | 9/21                           |

Partial and absent support remain in the summaries. Both dense/RRF closure drop
one seed; all six conditions retain both versions in all eight contexts. Four of
2,306 passage embeddings were right-truncated at 512 encoder tokens, with no
query truncation; reader source spans were unchanged. Passage encoding took about
192.16 seconds, query encoding 0.117 seconds and the resident native packing
wrapper 7.72 seconds on this host. These are descriptive single-run costs.
Source support does not predict unmeasured QA accuracy, and this small negative
result does not establish that dense retrieval generally fails.

Integrity and reproducibility

results/structured_study_summary_v14.json and
results/dense_source_support_summary_v14.json are the final hash-bound summaries.
The main execution audit passed 1,348 checks, including a separate relocated
metadata-only replay; its external pass made no model calls. The dense execution
audit passed 983 checks, replaying saved-vector rankings and source spans and
independently retokenizing all 48 final prompts in one session with zero
completions. It did not rerun encoder hidden states or certify semantic labels.
The full repository unit suite passed 484 tests. Tests and mechanical checks do
not turn source-support review into truth or establish method effectiveness.

Public artifacts contain code, protocols, projections, hashes, counts, locators,
reviews and manuscript sources. Raw captures, full contexts/prompts/answers,
token arrays, detailed source inventories and model/runtime assets remain
external. The old Qwen3-4B-Q8_0 weight was verified before cache eviction to make
space; old traces/runtime remain. Reexecuting that old reader requires downloading
its exact pinned weight again. Failed download/import attempts are preserved.
Portable checking, retained-artifact replay and new inference are different
operations; docs/reproduce_v14.txt gives their boundaries and commands.

Source-only expansion and the next research direction

Six issuer groups yielded twelve captured annual-report HTML objects: PFE, NFLX,
NVDA, LLY, BRK-B and SLB. Selection used a frozen 179-file/89-prefix release listing
and exposure exclusions, before source-body retrieval. All twelve immutable
released objects verify; two LFS content identities required an explicit offline
digest-namespace reconciliation. Berkshire's filename years 2022/2023 correspond
to actual fiscal years 2021/2022, so only five pairs meet the actual FY2022–2024
window. No replacement or new QA/reference creation followed. These are
development sources, not held-out or historically replayable histories. Unknown
preview/pretraining exposure, original SEC accession identity, completeness,
rendering fidelity, first-public availability and redistribution rights remain
unresolved.

All 23,651 ix:nonFraction occurrences in the captured HTML resolve to one local
context and unit under the implemented checks. This is a source-link inventory,
not 23,651 valid, distinct or normalized quantities. Hidden/nil/nested/repeated
facts, registry transformations, sign/scale, dimensions/defaults, taxonomy types,
duplicate consistency and displayed-table grounding still need validation.
Agreement between visible text and tags from the same filing is not independent
truth. Begin with a pinned conformant processor, original-filing identity and
deterministic rendered checks, then a fairly funded structured-reader baseline.

The candidate research question is whether selecting source witnesses that
distinguish competing entity/measure/period/unit/population/version bindings can
outperform equally funded structure-aware, relation-coverage and generic
sufficiency/ambiguity baselines. Missing evidence must remain unresolved, and an
answer needs positive support, not merely elimination of alternatives. The
supplied-judgment witness-selection scaffold has 14 authored contract tests,
including a deliberate greedy complementarity failure. It supplies neither
automatic candidate generation nor a validated source verifier. It is not a
validated method, approximation result or novelty claim.

Proceed only if natural source ambiguity remains after standard structured/XBRL
baselines, candidate-binding recall and verifier validity are adequate, and a
separately frozen entity-disjoint evaluation is feasible. Stop the specialized
algorithm route if conventional structure explains the benefit. The current work
supports a carefully bounded failure analysis and the next feasibility decision;
it does not yet justify an ACL 2027 submission claim.
