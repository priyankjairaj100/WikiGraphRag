Final scientific and aggregation audit: v24 task probe
=====================================================
2026-10-06

Conclusion
----------
The recorded conclusion is appropriately conservative. This probe does not
establish a novel retrieval advantage. The frozen two-history expansion threshold
is not met under the disclosed conservative post-retrieval interpretation: only
the LLY segment question is judged missing in every tested primary-budget policy,
and that is one issuer history. The PFE segment question
retains uncertainty under lexical retrieval and cannot supply the second
confirmed history. Reader competence, automatic coverage and confirmation remain
unestablished. No further experiment is required to state this checkpoint.

Audit scope and independence
----------------------------
Reviewed scripts/summarize_semantic_probe_v24.py and the recorded semantic,
exact-witness and replay-comparison artifacts. Rebuilt the semantic summary in
memory and compared it with the saved object; checked input hashes, complete
rosters, claim inventories, strict-verdict consistency and replay equality counts.
No retrieval, source-enlargement or model experiment was run for this audit.

The auditor is a separate assistant role from the root investigator and source
authors, but also performed the LLY pack review recorded separately. Therefore
this final audit is not an independent re-adjudication of all source truth and
must not be counted as a second independent LLY annotator. It does not turn
assistant annotations into human gold. For the other questions, the audit checks
the supplied review reasoning and aggregation rather than claiming a new complete
source/pack annotation pass.

Verified population and results
-------------------------------
All 24 unique prospectively scheduled item IDs remain in the reviewed source
artifact, matching the protocol exactly: 9 supported, 10 partial and 5 unresolved.
The partial/unresolved cases are not demonstrated globally unanswerable cases.
None was replaced. The semantic pack review covers the nine supported questions
and all four policies at the 4,096-word primary budget: 36 distinct pack records.

At the primary budget, the recorded counts on the nine supported questions are:

Policy          Exact recorded witnesses   All frozen reference claims   Question-level evidence
typed                       0/9                        0/9                   0/9
lexical                     3/9                        3/9                   4/9, plus 1 uncertain
structural                  4/9                        5/9                   7/9
decomposition               4/9                        5/9                   7/9

These columns measure different things. Exact source-interval inclusion can miss
an alternative typed card or table realization of the same premise. Full frozen
reference conjunctions can require detail unnecessary for the actual question.
The question-level column reflects a disclosed post-retrieval necessity review.
None of these columns is reader accuracy, model answer coverage, human-validated
semantic recall, or the measured performance of a new certificate policy.

The seven-question success counts use the nine source-supported development
questions as their denominator. They must not be described as 7/24 accuracy or
as evidence that the other 17 questions fail. The full source-status denominator
remains 24. Likewise, typed exact-span recall is zero partly by construction:
typed-card dependency anchors are not counted as rendered raw-source text.
The separate semantic review, rather than that mechanical zero, diagnoses what
information the typed packs actually contain.

Necessity and uncertainty handling
---------------------------------
The strict and question-level results are both retained. The changes affect PFE:

- The currency-growth question does not require an additional historical
  royalty-reclassification account when the retrieved current-filing comparison
  already gives the growth quantities, scope and operational/FX bridge.
- The stock-compensation question can be answered with the listed award-specific
  amounts and recognition periods without asserting an independently certified
  absence of all other outstanding award categories. The missing BPA-absence
  detail is not used to manufacture a failure of those packs.
- The lexical PFE segment pack is marked uncertain at question level, rather
  than turning absence of a fuller reference cost list into a confirmed residual.

The LLY stock review separately notes that a program-discontinuation explanation
is extra to the requested cost/period question. Its typed-pack failure rests on
missing recognition periods, not on that optional cause. The three duration
facts are source-tagged ix:nonNumeric, a conventional extraction-profile gap.
Ordinary text-capable packs already contain the requested LLY stock evidence.

Post-retrieval necessity adjudication is a useful correction to an over-detailed
reference for development diagnosis, but is not prospective confirmatory scoring.
The reference file is preserved and its strict score remains visible. Future
confirmatory references must fix required versus optional answer components
before predictions. The present record does not certify the substantive truth
of every assistant necessity judgment merely because its schema and hashes pass.

Aggregation and reproducibility checks
--------------------------------------
The stored semantic summary exactly equals a fresh in-memory build from its
recorded inputs. Every summary input hash matched. The 36 review rows cover
exactly nine questions times four policies; all claim IDs are unique within their
actual rows and match the frozen inventories. All actual strict overall labels
agree with the conjunction of their claim-level verdicts. Question necessity is
present under the two documented schema spellings claim_necessity/claimnecessity.

The classifier counts a question as an ordinary-baseline success when at least
one tested pack is sufficient, treats any remaining uncertain case conservatively,
and admits a confirmed residual only when all four packs are missing. This is
valid for asking whether a question survives all four baselines. It is not a
deployable per-question policy selector with free oracle choice. No such new
selector or method gain is claimed by the saved summary.
The protocol fixed the two-history candidate threshold but did not fully specify
this all-policy semantic decision algorithm. The later conservative interpretation
is disclosed in the research update; it is not a preregistered statistical test.

The replay receipt contains 288 unique scheduled cells. All 288 have identical
selected IDs/order, selected sets, rendered pack hashes, source interval unions
and word costs in the recorded original and controlled replay. The receipt's
aggregate counts agree with its rows. This is reproducibility evidence for these
runs, not evidence of semantic validity. The separate portability receipt records
floating-score differences across hash seeds on an authored fixture; that caveat
must remain visible despite identical selections in this corpus replay.

The interrupted initial retrieval attempt and its partial-artifact hashes are
preserved, as are the implementation amendments. It would be inaccurate to say
that no initial execution occurred. The record states that no initial outcome
contents were inspected, and that the fixes came from blind authored checks.
This audit verifies artifact identities, not private mental states or historical
blinding from hashes alone.

No observed aggregation error changes the recorded result. The audit identified
two latent validator gaps: set-only claim inventory comparison could admit
duplicate claim rows, and a trusted strict overall label could contradict claim
verdicts. The root investigator hardened both checks. Inspection of the updated
code and another in-memory build confirm that these guards are present and the
saved result remains unchanged. No retrieval rerun or result replacement was
needed. Threshold constants should remain explicitly tied to the governing
protocol when the script is reused for another study.

Limits on the strength of the baseline claim
--------------------------------------------
The implemented baselines are useful conventional controls with exact source
identities, numeric typed cards, BM25, structural closure and fixed clause-based
query decomposition. They are not an exhaustive test of competent retrieval.
There is no dense retriever, learned reranker or LLM query planner in this run.
Numeric-card ranking and fiscal-year routing are not a complete semantic
question-to-aspect interpreter. Duration-tag extraction and source-section query
planning still offer ordinary baseline improvements.

Structural versus lexical additionally changes source-date routing, so their
difference is a policy-package comparison, not a closure-only ablation. All
reported budgets are rendered whitespace words, not tokenizer counts or total
acquisition/compute cost. Reading/indexing the admitted source corpus remains
separate work. The source-reviewed LLY segment failure does not establish that
an adequate conventional reformulation would fail or that a new certificate
mechanism is needed.

Publication and next-stage decision
-----------------------------------
Keep v24 as a reproducible development task-discovery outcome with its negative
and uncertain findings. Do not launch or claim success on the 100 complete /
100 insufficient natural reader gate from this probe. Do not replace that gate
with the earlier typed controls, ordinary source annotations, or a source oracle.
No evaluated large-reader predictions, automatic proposer/verifier measurements,
new method comparison or fresh-issuer confirmation has occurred here.

The conservative two-history expansion criterion remains unmet. Any later task
family, source expansion or baseline amendment requires its own declared
development version; this probe must not be extended until it yields a win.
The current findings support retaining the infrastructure and closing this
bounded probe, while leaving the broader research hypothesis unresolved.
