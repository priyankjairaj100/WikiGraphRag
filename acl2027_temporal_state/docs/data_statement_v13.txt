V0.13 DATA, MODEL AND ANNOTATION STATEMENT
3 October 2026

Purpose and unit of analysis
This is a feasibility study of reading supplied version histories under bounded
retrieval. The two selected histories are Plug Power annual-report restatement
material and OPERA scientific-paper revisions. Eight development questions are
repeated across retrieval and rendering conditions. There are two histories,
not 32 independent examples. Some condition pairs have identical received text.

Sources and sampling
Four recovered PDF representations comprise 550 physical pages. Exact URLs,
source hashes, page counts and version membership are recorded in the v0.12
capture protocol and v0.13 corpus manifest. The sources were selected during
earlier project investigation; no random population sample is claimed. Current
representations are not certified as the exact first-public historical bytes.
JAMA access failures remain recorded and do not become admitted cases.

Language and people
The evaluated material and questions are in English. This study does not recruit
participants or collect new private personal records. It evaluates public
corporate reporting and scientific documents, with no claim about performance
on other languages, clinical questions or individual decision making.

Construction
Page-preserving extraction is chunked at 160 words with 30-word overlap. Two
OPERA v1 raster tables have tagged model-assisted transcriptions verified
against rendered tables. Other figures/equations are not globally certified as
OCR-complete. History assignment, version membership and order are supplied
development metadata. Fulltext is indexed within the known history; only six
selected chunks are sent to the reader. Layout follow-up preserves selected
words and restores internal source whitespace, with no added page evidence.

Annotation and review
Questions and reference facts are model-assisted. Separate review roles read
source evidence and relevant page images; they share project familiarity and
prior annotation roles. This is not independent human gold, a population study
or a blind annotation experiment. Question/reference amendments precede native
predictions. Required facts, scope, units and numerical tolerances are frozen.
Optional facts are not silently promoted to mandatory fields.

Model execution and grading
Qwen3-4B Q8_0 is a pinned small native reader, not a certified strong baseline.
The pinned model/runtime identifiers and hashes are in the frozen execution
records. Pretraining exposure is unknown. A source-only instruction is not proof
of knowledge isolation. Thirty-four planned native completions include two
authored controls and two sixteen-response batches; final receipts determine
the actual completed count. Source-bearing raw responses remain unchanged.

Condition labels are masked in domain grading packets. This does not establish
statistical blindness: context can reveal the method and reviewers have prior
development familiarity. Per-fact credit, whole-answer content, scope, citations,
format, output-budget failures and partial abstention are retained separately.
Adjudication overlays preserve original grader judgments and identify any
changed field, rule and source artifact. No reference is repaired from answers.

Availability and rights
The GitHub release contains project-authored code, protocols, questions,
references, bounded source paraphrases, capture/corpus/retrieval metadata,
token and answer hashes, output-free native process projections, model-assisted
grading rationales, reproducible descriptive joins, paper source and the readable
working PDF. Full third-party PDFs, their fulltext derivatives, source-bearing
prompts, token arrays, raw native responses, downloaded weights and runtime
binaries are outside the public package. Their exact hashes and reconstruction
requirements are recorded; this is a real limitation on independent semantic
regrading from the public package alone.

No blanket new license is assigned to third-party material or to the entire
repository. Follow original source and model licenses when obtaining them.
Original submission artifacts and their supplied license remain historical
material. Fresh downloads can change bytes. An exact audit requires matching
external inputs; metadata consistency is not the same as source occurrence,
semantic correctness or bitwise repeatability of future native computation.

Writing and release status
The working manuscript and code were prepared with model assistance. Human
authors remain responsible for scientific claims, source/citation checks,
authorship and final submission decisions. This public development repository
is not itself an anonymized submission package. The final paper requires its
actual venue format and current disclosure requirements before submission.
