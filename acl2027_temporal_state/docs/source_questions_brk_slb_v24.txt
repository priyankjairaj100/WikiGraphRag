Results
-------
Eight records: 1 supported, 5 partial, 2 unresolved, 0 not applicable.

BRK-B currency growth: partial. The FY2022 statement gives $302,089 million for
2022 and comparative $276,203 million for 2021. The derived increase is $25,886
million, approximately 9.3721%. This is arithmetic on displayed values, not a
quoted issuer growth rate. No consolidated constant-currency or operational
counterpart was located. Individual businesses' currency effects do not fill it.

BRK-B segment presentation: partial. Both notes list GEICO, Berkshire Hathaway
Primary Group, Berkshire Hathaway Reinsurance Group, BNSF, BHE, Manufacturing,
McLane, and Service and retailing. A generic prior-year financial-statement
reclassification statement does not identify a segment recast. Matching labels
do not prove every accounting population or allocation stayed unchanged.

BRK-B free cash flow: unresolved. Operating cash flow and capital expenditure
information is available, but no issuer-defined FCF measure/reconciliation was
located. No reviewer-created subtraction is presented as the issuer's measure.

BRK-B stock compensation: unresolved. No unrecognized SBC balance and recognition
period were located. The unrecognized tax-benefit disclosure is a different item.
No zero balance or absence of award programs is inferred.

SLB currency growth: partial. FY2023 reported revenue grew 18%, to about $33.1
billion. No constant-currency/operational counterpart was located. The percentage
of revenue denominated in US dollars and foreign-currency expense effects cannot
be substituted for a constant-currency growth calculation.

SLB segment presentation: partial. Both years describe Digital & Integration,
Reservoir Performance, Well Construction, and Production Systems, with matching
functional descriptions. No explicit comparative segment-recast statement was
located. No source-completeness or unchanged-allocation certificate is implied.

SLB free cash flow: supported. Its issuer definition deducts capital expenditures,
APS investments, and capitalized exploration-data costs from operating cash flow.
FY2023 amounts in millions: 6,637 - 1,939 - 507 - 153 = 4,038. The displayed bridge
has zero arithmetic residual. That says nothing about undisclosed unrounded
amounts. SLB labels this non-GAAP liquidity measure and distinguishes it from
residual discretionary cash. The definition is necessary: cash flow minus only
capital expenditure would produce a different number.

SLB stock compensation: partial. The note discloses $278 million of aggregate
unrecognized cost at December 31, 2023, scheduled as $164 million in 2024,
$89 million in 2025, $21 million in 2026, and $4 million in 2027. It does not supply
a weighted-average recognition period in the reviewed passage, and none was
located elsewhere by the bounded searches. Year buckets do not establish precise
within-year recognition timing or an issuer-reported weighted average.

Interpretation
--------------
SLB FCF is one source-supported definition/reconciliation candidate. This review
does not show that automatic retrieval misses it or that any proposed method
beats ordinary footnote/structural retrieval. All negatives remain in the probe.
Partial/unresolved labels describe this bounded annotation effort; they are not
ground-truth labels of global unanswerability or natural insufficient-evidence
reader items. Source-supported partial claims remain separately available.

Reproduction
------------
From acl2027_temporal_state:
python scripts/inspect_questions_brk_slb_v24.py --external /path/to/source/files

The script checks all four source hashes against the frozen protocol, checks each
selected node boundary, constructs span hashes, and records lexical occurrence
counts and reviewed spans. The script recreates disclosed assistant selections;
it is not an independent semantic adjudicator. The downloaded source HTML and
inspection text are external and are not added to the public repository.
