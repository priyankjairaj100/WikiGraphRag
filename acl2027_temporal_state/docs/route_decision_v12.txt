Research route decision v0.12

Decision

Stop expanding the current incremental-propagation contribution. The bounded
pilot recovers usable version pairs but supplies no certified nondirect
consequence on which dependency propagation adds value beyond direct correction.
This is a decision about the present evidence and research investment, not a
claim that such consequences never occur. The eight-history feasibility target
is not met, and no new solver, graph feature or artificial dependency should be
introduced to manufacture a positive result.

What changed

All eight frozen URLs were attempted once. Six returned HTTP 200 and parsed:
two Plug Power annual reports, two OPERA papers and their separate abstract
pages. The two JAMA URLs returned HTTP 403 challenge pages. Failed access is
retained and is not a semantic rejection of the underlying article. There were
no new searches, retries, sign-ins, challenge bypasses or native model calls.

Plug provides one controlled-version direct-correction development control.
Its loss and dependent EPS are both explicitly revised. Unchanged service
revenue and share count are freshly reaffirmed in the restatement table; this
does not restore blanket reliability to the earlier financial statements.
Unknown first-public availability remains unknown. This control is not a
historically clock-certified replay, benchmark admission or propagation gain.

OPERA provides a complete version pair but changes the selected event set as
well as correcting timing. Arrival time, relative velocity and the conclusion
are explicitly revised. The pair therefore does not satisfy the unchanged
same-scope correction core. Its retained geodetic information can inform a
separate reading task, but cannot turn changed-scope reanalysis into a pure
correction experiment. Version labels and current hashes do not certify that
the downloaded bytes were exactly those first made public at the stated time.

Narrow next experiment

Study paired-version, scope-aware reading using the two recovered PDF pairs as
development material. Do not call the task residual-document inconsistency:
these reviews have not established such an inconsistency in the complete
documents. Do not relabel this new task as a successful longitudinal method
evaluation. The current JAMA passage diagnostic remains separate.

The next executable increment is one reader experiment and error analysis:

1. Freeze at most eight source-supported questions across the two histories.
   Cover a revised result, a retained result, the stated revision reason and
   whether the relevant scope is unchanged. Omit an unsupported question type;
   do not force a quota. Freeze separate reviewed answers and page/table
   evidence before predictions, including scope-changed and not-established
   outcomes where appropriate. Disclose development familiarity and model
   assistance; these are not independent human gold or held-out histories.
2. Use all complete PDF text as a searchable corpus. The reader receives a
   bounded retrieved context, so describe this as full-document access, not
   full-document model input. Retain page boundaries and verify relevant table
   extraction. Record retrieved passages, tokens and coverage for every answer.
   Oracle assertion IDs and annotated dependency graphs are not reader inputs.
3. Compare ordinary retrieval-and-reading with paired-version retrieval-and-
   reading under the same reader, question and evidence budget. The paired
   comparator deliberately retrieves from both versions; it is an engineering
   comparator, not an asserted novel algorithm. Record any corpus, retrieval,
   prompt or context differences explicitly.
4. Establish basic reader competence with short, plainly supported controls
   before the main run. Freeze checkpoint, prompt, decoding, resource caps,
   scoring and failure handling. The prior 4B schema failures do not establish
   a strong reader baseline. Do not promote small-model failures into proof of
   a general research problem.
5. Score factual content, scope, citation support and output format separately.
   Keep raw outputs and failures; no post-output reference repair. Report
   retrieval, extraction and reading failures separately. No oracle-context
   result may be presented as ordinary retrieval performance.

Go/no-go

If ordinary reading handles the questions, stop method development around
these examples. If paired evidence fixes errors, the result is only a two-
history development signal for revision-aware retrieval. Expand only after a
real failure mode survives a competent matched baseline. If extraction or
model competence fails, resolve that upstream limitation before claiming a
reasoning contribution. Any publishable method claim still needs independently
held-out histories, stronger comparisons, uncertainty, ablations and a current
novelty audit. None is established by this checkpoint.

Execution boundary

This checkpoint completes source access, source-only review and the route
decision. It does not execute the proposed reader experiment or repeat oracle
support lookups already known to tie. The existing correction core, all prior
results and all direct correction edges remain unchanged. The proposed next
design must be frozen before new predictions; no paid compute or unattended
work has been initiated.
