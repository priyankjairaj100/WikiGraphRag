The recipe ranks exact source units with BM25.
It selects at most 30 units and three units per source.
It preserves exact UTF-8 source spans.
C0 uses at most 512 evidence tokens.
C1 uses at most 1,280 evidence tokens and retains every selected C0 unit.
Packing reserves 768 output tokens and 768 previous-output tokens for revision arms.
It also reserves 128 tokens for boundary effects.
Whole units can be omitted before predictions; units are never silently truncated.
