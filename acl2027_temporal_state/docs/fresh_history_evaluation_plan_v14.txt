Fresh-history comparison-evidence evaluation proposal v0.14
3 October 2026 — design proposal, not a frozen experiment or acquired benchmark

Decision and immediate increment

Start with a bounded, source-first audit of eight previously unexposed issuer
histories, each containing two consecutive annual reports. Multi-Doc-2025 is the
best currently identified source pool for that audit because its dataset repository exposes issuer/year HTML filings. Admit exact source representations,
then ask whether genuine comparison-binding ambiguities recur. Do not implement a
specialized selector merely because it might fix Plug Power or OPERA.

The next deliverable is an eight-group acquisition feasibility packet: a pinned
metadata-only inventory, exposure exclusions, deterministic source order, at most
16 source captures, exact identity/layout checks, and an admission/failure ledger.
No new source history, dataset download, question, split or model execution was
completed for this proposal. Metadata access is not acquisition or admission.

This route evaluates evidence needed to warrant comparisons: entity, metric,
period, unit, population, version and structural attachment. It is separate from
the closed search for nondirect same-authority whole-assertion correction
propagation. Successive reports can be useful here without being corrections.
A changed sample or definition is a valid comparison-scope issue, not proof of
replacement of the same assertion. Keep the old admission/result history intact.

Existing material is development only

The v07 frame targeted 20 histories but actually registered 19 metadata histories;
its HS2 correction lead was within an existing history. The v08 capture ledger
records 39 attempts,32 provisionally usable sources and 15 complete provisional
histories. None becomes held out by being renamed. All source registers, discovery
candidates, acquired documents and old questions remain exposed development
material, including the familiar four PDFs/two histories/eight v13 questions.
The proposal JSON binds the exact inventory and prior-work file hashes.

The relevant prior-art requirements are already documented in related_work_v14,
method_hypothesis_v14 and related_work_hcrag_followup_v14. Reuse those findings.
HC-RAG is an explicit baseline; structural graphs, intent routing, generic evidence
closure, missing-slot retrieval, sufficiency and interpretation enumeration are
not claimed inventions. This proposal adds evaluation mechanics, not a novelty
certificate. Any distinct claim must survive those baselines at charged budgets.

Public starting pools: checked metadata and remaining gaps

Multi-Doc-2025 reports 2,327 questions,179 filings and 87 companies for FY 2022–2024.
Its S3 cross-year subset has 243 questions, and S5 has 122 combined comparisons.
The card declares CC BY 4.0 and exposes company/year/structural fields with
section-name evidence. The paper describes generated candidates, rule filtering
and expert review; these are not naturally occurring user queries. Its official
split separates primary companies but allows supporting comparison companies
across splits. Rebuild the split over every answer-bearing entity. The inspected
source listing contains ticker_year HTML files; verify each against the original
SEC filing/accession. Fine source spans and revision-pair labels are not supplied
by the inspected standard schema. A later report may alone answer a cross-year
question; do not force two-document retrieval. [D1–D3]

LOFin offers 1,595 QA pairs over 145,897 SEC documents. Evidence objects identify
document names and pages, which could support a secondary retrieval comparison.
Its card declares CC BY-NC-ND 4.0; the maintainer says the formerly encrypted
benchmark became public in May 2026. A viewer schema-cast failure was observed,
so validate individual files instead of assuming a uniform loader. Do not
materialize the entire corpus for this pilot. Page numbering/source matching,
release compatibility and document-revision relations remain to be checked. [D4–D6]

DocFinQA has 7,437 FinQA-derived questions over 801 complete filings. The exposed
schema is Context, Question, Program and Answer; its card declares MIT. The
full-report text is useful, but upstream document/page identity joins must be
reconstructed before source-bound history evaluation. Dataset-maintenance
versions are not annual-report revisions. Use it later for a separately labeled
long-document/numerical comparison, not the first history-admission pool. [D7–D9]

Dataset license labels do not establish blanket redistribution permission for
underlying reports. Keep dataset, code and source-document terms separate. Where
source redistribution is unresolved, release hashes, URLs, locators and recovery
code rather than silently bundling third-party captures.

Metadata pages automatically exposed public QA previews during these checks.
AAL and displayed Multi-Doc entities are now development-exposed. The JSON records
a minimum exclusion list, explicitly incomplete. Finish a preview-ID/entity
exposure ledger before any split; never call these examples unseen. Public
benchmark material also has unknown pretraining contamination even when it is
unseen by this project's evaluators.

Deterministic source-first sampling and split

1. Pin the metadata inventory and source listing, release revision, license
   records and retrieval date. Build a source-only view without question/answer
   text. Resolve canonical issuer IDs and related corporate entities. Group all
   report years, duplicates, reprints and translations. Do not merge companies
   merely because they are incidentally mentioned; link entities participating
   in the answer evidence or belonging to the relevant corporate group.
2. For the pilot, use the fixed 2022–2024 window. Eligibility requires two
   consecutive annual-report years with resolvable source identity. Exclude the
   union of prior project exposures and preview exposures. No selection based on
   answer changes, correction existence, difficulty or expected model behavior.
3. Order eligible issuers by SHA256(seed + issuer_id), with seed proposed as
   WikiGraphRag-fresh-comparison-v14-20261003. For each issuer choose its first
   consecutive pair in a separately hashed pair order. Freeze all IDs/URLs and
   any reserve order before acquisition. Attempt the first eight groups, up to
   two retrieval attempts per URL. Record every failed attempt; no silent
   replacement, changed seed or criteria relaxation to achieve eight admissions.
4. If the pilot supports further investment, prospectively pin a broader official
   SEC 10-K metadata frame for the same 2022–2024 window, excluding every exposed
   group. This is a separate acquisition stage, not a retroactive pilot fallback.
   Proposed full-study target:60 groups, allocated 12 development,8 validation and
   40 untouched test groups in a single deterministic hash order. Keep all
   report pairs/questions for a group together. Target four eligible questions
   per group; do not manufacture that count. Counts and statistical power are
   feasibility targets, not acquired data or promised significance.
5. Assign test groups from metadata before test bodies/questions/references are
   used for method development. Test annotation roles may see full sources;
   method authors may not tune on those references. For an optional cross-company
   track, co-locate all supporting entities or exclude cross-split questions with
   a ledger. Primary-company-only disjointness is insufficient. Check template
   and near-duplicate leakage; reserve a family-disjoint secondary evaluation
   before claiming generalization beyond the financial-report family.

Admission needs evidence, not just transport

Record response/redirect/type/hash separately from source identity, completeness,
extraction fidelity, authority, history relation and historical availability.
Verify issuer, reporting period and form/accession or publisher identifier from
actual source content. Preserve complete HTML/PDF plus exact extraction recipe,
text/layout hashes and rendered spot checks. An HTTP 200/title/full PDF alone is
not admission of a valid comparison reference.

An exact captured document-version comparison can proceed with an unknown
first-public-availability clock. It cannot be sold as historical replay. Record
filing dates, report periods, observation periods and retrieval time separately.
Likewise, repeated numerical digits do not establish comparable definitions or
populations. Label source-supported relation, changed scope or unresolved.
No nondirect-dependency requirement and no positive-correction quota apply here.

Separate source truth from extraction and retrieval. A fact visible in the PDF
but missing from text is source-answerable/representation-incomplete; an absent
source is a capture failure; an irreducibly ambiguous source is not an extraction
error. Keep these denominators instead of excluding every difficult example.

Questions and annotation

Use unmodified real documents for the primary natural-source track. After source
selection and split assignment, deterministically sample relevant pre-existing
question IDs and preserve their provenance/wording. A source-authored extension
is separately tagged: reviewers enumerate up to four concise analyst-style
questions from fixed Item 7/Item 8 material and explicit report cross-references,
then apply the frozen selection rule. These are questions authored over natural
sources, not observed user queries. Do not demand two source documents when one
later comparative report is sufficient.

The primary task asks for reported quantities, their scope and warranted
relations, without computing new numbers. Existing arithmetic questions belong
to a separately frozen calculator-enabled secondary cohort. Never relax the
current no-recomputation contract quietly to make a dataset fit.

Include ordinary unambiguous questions, unchanged results, changed-scope
comparisons, later-only sufficient evidence and naturally unresolved cases at
their observed rates. A structural-feature-enriched diagnostic may be reported
separately, with its complete sampling denominator. Do not retain only cases
where the proposed method has an apparent advantage.

Author attachment stress tests separately: swap or withhold headers, units,
periods, footnotes or continuation cues while balancing token count, lexical
clues, entities, years and numbers. Record the intervention, preserve the
original and randomize presentation. Artificial withholding is not natural
missingness; stress accuracy never enters the primary natural-source score.

Before any predictions, annotate required claims, possible valid answer actions,
entity/metric/period/unit/population/version bindings, material plausible
alternatives, exact source witnesses and alternative sufficient evidence sets.
Use physical pages and line/character spans for PDFs, immutable object/DOM spans
for HTML, and rendered checks for merged cells/multiline headers. A page number
alone is not evidence of the required relation. Units and exceptions need their
own source witnesses. Allow jointly sufficient bundles and later-only evidence.

Use supported/contradicted/unresolved labels. Missing evidence is not contradiction;
a single surviving hypothesis without positive source support is not success.
Review content, scope, citations, partial abstention and format separately.
Two genuinely independent source-aware reviewers and adjudication are proposed
for final test references. Shared-session model-assisted roles can prepare
provisional labels but do not create independent human gold. Annotation resources
remain unresolved; that limitation must remain explicit if no human review occurs.

Failure attribution must distinguish source unavailable, source unresolved,
extraction loss, candidate-source miss, correct-binding proposal miss, lost witness
in packing, false verifier contradiction, and reader error despite complete
received evidence. Gold bindings/reference pages are never inference inputs.
Oracle evidence/hypothesis runs are diagnostic ceilings only.

Baseline and budget contract

Run a ladder with the same source corpus, faithful representation, competent
reader, abstention policy and token accounting:

- Lexical BM25 as a legacy diagnostic; dense semantic and hybrid retrieval with
  strong reranking as serious candidate-recall baselines.
- Hybrid retrieval with parent/table/section expansion and generic structural
  closure. All verified source witnesses must be available symmetrically.
- HC-RAG-style hierarchical/intent-aware text-table routing, labeled a controlled
  adaptation unless the released procedure is actually reproduced.
- HiREC/S2G/RINSE-inspired generic gap/sufficiency complementary retrieval with
  the same relation slots, pointer access and query/controller permissions.
- ToC/REFUEL-inspired interpretation enumeration, reranking and scope pruning,
  using the same automatically proposed alternatives as the specialized selector.
- Relation-slot coverage/diversity packing and relevance-matched witness choice.
- Only then the proposed incompatible-binding witness policy.

For objective isolation, freeze the candidate evidence pool and hypothesis
proposal process across methods; separately report whole-pipeline candidate
retrieval. Ablate competition, separating-witness choice, action-weighted versus
relation coverage, proposal weights and complete-bundle packing one at a time.
Do not remove structure from a comparator while retaining it for the proposal.

Proposed primary ceilings:3,500 full final-input tokens,384 answer tokens,
14,000 cumulative model-input tokens and four total model calls per question,
including proposal/controller/verifier/final generation. Retrieval allows the
initial pass plus two updates. All controller outputs, source IDs, hypotheses,
headers, units and retries count. Cheap baselines may use fewer calls; do not pad
them. Give them equal ceilings for generic reranking/querying and report actual
cost. Full-context curves at 1,800/3,500/7,000 tokens need separate feasibility
checks and compatible context windows; the larger-context parent baseline must
be visible so a tight-budget-only effect is not presented as universal.

Pin reader/template/quantization/tokenizer/decoding using development-only
complete-source controls. Proposed entry gate:at least 90% grounded scope-correct
answers on 32 checked cases and no more than one critical unit/sign/year/population
error. Passing is permission for the experiment, not proof of general competence.
Pack overlapping source spans once and retain whole required dependency bundles;
no answer-aware backfill, invisible free metadata or stripping of source whitespace.

Candidate validity and scaling

Primary proposal cap:K=8 alternative bindings, M=64 source bundles. Measure correct
scoped-binding recall before selection, material-alternative recall and retention
of valid bindings after every verifier stage. Record beam truncation/unknown
alternatives. False source contradiction is distinct from unresolved support.

Naive M*K*(K-1)/2 checks would yield 1,792 bundle/pair candidates. Those are not
free LLM calls. Factorized source-conditioned judgments/caches must obey the same
four-call cap, with all inputs charged. Vary K and report the correct candidates
lost by pruning. No heuristic approximation theorem is assumed.

For scaling, propose 16/160/1,600-file indexes with metadata-sampled relevant and
unrelated distractors fixed across methods, independent of answers. Report index
build/storage, P50/P95 query latency, peak memory, actual calls/tokens, evidence
recall and answer support. These are planned experiments, not downloaded corpora
or demonstrated scaling behavior.

Metrics and proposed publication investment gates

The primary metric is fully grounded, scope-correct answer coverage among
source-answerable natural-source questions. A numeric match without supported
scope/citations does not pass. Report unsupported comparison claims at matched
answer coverage, selective-risk curves, appropriate abstention, candidate recall,
false elimination and full costs. Tune confidence thresholds and select the
strongest generic comparator on validation; freeze analysis before test.
Use paired question comparisons with uncertainty clustered by entity/history,
not an independence assumption over many questions from one report.

These numerical gates are proposals to finalize after feasibility and a power
review; none is an achieved result, preregistered guarantee or ACL acceptance rule:

- Data investment:at least six admissible pairs from the eight attempted groups.
  Otherwise pause and resolve source/provenance access, retaining every failure.
- Residual question:in at least 24 audited natural-source questions across six
  groups, find at least six resolvable answer-changing binding ambiguities across
  four groups. If the phenomenon is rarer, stop specialized-selector investment;
  do not select a hard-only evaluation to force prevalence.
- Reference quality:at least 90% independent initial agreement on scope/support,
  followed by adjudication of every material disagreement. Without independent
  reviewers, retain the provisional model-assisted label and limited claims.
- Candidate gate on validation:95% correct-binding recall,85% material-alternative
  recall, and at most 2% false elimination of valid bindings. Repair the proposal/
  verifier before testing selector gains if it fails.
- Confirmatory target:40 untouched test groups and 160 eligible questions, with
  cluster-based power assessed on development variation. A smaller yield supports
  a descriptive pilot, not an automatic superiority claim.
- Method investment:at least five percentage points more fully grounded coverage
  than the validation-selected strongest generic baseline at the primary ceilings,
  with the 95% history-clustered interval for improvement above zero. Unsupported
  comparison risk must not worsen; claimed risk reduction must survive matching
  answer coverage. Report every predeclared condition, not the best-looking cap.
- Robustness:the direction should persist with a second competent reader and a
  larger-context structural baseline. Authored stress, one template, oracle
  hypotheses and uncharged extra calls cannot establish the method claim.

Stop the distinct algorithmic route when conventional structure, generic gaps or
interpretation retrieval explains the benefit; candidate/verifier failures negate
it; source references remain unreliable; natural ambiguity is too rare; or prior
work already implements the same mechanism. A careful reproduction, resource or
failure-analysis result may be the defensible paper direction. Do not promise
ACL 2027 acceptance or a positive innovation before that decision is supported.
