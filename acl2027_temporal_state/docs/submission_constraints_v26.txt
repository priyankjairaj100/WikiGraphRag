Paper format

Short submissions allow four content pages.
Long submissions allow eight content pages.
Figures and tables count within those limits.
References have no page limit.
Place a required Limitations section after the conclusion and before references.
Limitations and optional ethical considerations do not count toward the content limit.
They cannot contain extra methods, results, or analysis.
Appendices follow references and normally use two columns.
Math-heavy appendices can use the specified exception.
The main paper must support its claims without requiring reviewers to read appendices.
ACL grants one additional content page after acceptance.
This extra page does not apply to the review submission.

Use the official ACL template without changing its style files.
Use A4 pages, two columns, line numbers, and page numbers for review.
Embed all PDF fonts.
Do not compress margins, fonts, or spacing to evade page limits.
