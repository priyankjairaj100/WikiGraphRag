Interpretation for the research paper

A conventional taxonomy-aware processor now provides observed normalized-fact
and English-label outputs for this fixed 12-source sample. Basic inline-number
normalization and label lookup should therefore be treated as baseline
capabilities. The prospective contribution must be demonstrated through
evidence selection, context/period disambiguation, verified reader behavior or
other measured improvements over that baseline. None of this run establishes
natural QA gains, held-out accuracy or method novelty.

DTS validation is requested, with SEC filing rules, calculation checking, UTR
checking and formulas disabled as declared. The original HF source bytes are
preserved; exact identity with original SEC filing bodies remains unverified.
