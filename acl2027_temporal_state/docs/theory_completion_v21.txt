The formal contract fixes complete joint answers, with every requested source
version, material scope binding and comparison relation. Positive source
support and unique query resolution are separate guarantees. A certificate
needs complete positive support, explicit refutation of every different-action
candidate, conflict freedom, a justified valid-class coverage assertion and
the declared budget. Missing evidence is unresolved, not refutation.

Completed results

1. Source-relative soundness and a weaker target-preservation corollary.
2. Exhaustive finite-map decision completeness and conditional semantic
   completeness under witness expressibility, joint budget and complete search.
3. Error localization into representation, candidate-coverage, false-support
   and false-elimination events, with the correct answer-rate denominator for
   selective risk and an explicit adaptive-call qualification.
4. Transcript-restricted coverage impossibility and concrete failure cases
   for missing support, class representatives, source semantics, conjunctions,
   version collapse, zero-weight rivals and conflicting judgments.
5. Exact Boolean/MILP formulation, exact oracle-cost search, classical set-cover
   embedding and a fixed-lookahead counterexample with unbounded cost ratio.
6. Restricted classical cover bound min(m, rho H_m) under complete unique
   premise-action sets, conflict freedom and additive union-resource costs.
   This is an attributed conditional adaptation, not a new general cover
   theorem, and it does not preserve an arbitrary hard budget.
7. A non-anticipating certificate-directed acquisition specification: predicted
   witness plans rank actions; acquired, verified premises alone authorize.
   It has no claimed online approximation guarantee.

Implementation and checks

src/temporal_state/certificate_v21.py is a strict reference checker and exact
subset solver over at most 20 supplied evidence actions. It handles conjunctive
premises, shared atoms, every rival candidate, global conflicts, same-class
survivors, an explicit coverage assertion and arbitrary deterministic nonnegative
integer/Fraction cost. It never prunes on an unjustified monotone-cost premise.
Search-cap refusal is not infeasibility; a local optimum with open coverage is
not answer authorization. Atomic normalization and semantic labels are inputs.

36 targeted unit tests and 9 authored finite controls pass. A separate model-role
review also checks the math and code and records its corrections. These are
theory/implementation checks, not human peer review, natural QA measurements or
empirical evidence that the semantic premises hold. The historical 1,477-test
integrated checkpoint is retained; it was not relabeled as a newly executed
full suite. The new module does not alter frozen v14/v18 behavior.
