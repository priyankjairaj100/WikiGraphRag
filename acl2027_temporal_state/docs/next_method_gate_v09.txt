NEXT METHOD GATE — v0.9 (declared before inspecting research scores)

The selected route remains correction-dependent state reconstruction under uncertain factual interpretations. A larger scorer passing a four-statement challenge would establish a useful execution path, not a new algorithm or a strong benchmark baseline.

The next substantive method experiment must compare correction handling under identical source access. New leadership successions and schedule revisions supply useful modality and state-change controls, but they do not by themselves exercise explicit correction or withdrawal of dependent conclusions. Do not count them as positive evidence for correction-specific value.

1. Prioritize new primary-source histories containing explicit factual correction or retraction, plus an observable downstream claim whose support changes. Keep ordinary succession and plan changes as controls. Distinguish current-document streams from verified historical versions. Record failed searches and unsuitable histories without inventing dependencies to meet a quota.
2. Convert reviewed source semantics to executable candidates only after defining mappings for modality, actual events, planned timing, scope, co-holders, and unsupported dates. A semantic annotation is not already a decoder input. Preserve plausible alternatives and measure proposal coverage separately; do not use reference answers to prune them.
3. Run a pinned full-prefix reader and a contextual independent candidate scorer before optimizing joint search. All methods receive the same complete eligible source text and proposals. If context exceeds a model budget, report the exclusion or an explicitly distinct context policy; do not silently truncate.
4. Compare no correction handling, whole-assertion replacement, dependency-aware withdrawal, and ordinary iterative revision under shared evidence and scoring. Keep the active-support objective failure from v0.6 visible. Exact optimization cannot repair an objective that rewards the wrong state.
5. Inspect residual errors by candidate omission, source entailment, correction targeting, unsupported persistence, dependency withdrawal, and search failure. Novel search is motivated only by errors that remain after good proposals and scoring and survive strong ordinary iterative baselines.
6. Establish answer agreement across alternative feasible states before calling an output certain. Compare accuracy, abstention, retained evidence, cost and runtime at history level; repeated prefixes and authored statement contrasts are not independent samples.

The existing twenty-history development gate remains unfinished. No test split, human gold, naturally sampled accuracy estimate, publishable effect, or method novelty follows from the present diagnostic. The decision is whether a narrowly justified correction/dependency method can produce an empirical advantage over the strongest same-source baseline. If it cannot, narrow the paper rather than inflate the claim.
