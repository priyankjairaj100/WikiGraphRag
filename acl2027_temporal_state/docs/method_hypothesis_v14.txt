Counterfactual distinguishing-witness retrieval: a falsifiable hypothesis v0.14

Status and investment decision

This is a method hypothesis and challenge specification, not an implementation,
preregistration, novelty claim, or demonstrated natural-data gain. It is authored
independently of the eight v0.13 question wordings and reference page sets. Those
questions, their outputs and the two familiar histories cannot become its test
set. Prior project familiarity and model-assisted authorship remain disclosed.

There is a potentially useful objective beyond collecting relevant facts: spend
a limited evidence budget on source material that distinguishes interpretations
which would produce different scoped answers. A system might have every value,
entity, year, unit and sample phrase somewhere in its context yet still lack the
relations that attach those phrases correctly. Retrieving another value-bearing
passage need not resolve that ambiguity.

This is not inherently new mathematics. With a finite set of hypotheses and
individually separating witnesses, it is decision-weighted test-cover or active
information selection. Strong evidence-sufficiency, pointer-grounding or
structure-aware retrieval can already implement the same behavior. A defensible
paper would therefore need a specific residual failure, a reliable way to
construct and test competing attachments without gold metadata, and an advantage
over matched strong versions of those mechanisms. If a simple source-structural
closure solves the problem at the same cost, stop developing this as a separate
algorithmic contribution.

1. Task and representation

Input: a question q; a corpus of source documents with physical/version identity;
and budgets for retrieval/verifier computation and final reader input. Source
spans have immutable anchors: document hash, physical page or native object,
coordinates/offsets, table/section identity where established, and exact text.
A source citation supplies evidence only when the received span contains the
needed assertion or relation. A document ID or nearby unit token alone is not a
scope certificate.

Represent a candidate answer-bearing item by a value anchor plus possible
bindings to entity, measure, reporting/observation interval, unit/scale,
population/inclusion rule, analysis definition and document version. These are
relations with source witnesses, not freely supplied labels. Unknown is a real
state; a parser's proposed attachment must remain distinguishable from an
explicit source statement. This task does not certify first-public availability
from a version date or merge publisher and asserting authority.

A hypothesis h contains a locally consistent assignment of these bindings and,
for comparison questions, a proposed relation between two results. Candidate
relations may include a comparable change, explicitly stated correction,
changed-scope reanalysis, retained quantity, or unresolved comparison. Their
precise operational definitions would need independent source review before an
experiment. None should be inferred solely from document recency.

Let a_q(h) be the answer action implied by h, including its value, scope and
comparison relation. Identical answer strings can still represent incompatible
actions if they assign the number to different entities, periods or samples.
Conversely, harmless alternative parses which yield the same scoped answer need
not be separated. The incompatibility relation I_q(h,g) is thus query-relevant,
symmetric, and defined before outputs, not a reward for matching a generated
answer string.

Construct a finite proposal set H from ambiguous source structure and query
bindings, not from answer keys: for example two plausible table-continuation
links or two column-year assignments that are unresolved in the initial
retrieval. Include an explicit unmodeled/unknown alternative in the reporting
layer. Do not fill H with arbitrary false statements simply to make elimination
easy. Hypothesis construction, structural proposals and verifier calls are part
of the method's cost and error surface, not oracle preprocessing.

2. A selection objective

For evidence E, define V(E) as hypotheses not explicitly contradicted by jointly
available source evidence. Source absence, failed retrieval, lack of repetition
in a newer document, and a verifier's inability to prove a hypothesis are not
contradictions. Inference remains three-valued: supported, contradicted or
unresolved. Comparisons requiring several links use a complete support bundle;
one page label cannot stand in for the other links.

With fixed nonnegative proposal weights w_h, one development objective is:

  L(E) = sum over unordered {h,g} subset V(E) of
         w_h * w_g * I_q(h,g)

  choose E to minimize L(E), subject to Cost(E) <= B,
  nonempty V(E), and the support/unknown constraints below.

Equivalently, maximize the weight of incompatible pairs that evidence rules
out. Initial weights remain fixed during this selection objective so that
renormalization does not create a misleading apparent information gain. Uniform
weights provide an essential baseline; model confidence is not assumed to be
calibrated. A worst-case version can prioritize the largest surviving
answer-changing ambiguity rather than common alternatives. These are candidate
objectives, not simultaneously optimized or retrospectively selected outcomes.

Positive support is an additional requirement. Eliminating every proposal gives
zero pair loss but no warranted answer, so it is prohibited as a success. Even a
single surviving hypothesis is insufficient if the requested fact and all
necessary attachments lack source support, or if material unmodeled ambiguity
remains. Return an answer only when its claims have complete supported bindings
and all remaining modeled alternatives yield compatible actions; otherwise
withhold the unsupported part. Unknown hypothesis mass cannot be magically
removed by a finite beam. Calibration and source-only coverage audits must test
this limitation, and the result is never advertised as a proof over all possible
interpretations.

Cost(E) counts the union of actual source spans and their citation/structure
labels in final input tokens, including required headers, units, continuation
links and footnotes. Overlap does not cost twice, and a shared header is not free.
An indivisible relation bundle that cannot fit is a budget failure, not permission
to truncate the very witness that grounds it. Separate costs include candidate
retrieval, page/OCR access, hypothesis generation, verification and indexing.
A system that reads all candidates with a powerful verifier before packing them
has used that extra computation and must not claim a cheap retriever advantage.

This objective is distinct from unary slot coverage only when the latter treats
presence of entity/year/unit/sample tokens as sufficient and ignores competing
attachments. It is not distinct from a sufficiency objective which already asks
whether every answer-changing interpretation is excluded. If RINSE-style
sufficiency or a S2G-RAG-style pointer model implements that stronger criterion,
our objective can be a reformulation or controlled adaptation, not a new family.

3. Minimal authored counterexample, and exactly what it proves

Consider an invented two-table report, unrelated to the development corpus.
Both tables concern the same company and reporting year and repeat the row label
"service revenue". One table reports consolidated amounts in thousands; the
other reports segment amounts in millions. A continued-page value row contains
12 and 9, but the continuation identity is not in the initial snippet. Initial
retrieval includes both entity/year declarations and both unit/sample phrases.
All unary slots are therefore covered, yet either of these attachments remains
plausible:

  h_A: the selected row continues the consolidated/thousands table;
  h_B: the selected row continues the segment/millions table.

The question asks which scoped reported value applies, not for arithmetic. The
source has a short continuation/caption witness identifying the correct table.
Another candidate repeats a value-bearing row without resolving continuation.
Only one additional bundle fits. The binding witness rules out an incompatible
answer; the duplicate provides no such distinction. A method scoring only slot
presence can assign zero incremental coverage to both and prefer the duplicate
through relevance or a fixed tie-break. More fact tokens do not solve attachment.

A narrow impossibility statement is possible: a selector whose complete input
representation is invariant to table-link permutations cannot always distinguish
two documents with the same values, metadata tokens, slot-coverage vectors and
costs but opposite continuation attachments and correct scoped answers. It must
make the same selection/action for indistinguishable represented inputs, and at
least one action is wrong or unresolved. This only excludes that deliberately
restricted representation. It does not prove that BM25 with a reader, a parent-
table retriever, an ordinary sufficiency judge, HiREC, or S2G-RAG cannot solve the
example. A source-faithful attachment closure could retrieve the caption and
solve it immediately. That is a required baseline, not an inconvenience to hide.

The example is a unit challenge for representation/objective behavior, not a
natural-data result or evidence of prevalence. Do not optimize the method on it
and advertise success as empirical innovation. A nontrivial natural study needs
cases where globally attaching every plausible parent would exceed the same
budget, whereas a small verified witness resolves a relevant ambiguity without
displacing other necessary answer evidence. Whether such cases occur often
enough to justify a paper is currently unknown.

4. A candidate mechanism, with the difficult parts exposed

A possible implementation, if later warranted, would have four stages:

  A. Produce the same candidate pool for all methods using lexical/hybrid
     retrieval and source-structural neighbors. Keep all candidate costs and
     extraction uncertainties. Pin the parser and retrieval settings before
     questions from test histories are visible.
  B. Propose a bounded set of plausible competing bindings at explicitly
     ambiguous edges. Reuse source-structure indices instead of making a
     combinatorial Cartesian product of every metadata field. Preserve an
     unknown flag when pruning may have removed a material alternative.
  C. Score potential bundles for separating incompatible actions using a
     source-conditioned verifier. An explicit continuation ID, a row/column
     relation, a unit exception footnote or an inclusion-rule sentence can
     be a witness. The verifier must name the exact relation and anchor;
     fluent self-generated explanations are not evidence.
  D. Select within the declared cost budget and answer from supported bindings,
     with explicit partial abstention if the requested relation remains
     unresolved. The same reader, abstention instruction and output budget
     must also be available to comparators.

The hypothesis is not that counterfactual prompts alone improve the model.
Counterfactual alternatives direct which source witness to seek; they do not
alter the source, manufacture a correction, infer a new numerical value, or
justify withholding all difficult questions. A generic probe such as "what
would distinguish these two plausible table bindings?" is an engineering
ingredient until its selection objective and source verification show a
controlled benefit.

Joint witnesses matter. A table identity plus an applicability footnote may
separate a pair although neither alone does. Such complementarities invalidate
a blanket diminishing-returns assumption. After truly complete bundles are
fixed and each pair is independently separated by any one selected bundle, the
objective becomes weighted pair coverage with familiar combinatorial structure.
Without those conditions, do not claim a greedy approximation guarantee. Bundle
construction and overlap-aware token packing are substantial parts of the task,
not cosmetic implementation. Small exhaustive toy optima can diagnose heuristic
loss; they would not constitute a new solver theorem.

5. Necessary baseline ladder and ablations

The main comparison must hold candidate documents, available structure, reader,
question wording, final token budget and verifier-compute budget fixed. At
minimum include:

- A competent reader with structure-preserving parent-table/section closure and
  ordinary relevance ranking. Verified attachment is available symmetrically.
- Version/scope filtering and the same partial-abstention policy, without
  hypothesis competition. No gold scope labels are inference inputs.
- A coverage packer over explicit relation slots, not merely a strawman bag of
  entity/year/unit words. Include a diversity or submodular coverage variant.
- A sufficiency-driven complementary retrieval policy using the same candidate
  pool and verifier budget, adapted honestly from the closest reproducible
  HiREC, S2G-RAG and RINSE mechanisms after their exact contracts are verified.
- An interpretation-enumeration, evidence-reranking and scope-pruning comparator
  informed by ToC and REFUEL, using the same automatically generated initial
  hypotheses as the proposed selector. Ambiguity detection, answer partitioning
  and hypothesis pruning are already prior mechanisms.
- A random or relevance-matched witness-selection control with the same source
  bundles, and a strong reranker. If extra verifier compute explains the gain,
  give the reranker or sufficiency baseline that compute too.

Ablate one component at a time: competing hypotheses versus a single-best
binding; action-weighted separation versus relation coverage; verified source
contradiction versus model self-consistency; adaptive witness selection versus
static structural closure; uniform versus calibrated proposal weights; unknown
alternative accounting versus forced finite-beam certainty. Keep all available
facts, headers, page anchors and budget accounting unchanged. Removing the
competing hypothesis while also removing source structure is not an isolation
of the objective.

An oracle hypothesis set and an oracle separator map can be diagnostic ceilings
only. They are excluded from primary retrieval performance. Compare actual H
with reviewed plausible alternatives to measure missing-hypothesis error, and
compare automated separator judgments with source-only review to measure false
elimination. Evaluate extraction/binding errors separately so that an improved
parser is not mislabeled improved witness selection.

6. Falsifiable study and decision rule

Primary hypothesis: on independently held-out histories with genuine source-
binding ambiguity, choosing evidence to eliminate incompatible scoped answers
improves fully grounded, scope-correct answers at a matched input and compute
budget beyond structure-aware closure and sufficiency/complementary retrieval.
A distinct calibration hypothesis is that it reduces false supported comparison
claims at matched answer coverage. Neither can be established by more abstention,
more context, weaker baselines, gold metadata or selective reporting.

Build the structural ambiguity audit before QA outputs. Sample cases by source
properties, including cross-page continuations, repeated measures, unit
exceptions, entity changes, competing intervals and explicit inclusion changes.
Also include ordinary unambiguous cases, irreducibly unresolved cases, apparent
changes caused only by revised scope, and same-scope comparisons. Avoid a set
consisting solely of examples designed around the proposed selector. Review
complete source evidence and disagreement labels before predictions, disclose
model assistance, and seek genuinely independent human checking where feasible.
Split by source history and document family/template, not random question, so
that a learned template or prior familiarity cannot cross the test boundary.

Measure the following separately: factual answer completeness; scope correctness;
claim-level citation support; false binding elimination; correct-hypothesis
retention; coverage of material alternative hypotheses; abstention/answer
coverage; final evidence tokens; total verifier/model calls and cost; and
retrieval versus reader failure. Report full-input-budget and compute curves,
not a cherry-picked operating point. A stable numeric answer without supported
scope does not pass. Pair comparisons within questions and uncertainty estimates
clustered at the held-out history level are appropriate; multiple questions
from one report are not independent histories. Exact data counts, risk targets,
stopping thresholds and analysis choices still require a prospective protocol.

Explicit falsifiers / stop conditions:

1. Generic attachment closure or a competent sufficient-context reader removes
   the residual ambiguity at the same budget. The result is a baseline repair.
2. Relation-aware coverage or equally funded sufficiency/complementary retrieval
   matches the selector. The proposed objective adds no demonstrated benefit.
3. Gains vanish with matched context and verifier cost, matched answer coverage,
   correct structural inputs, or a stronger reader.
4. False contradiction judgments remove the correct binding, or hypothesis
   pruning hides the true interpretation often enough to negate answer gains.
5. The advantage requires oracle hypotheses, curated gold witness links, a
   convenient source template, or the familiar development histories.
6. Natural source audit cannot establish enough real, resolvable attachment
   ambiguities with compact distinguishing witnesses to support a held-out study.
7. The closest prior method already performs the same hypothesis-separating
   objective and budgeted selection. Recast the work as reproduction or a
   carefully justified extension; do not preserve novelty through renaming.

There is no promised positive result. A useful early outcome may be showing
that structural closure dominates the more expensive hypothesis mechanism.
That would narrow the project intelligently rather than justify further solver
complexity.

7. Scaling and uncertainty

With K candidate bindings there can be K(K-1)/2 relevant pairs. With M candidate
bundles a naive verifier can require O(M*K^2) pair checks before packing, often
far more expensive than ordinary retrieval. Testing each bundle against each
hypothesis can reduce repeated comparisons to O(M*K) only when judgments compose
as assumed. Query-action equivalence classes, source-structure factorization and
shared witnesses may reduce work, but pruning can miss the decisive alternative.
Worst-case assignment space across entity, unit, period and sample still grows
multiplicatively. Latency, preprocessing amortization and hypothesis coverage
must be reported; none is free because it is called indexing.

False attachments can be highly confident; independence among verifier errors
is not assumed. Correlated models generating hypotheses, assessing witnesses
and grading answers can make the whole loop self-confirming. Use held-out
source-only attachment audits, independent grading and explicit disagreement
records. This document does not establish that confidence weights, elimination
judgments or a stopping certificate are calibrated. A finite surviving set
cannot prove universal uniqueness under an open corpus.

Current recommendation

Do not start a solver yet. First audit the closest prior objectives and whether
natural, source-verifiable ambiguities survive generic structural closure under
realistic budgets. If both gates pass, freeze a small falsification study with
relation-aware coverage and sufficiency baselines. The publishable possibility
would be evidence for an economical, reliably grounded selection rule over
competing bindings; the present state is only that hypothesis.

Literature boundary

This reasoning uses the established v0.13 primary-source notes in
 docs/related_work_v13.txt and docs/related_work_scope_followup_v13.txt,
and the source-bound baseline requirements in docs/next_retrieval_gate_v13.txt.
The newly identified HiREC, S2G-RAG and RINSE overlaps were supplied by the
coordinating research task and are being checked by the related-work reviewer;
the descriptions above are provisional mechanism comparisons, not a claim to
have independently verified or executed those methods. No additional web
queries, experiments, solver implementations or model predictions were made
for this hypothesis document.

The related-work reviewer additionally inspected ToC (EMNLP 2023, Section 3.2)
and REFUEL (ACL 2021, Section 2). The former enumerates disambiguated question/
answer nodes, reranks retrieved evidence, prunes by original-question scope and
passage content, and covers valid interpretations. The latter expands answer/
disambiguated-question candidates through round-trip prediction over a shared
retrieved pool. These mechanisms substantially narrow the potential distinction.
The reviewer did not identify explicit numeric source-attachment witness
acquisition in those inspected sections; that bounded observation does not
establish novelty or absence elsewhere. The operative test is whether witness
selection adds value when initial hypotheses, source structures, reader and
compute are matched, not whether interpretation enumeration can be renamed.
