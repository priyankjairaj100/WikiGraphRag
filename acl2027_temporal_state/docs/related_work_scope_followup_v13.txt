Scope and table-evidence literature follow-up v0.13
Checked 3 October 2026; four additional queries, separate from the first audit

Decision

The novelty boundary is tighter than the first bounded check established.
Version/scope filtering, typed lineage, evidence-sufficiency gating and
abstention already appear together in a September 2026 primary manuscript.
Table structure preservation and parsing-versus-reasoning diagnosis also have
substantial prior art. A successful whitespace restoration or header addition
would be an upstream repair, not evidence for a new reasoning method.

The following four source notes are intentionally brief. The first three use
primary manuscripts; TIDE uses verified author-project documentation because
its arXiv page remains inaccessible. Earlier literature files are unchanged.

1. Wang et al., arXiv v1, 16 September 2026.
   https://arxiv.org/html/2609.18769v1
   The AI Approach section describes reviewed scope metadata, article-based
   chunks, typed lineage, applicability predicates and external evidence
   checks; insufficient evidence can trigger abstention. The Evaluation
   Methodology explicitly says the compared systems differ in model, index
   and pipeline. Supported citation claim: these mechanisms predate our
   proposed scope-aware route. Unsupported claim: its result isolates the
   causal benefit of scope filtering. Our inference: generic scope constraints
   plus abstention cannot be our novelty; a matched component study would be
   needed even if this design were adapted.

2. Yu et al., TableRAG, EMNLP, November 2025.
   https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.710.pdf
   Sections 3.1-3.2 preserve tabular data through a parallel table-schema
   database and textual corpus, combining retrieval with SQL execution and
   query decomposition. Supported citation claim: structural table handling
   already goes beyond flattening everything into passages. Unsupported
   claim: this paper proves any particular treatment of our financial unit
   headings is sufficient. Our inference: compare structure-preserving input
   and competent table reasoning before attributing our errors to revision
   reasoning. Restoring native spaces alone is not a new TableRAG method.

3. Strich et al., T2-RAGBench, EACL, March 2026.
   https://aclanthology.org/2026.eacl-long.8.pdf
   Sections 4-5 study context-independent financial questions, ordinary
   retrieval and oracle context; section 5.6 distinguishes parsing,
   calculation and other errors. Appendix B/C adds entity/report metadata
   during question preparation. Supported citation claim: corpus retrieval
   and error decomposition for financial text-plus-tables are established.
   Its number metric uses absolute values and tolerates scale shifts.
   Our inference: importing that metric unchanged would obscure sign/unit
   errors central to our diagnostic. Citation presence also cannot substitute
   for checking that received evidence contains the relevant headers.

4. TIDE author-project README, inspected 3 October 2026.
   https://github.com/icsetepa44/TIDE
   The task and evaluation sections document governing-version resolution,
   context-misalignment questions, and parametric, retrieved and gold-context
   access. Date correctness gates scoring. Supported citation claim: this
   project already explicitly tests rejection of an inapplicable version.
   Verification is limited to the repository documentation, not an execution
   or a full manuscript review. The arXiv abstract URL again returned an
   internal tool error. Our inference: do not claim that rejecting an
   inapplicable version is a new task without resolving this comparison.

What remains a possible research hypothesis

After supplying complete, correctly associated headers, units and scope
evidence, does a competent matched reader still assert unsupported
comparability between two reported quantities? A comparison may involve an
actual correction to the same estimand, a changed population or definition,
or insufficient evidence to choose between them. The empirical target would
be false comparison claims while retaining correct supported comparisons.

This is a candidate evaluation question, not a verified literature gap or a
new algorithm. Scope predicates alone are already covered by the first work.
A useful study would have to isolate pairwise comparability from ordinary
document applicability and numerical extraction. It would need complete
evidence with reviewed unknown-scope cases, a strong reader, structure-aware
retrieval, version filtering and matched evidence budgets. Any learned or
rule-based compatibility module must then improve this residual task beyond
those controls without receiving reference scope labels at inference.

Reject or redirect the hypothesis if:

- Layout/header repair or an adequate reader removes the failures. Report
  the engineering repair and diagnostic, without a new reasoning claim.
- The apparent gain comes from more words, added oracle metadata, easier
  questions, selective abstention or favorable formatting rather than the
  proposed comparison operation.
- Ordinary metadata filtering and structure-preserving retrieval match it,
  or the remaining errors concern values/units rather than comparability.
- Independently reviewed histories cannot support the required distinction,
  or independent held-out evaluation does not reproduce the residual error.

This lookup changed no experimental corpus, questions, references, prompts
or outputs. It does not reopen the stopped dependency-propagation claim.
No reviewed method or benchmark was executed. Four queries cannot certify
exhaustiveness or establish absence of closely related work.
