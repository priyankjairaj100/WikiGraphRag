Objective implementation
The decoder accepts one question-independent objective callback after the shared
feasibility projection. Independent, iterative, restarted and exact search use
the same selected objective, candidates, scores, correction policy and budgets.
The independent baseline keeps its prior per-mention maximum net-unary reading
choice and then optimizes links; it is not a global active-support optimizer.

The historical objective scores every selected reading and link as before.
The active_support ablation replaces each inactive resolved reading by that
mention's maximum net unresolved score, and each inactive temporal link by the
outgoing mention's null score. Operative correction notices retain their link
contributions, including notices whose replacement assertion is later retired.
The breakdown retains selected, reference and scored values for every term.
Objective identity hashes bind both the frozen correction cache and the exact
scoring convention. Old correction-pipeline callers keep historical behavior.

Reference anchoring preserves rankings under a constant added to every reading
at a mention, and under a constant added to every outgoing net-link score and
its null reference. Naively zeroing inactive terms lacks these invariances.
This property does not imply scale invariance, source independence, duplicate
invariance, calibration, or a joint posterior interpretation. Removed inactive
mass is signed and may be negative. A negative historical reading can be chosen
and then neutralized by correction; these failure controls remain disclosed.

Research implications and next evidence gate
The objective audit argues against selling active-support suppression as an
advance. The next useful step is to audit the shared natural candidate/score
artifact, inspect unsupported and missing candidates, measure genuine candidate
alternatives and recall, and separate extraction errors from score errors and
search errors. Test ordinal spacing and relative unary/link scales without
tuning against desired QA outputs. Calibration, independent natural histories,
strong contextual baselines and answer uncertainty remain necessary.

No held-out natural accuracy, candidate recall, calibrated likelihood, scalable
joint solver, natural joint-over-pipeline gain or ACL acceptance claim exists.
No manuscript result should be inferred from contract counts. Preserve the
negative findings and failed attempts when deciding the paper contribution.
