Retrieval-support audit v0.13

This audit evaluates the actual sixteen retrieved contexts before the reviewer inspected any native outputs. It is model-assisted, aware of the retrieval condition, and performed by the prior reference reviewer with shared development context. It is not blind human annotation or a model-performance result. Context, corpus, question and reference hashes are bound in the JSON. All selected chunk texts and hashes were checked against the supplied contexts; neither contexts nor references were edited.

Complete answer support appears in two questions per condition: Plug's maintenance-accrual explanation and OPERA's revised main result. Overall, ordinary retrieval has two complete, four partial and two absent-support contexts; paired retrieval has two complete, five partial and one absent-support context. These categories refer to the required answer, not whether a chunk is merely topical. Partial includes a relevant fragment, one side of a comparison or an unresolved unit/scope binding. It does not establish that an error must be caused by the reader.

Plug revised loss/EPS: both conditions contain the old values with enough old-year and unit context, and later reported numbers. The later table's year header and thousands multiplier are missing. Matching old labelled rows can align the old EPS value across chunks; that does not license importing unseen later headers.

Plug retained service revenue: ordinary retrieval misses the target FY2018 amount and its unchanged row. Its retrieved no-change tables concern FY2019 or quarters. Paired retrieval adds an old service-revenue fragment ending at 22.0, but the continuation and corrected FY2018 row are absent. Service-cost adjustments are not service revenue. Neither context fully supports the answer.

Plug maintenance accrual: both conditions retrieve the specific omitted-cost explanation and its FY2018 additional-accrual consequence. No number is required. Plug scope: both retrieve the company definition. Ordinary also retrieves an FY2018 footnote anchor beside the EPS restatement; paired instead has an ambiguous flattened year sequence. Neither retrieves the later report's explicit fiscal-year cover declaration. Its document identifier provides an orientation hint, recorded separately from source-body support.

OPERA revised result: both conditions contain the main estimate, statistical uncertainty, asymmetric systematic uncertainty and compatibility conclusion in the same later chunk. The contexts are identical for this question. OPERA instrumental reasons: the identical contexts contain only generic mentions of instrumental sources, omitting both specific problems.

OPERA retained baseline: ordinary retrieval gives a different effective distance and time-baseline entries. Paired adds the old geodetic paragraph. Although the final displayed sum is beyond its chunk boundary, the visible source-specified component sum supports the old distance at the reported precision. Both versions' matching final time-baseline entries are recorded as relevant indirect retention evidence, but their exact link to the asked geodetic-distance scope is not fully present. The later geodetic paragraph remains missing, so neither context is certified complete.

OPERA selection scope: both show the original selection but omit the revised selected sample and isolated-earliest-hit exclusion. Later instrumental fixes or matching running years do not supply the missing comparison. Optional old counts can be derived from displayed internal/external totals without revealing the new selection.

These findings must remain separate from subsequent answer scoring. An unsupported confident assertion may be a reader grounding error, while omission under incomplete context is not automatically a reasoning failure. Correct prior knowledge is not proof of retrieved citation support. Preserve factual, scope, citation and format judgments separately. The audit does not predict output correctness, establish an algorithmic advantage or generalize beyond these development contexts.
