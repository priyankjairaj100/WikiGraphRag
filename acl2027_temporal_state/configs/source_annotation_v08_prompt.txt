Produce exactly one JSON object conforming to the schema. Bind request_sha256 to

Extract atomic claims relevant to the named history: organizational role changes,
public plans, their stated timing and modifications, and explicit corrections.
Retain relevant claims from every supplied source. Each claim has one subject,
one namespaced relation (e.g. leadership:ceo_holder or plan:availability), one

when text supports them. Natural non-role relations are allowed. Do not force

Use stable IDs within this single output. Empty lists are valid; do not invent
competing readings, corrections, links or dates to reach a quota.

Distinguish observed incumbency or completed actions from planned future actions,
announcement acts and retrospective reports. A future effective date in an
announcement does not prove the event actually happened then. A later role label
does not prove continuous incumbency or the exact first day. A source's quoted
old assertion may be a reported_prior_statement, or quoted_as_error when the
source explicitly calls it erroneous; never promote it to asserted_by_source.
An explicitly labelled error is not a competing current factual value. The
assertion_status denotes what this source is doing, not whether the world agrees.

Date constraints use canonical YYYY-MM-DD bounds, inclusive lower and upper
bounds on the date of the named event, not half-open validity intervals. Use equal
bounds only for a supported exact day. Month/year mentions may use their complete
calendar bounds with matching precision. Keep bounds null for unresolved relative
or unknown dates and explain the uncertainty. No publication/retrieval/delivery
date may silently become an event start. Preserve planned versus actual timing.
Do not infer open-ended persistence, mutual exclusivity, temporal consistency,
continuous episodes or future availability from silence.

Every claim, temporal constraint, correction and link needs exact evidence spans.
Offsets are zero-based Python Unicode character positions, [start,end), in the
request source text. quote must equal text[start:end] exactly, including spacing.
Use the smallest spans that support your interpretation; multiple spans may be
needed. You may use Python solely to locate and verify spans in the assigned
request. Repeated quotes require the intended occurrence, not the first by default.

Corrections require explicit correction language, a target description, a corrected
claim, and evidence. If the original incorrect statement or document was not
captured, target_claim_id is null and target_description identifies only what the
notice says; record that unavailable-original limitation. If the old wording is
quoted in this supplied source, a quoted_as_error claim may be the target, but this
does not recreate a captured historical original. authority_name attributes the
notice issuer only when supported; otherwise null. authority_basis explains the
textual attribution or uncertainty, not an externally certified right to correct.
A changed plan, succession, cancellation or disagreement is not by itself CORRECTS.

Optional links connect claims in this same output. Direction: supports goes from
the supporting claim to the supported claim; changes from the later change claim
to the prior plan/state claim; corrects from the replacement claim to its erroneous
target. A corrects link must match an explicit correction record with a non-null
target_claim_id. Do not add a link merely because two claims have similar wording.
An unavailable external target belongs only in the correction record, not a fake
claim or dangling link. All endpoint claims must already be included.

Use unknowns to record important missing start/end dates, missing original versions,
unclear authority, scope, or other uncertainty. Its evidence may be empty when the
unknown is absence of information; describe it as unestablished in this prefix,
not a proven absence in the world. representation_limits lists claim IDs and a
finite reason for any unsafe core projection; do not claim these annotations are
already executable decoder inputs. Preserve material uncertainty in notes.

Do not use other requests to repair or enrich this one. Return the JSON to the
assigned output path and report only output path plus counts. Do not generate
reference questions or inspect answers. A later schema/span validator checks
structure and artifact consistency; it does not certify your interpretations.
