SOURCE-ONLY MIGRATION TO BOUNDED TEMPORAL CLAIMS v0.3

You receive a source-prefix input, one frozen v0.2 extraction pass, and this
schema. Do not read any questions, labels, predictions, other passes or later
sources. Do not browse. This migration is a further model-assisted semantic
interpretation, not a deterministic format conversion or independent human
annotation. Preserve raw extraction outputs untouched. Do not assume agreement
with a raw field establishes its truth when source text conflicts with it.

Produce one derived JSON file with:
schema_version "0.3", pass_id, provenance_kind "source_only_model_assisted_migration",
backend {model_revision:null,decoding_settings:null,pinned:false,
kind:"conversational_model_agent"}, prefix_input_sha256, raw_extraction_sha256,
prompt_sha256, claims, aliases, change_log, limitations.

Each claim uses EXACT fields:
claim_id, raw_assertion_id, source_id, subject, relation, scope, value,
polarity (positive or negative), modality (reported_actual, announced_future,
conditional, or uncertain), reported_at (ISO day or null), start {lower,upper},
end {lower,upper}, state_observed_at (list of ISO days), evidence_spans
[{start,end,quote}], context_source_ids, derivation_note, role_qualifier,
operation (ASSERT; other raw update labels may be retained but are provenance
only; no CORRECTS unless truly explicit, in which case record unsupported).

Bounds lower and upper are inclusive possible dates of a latent endpoint;
occupancy/non-occupancy holds over [start,end), with end exclusive. Null means
unbounded/unknown, never a guessed date. Exact endpoint has equal lower/upper.
Each claim denotes one continuous interval in the supplied account. An observed
state constrains start <= observation < end; it does not date appointment.
Membership must be valid for every admissible endpoint pair before it is called
certain. Preserve genuine uncertainty over last-service-day vs first-out-day
using bounded endpoints; explain any interpretation. Year-only appointment can
be bounded by Jan 1 and Dec 31 of that year without choosing either exact day.

Polarity and modality are independent: an announced future departure is a
negative announced_future claim. Its interval is non-occupancy after cessation,
not positive service beginning then. A raw positive occupancy claim with a
future end can remain reported_actual, with that endpoint explicitly described
as scheduled/announced in derivation_note. This prototype cannot encode separate
modality for each endpoint; log this representational limitation where relevant.

reported_at is when the statement was made, distinct from archive availability.
state_observed_at is when the source asserts the role actually holds (or does
not hold for a negative claim). Do not put report dates into state_observed_at
for a future appointment or prospective interim promise. Source-present-tense
roles may use the document's dated observation if directly justified. If a source
only describes appointment approved with future effectiveness, leave observations
empty. Later certifications establish only their dated observations, not exact
appointments or uninterrupted occupancy back to earlier reports.

Use same-source references to resolve defined Effective Date where justified.
If a source explicitly says a role is retained during another stated interval,
an endpoint bound may express that guarantee without inventing the eventual end.
Mark the announced nature of any projected guarantee in derivation_note.
Do not add implicit indefinite persistence. Unknown endpoints remain unknown.
If evidence requires another eligible source, list it in context_source_ids.

Keep the raw subject/relation/scope/value labels when supported. Subject naming
differences may be represented by aliases below. Every raw assertion ID must
have one or more derived claims OR a documented exclusion in change_log with
source rationale. If changing polarity or interpreting vague boundaries, record
that explicitly; do not characterize the migration as unchanged extraction.

Aliases: source-supported entity names only, no answer strings or gold IDs.
Each alias has EXACT fields subject, alias, source_id, evidence_spans (same
offset schema). subject must equal a claim's subject. Evidence must support that
the alias names that subject within this history. Include the canonical name
where supported, and short corporate names that actually occur in the source.
An identical surface alias may refer to multiple subjects; never silently merge
them. No full-corpus alias discovery or future source context is permitted.

Evidence offsets are zero-based Python Unicode codepoints, end exclusive, into
the exact source text field. Verify every span. Fields must have evidence for
substantive inferences, including year bounds, report time, observation and
endpoint references. Use short exact spans that preserve required context.
Give full source-local reasoning in derivation_note. No questions or confidence
probabilities, and no claims of human adjudication or pinned-model reproducibility.
