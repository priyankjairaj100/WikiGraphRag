\section{Additional controlled document streams}
\label{sec:sources}
Four histories selected from the existing development frame cover Disney,
Intel, CVS and Artemis. Eight fixed URLs yielded usable current captures and
eight cumulative document prefixes. Six extracted-text hashes match the earlier
checkpoint and two NASA text hashes differ; all eight are recorded as new
captures. The Intel PDF's archive metadata and its article date differ, and the
NASA pages contain current navigation. Document delivery therefore remains an
author-defined experiment, not certified historical public availability.
\claim{V09-C01}

Eight isolated source-only annotation roles produced 123 claim instances.
Separate reference roles saved seven artifacts containing fourteen judgments.
A content filter blocked the second Disney reference artifact without a
specific policy reason; no saved reference or substitute was produced. All
fifteen saved raw outputs passed schema, exact-span and prefix-binding checks.
Separate source-only model reviewers selected one annotation revision: an
unsupported exact Executive Chairman start day was removed while the stated
month-level retrospective evidence was retained. The other fourteen artifacts
were unchanged. Model-assisted review is not independent human gold.
\claim{V09-C02}

The selected records contain no explicit factual correction and four change
links. Together with the prior three controlled streams, seven histories have
fourteen annotated prefixes and 180 claim instances. Thirteen prefixes have
reference artifacts, yielding twenty-six judgments; only six histories have
both reference prefixes complete. Repeated-prefix counts are not independent
samples. These records have not yet been projected into executable candidates
or evaluated through the QA decoders.\claim{V09-C03}

\section{Matched source-grounded scoring diagnostic}
\label{sec:design}
The first two ready histories in the frozen selection order, Disney and Intel,
were fixed for a bounded scorer comparison. Before research scoring, a separate
source-only model role authored one supported statement and one semantic
near-miss for each first prefix. The Disney contrast changes executive to
non-executive chairman; the Intel contrast replaces the stated effective date
with the announcement date. Separate source review accepted all four statements
and expected labels before any research distributions. The scorer received
neutral item identifiers, statements and complete source text without labels
or rationales. This balanced authored challenge set is not a sample of natural
candidate errors. All four items are reading statements; the 4B model has not
been evaluated on the earlier link, unresolved-reading or no-link options.\claim{V09-C04}

Official pinned Qwen3-0.6B and Qwen3-4B Q8\_0 assets used the same pinned
llama.cpp runtime and source-bearing prompt wording. Each model evaluated four
statements under two label-display orders, for sixteen retained distributions.
Both model tokenizers were checked before scoring; complete rendered prompts
fit the 4,096-token context without truncation. Every score used a fresh process,
four CPU threads, thinking disabled, and a 5.5 GiB address-space guard. The score
is the unmodified next-token log-probability difference
$\log P(\mathrm{Yes})-\log P(\mathrm{No})$; its sign selects the label and exactly
zero abstains. Native templates are recorded for both models. This is a matched
model comparison, not a causal identification of parameter-count effects.
\claim{V09-C05}

\section{Implication for the research route}
\label{sec:implication}
The source extension supplies succession and planning controls but no new
explicit correction case. It cannot support a correction-specific method claim.
The next method experiment should prioritize explicit correction histories
with supported downstream dependencies, compare against a pinned full-prefix
reader and contextual independent scorer under equal evidence access, and
separate proposal, entailment, correction-targeting, dependency-withdrawal and
search errors. The earlier active-support objective failure remains relevant:
optimizing a misspecified objective does not recover the desired state.
No new scalable search algorithm is justified by the present diagnostic alone.
\claim{V09-C08}

\section{Reproducibility and limits}
\label{sec:limits}
The restored v0.8 archive passed CRC and all 433 manifest-entry checks. Its
packaged evidence was recovered; excluded source fulltexts and model binaries
were not. The v0.9 package retains compact semantic projections, exact capture
and request bindings, model/runtime identities, prompt/token hashes and counts,
projected raw score responses, execution logs, failed attempts and review
receipts. Full external source strings and recoverable input-token arrays remain
outside the package. Default replay verifies packaged metadata and score
arithmetic, not exact source occurrence, tokenization or a new model execution.
The separate external audit records what was checked while matching inputs were
available. Fresh public downloads may differ.\claim{V09-C09}

All findings are development observations. There is no human-gold benchmark,
naturally sampled accuracy estimate, independent calibration study, held-out
result, candidate-recall result, or end-to-end comparison on the new histories.
The four statements share two histories, and annotation roles can share training.
No confidence interval or significance test is reported for these authored
contrasts. A completed submission manuscript and verified layout remain future
work.\claim{V09-C10}
