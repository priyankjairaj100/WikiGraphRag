\subsection{Observed results}
\label{sec:results}
The 0.6B model changed the sign of all four statement scores when the label
order changed: every Yes-first decision was Yes and every No-first decision
was No. Each order agreed with two of the four authored reference labels.
The 4B model changed no decision, but agreed with only three references in
both orders. It accepted the unsupported non-executive chairman statement,
with log odds above $21$ in both conditions. Stability under this one prompt
change therefore did not imply correct source interpretation. No condition
was selected, averaged or calibrated after inspecting the results.
\claim{V09-C06}

The final matched run took 1,240.80 seconds (20.68 minutes). Median native
prefill time was 30.42 seconds for 0.6B and 114.71 seconds for 4B. All sixteen
scores had zero reused prompt tokens. Their literal label masses were
0.999179--0.999899 for 0.6B and approximately one for 4B, with numerical
overshoot of at most $7.3\times10^{-10}$ for the latter. These masses and score
margins are not calibrated correctness probabilities.\claim{V09-C07}

Execution also retained an initial pre-load watchdog stop with no distribution,
one successful authored smoke distribution, and one discarded research
response. The first watchdog treated reclaimable download cache as resident
model pressure; a separately frozen amendment used a documented resident
working-set proxy while preserving the address-space guard. The first research
response then exceeded a $10^{-6}$ returned-mass accounting tolerance by about
$1.82\times10^{-7}$. After independent numerical review, a disclosed $10^{-4}$
tolerance was fixed for both mass checks and the complete sixteen-call design
was restarted. Log probabilities, prompts and the decision threshold were not
altered. This engineering tolerance is not a proved numerical error bound or
an entirely pre-output protocol. Eighteen returned distributions are accounted
for: sixteen retained, one discarded, and one separate smoke. The exact-artifact
audit additionally checked forty-eight vocabulary-only prefix/label
continuations with zero new forward-pass completions.\claim{V09-C07}
