\section{Bounded access pilot}\label{sec:v12access}
We froze eight primary-source URLs across three previously discovered histories,
one attempt per URL, no new searches, a thirty-second transfer limit and an
eight-MiB response limit. A host-network transport amendment was recorded before
execution. Six responses returned HTTP 200 and yielded text: two Plug Power
annual reports, two OPERA paper versions and two separately identified abstract
pages. The two JAMA URLs returned HTTP 403 challenge pages. All eight outcomes
are retained. Successful byte transport is distinct from access to the intended
source; the abstracts do not substitute for the papers. No native reader calls
were made in this checkpoint.

\section{Source-only adjudication}\label{sec:v12review}
Separate model-assisted reviewers examined the recovered source pairs, including
relevant rendered PDF pages. They had prior discovery familiarity and common
project context; this is neither blinded review nor independent human gold.
Admission separates completeness, document identity, actual version order,
asserting authority, correction scope, direct dependent corrections, essential
dependencies and independent retention. First-public availability is a separate
criterion from controlled document-version order.

Plug Power supports one controlled-version direct-correction development
control. The restatement explicitly revises both the common-shareholder loss
and basic loss per share. The unchanged share count and service revenue are
freshly reaffirmed in the later table; they do not imply general reliability of
the earlier statements. Every explicit dependent correction remains available
to the direct baseline. This case supplies no residual propagation benefit and
is not a clock-certified historical replay or a benchmark admission.

OPERA's complete versions expose both instrumental corrections and changed
event selection. The selected-event count changes from 16,111 to 15,223, and the
later version explicitly revises the timing result, relative velocity and
conclusion. It therefore does not satisfy the unchanged same-scope correction
core. Retained geodetic information does not remove the analysis-scope change.
Version stamps establish an observed version relation. Physical and printed
page counts are distinguished. Later PDF-generation
metadata prevents certifying current bytes as the exact first-public
representation. Two v1 tables are absent from plain-text extraction and were
read visually; future retrieval requires verified table recovery.

JAMA remains unavailable as a complete-document case in this pilot. Its prior
selected-passage diagnostic cannot be promoted to a complete-source evaluation.
The pilot produces zero certified nondirect propagation cases, zero newly
admitted benchmark histories and zero matched natural QA comparisons. Neither
source selection nor access failure supports a prevalence estimate.

\section{Research decision}\label{sec:v12decision}
The prespecified stopping rule is triggered. No certified nondirect propagation
case was admitted: the selected dependent results are explicitly revised, and
OPERA also changes analysis scope. We stop expanding that contribution on the
present cases. This is an evidence-bound
research decision, not proof that nondirect consequences do not exist.

The next proposed feasibility study concerns paired-version, scope-aware
reading. Two complete source pairs can support questions about revised facts,
retained facts, revision reasons and changed analysis conditions. They do not
yet establish residual inconsistency in a complete document. A future comparison
will use ordinary versus paired-version retrieval with the same reader and
bounded evidence budget. Full-document access must not be confused with giving
every page to the model. References, controls and scoring must be frozen before
predictions; factual content, scope, citations and format will be assessed
separately. Oracle assertion-ID lookups are not a natural reading baseline.
This proposal is not an executed result or a claim of algorithmic novelty.

\section{Reproducibility and limitations}\label{sec:v12repro}
All 416 unit tests pass, including six offline access-collector tests. Packaged
provenance records the exact attempts, source hashes, bounded paraphrases and
review locators. Full third-party captures and page renderings remain external;
portable verification cannot reconstruct them or certify semantic annotations.
The previous correction implementation and historical results are preserved.
This source-only addendum has no newly compiled or visually verified PDF.
Two development source pairs, one direct control and no demonstrated natural
answer-level gain are insufficient for a general method or submission-readiness
claim.
