\section{Support answering contract}\label{sec:v11support}
A new assertion-ID adapter separates support status from temporal answering. It returns active support, withdrawn support or unresolved, conditional on explicitly supplied candidate selections and correction policy. It re-materializes the unchanged core and rejects inconsistent memories. Typed evidence preserves assertion records, correction references, essential dependencies and source-authority metadata; historical evidence is not uniformly active support. The adapter preserves unknown event bounds and neither adjudicates truth nor computes a replacement number. It supplies no natural-language routing, alternative-support disjunction, new extraction method or scalable solver guarantee.

On the two prior financial annotations, thirty lookups across three policies return twenty-two active and eight withdrawn statuses. Direct and dependency policies agree on all ten corresponding assertion statuses because both dependent conclusions are explicitly corrected. Both direct and dependency withdrawal reasons remain in the ledger. An independent integration audit verifies the selections and evidence projections. These are annotation-conditioned interface checks, not natural QA accuracy and not a baseline comparable to the reader below.

\section{Selected current-document diagnostic}\label{sec:v11reader}
A separately reviewed current JAMA article retains a prescribing-effect ranking sentence inconsistent with its displayed corrected point estimates. The notice assigns the payment and recipient coefficients differently, while retaining the study's broad conclusions. Only source-reported numerical ordering is assessed: no causal ranking, significance-of-difference judgment, or scientific re-estimation is implied. Methods terminology remains visible in the supplied evidence.

The exact historical original was not recovered. An official publisher notice is not silently treated as a statement by the article's named authors. The unchanged core also does not recompute numerical rankings. Accordingly, this example is excluded from correction-core replay and used only as a current-document consistency diagnostic.

Four fixed web-reader passages supply methods, the two outcome-specific results and the correction notice. Three questions ask for the largest displayed prescribing estimate, prose-versus-number agreement, and the separate mortality ordering. A source-only model-assisted reference was frozen before native prediction; it is not human gold. The same passages and questions appear in both conditions, with the notice first or last. Selection followed discovery of the inconsistency, so this is a development diagnostic, not a held-out test or representative benchmark.

\section{Reproducibility and research decision}\label{sec:v11decision}
The first attempt stopped before a model process launched because the sandbox hid required memory counters. A separately frozen engineering amendment records host execution with the same inputs, model, generation settings and resource limits; only the collector's output paths changed. The unsuccessful attempt is retained. This is a disclosed pre-inference relaxation of the original no-retry policy, not an unmodified initial run. No completion was repaired or rerun after its output became available.

The paper's method hypothesis remains conditional: does source-grounded correction attribution and selective support retention improve answers beyond a competent direct updater and a strong reader with matched complete evidence? This checkpoint does not establish algorithmic novelty or a natural answer-level advantage. The next bounded access pilot targets previously identified primary representations. Complete versioned evidence, a genuinely nondirect consequence, a direct-correction control and an independently supported retained conclusion are prerequisites for a matched evaluation. If the pilot cannot recover qualifying histories, the longitudinal claim should stop expanding; any full-document inconsistency study requires its own complete inputs and frozen design.

Full source strings, exact prompt/token arrays and model binaries are omitted from distribution. Hashes identify retained representations but cannot reconstruct omitted bytes, certify source authenticity or establish historical availability. The manuscript remains source only, without a compiled PDF or submission-ready claim.
