\begin{abstract}
Evidence can contain the right numbers and metadata while failing to establish
which scope belongs to which result. We formalize a certificate for a complete
joint answer: every required claim has positive source support, every modeled
answer-changing rival is refuted, no active judgment conflicts, and an external
coverage assessment accounts for admissible alternatives outside the proposal
set. We prove source-relative soundness and conditional completeness, separate
joint error from selective risk, and show precisely where missing candidates
and fallible verification invalidate assurance. A finite optimization model
represents conjunctive witnesses, shared source spans and adverse judgments.
We give exact formulations, a classical set-cover reduction, a counterexample
to fixed-lookahead guarantees, and a restricted cover approximation with
explicit assumptions. A reference checker and exhaustive solver accompany the
theory. These are conditional results and authored implementation checks;
no natural-data improvement or general semantic-verification guarantee is
established. The intended contribution is a testable certificate-based method
for scoped retrieval, whose empirical advantage remains to be measured.
\end{abstract}

\section{Problem and contribution boundary}
\label{sec:position}
A source may report one quantity for a segment in thousands and another for a
consolidated population in millions. A continued table row, a unit header and a
population phrase can all be present without the relations that connect them.
For a question comparing two document versions, both requested versions belong
to the same joint answer. The retrieval task is to obtain the evidence needed
to warrant that joint answer and distinguish relevant alternative bindings.
The source corpus, interpretation rules and output contract must be fixed;
``the newest document wins'' is not a general resolution rule.

The starting point is established work. Distinguishing decision-equivalent
hypotheses is the purpose of equivalence-class determination. Sufficient-context,
gap-judging and complementary-evidence methods already address important parts
of evidence adequacy. Conjunctive sufficient evidence and set-level retrieval
also appear in recent work. Therefore neither hypothesis partitioning nor the
statement that evidence must be jointly sufficient is claimed as new here.
Section~\ref{sec:related} specifies the overlap and the remaining empirical test.

This manuscript closes the mathematical contract for the proposed route. Its
contributions are a precise integration of source support, rival exclusion,
candidate coverage and complete joint scope; proofs of the resulting
conditional guarantees; and an optimization and implementation contract that
does not confuse known-map packing with online acquisition. The elementary
reductions and cover bounds are attributed adaptations of standard machinery.
The source-semantic assumptions are exposed rather than supplied by the
finite checker. No claim of a fundamentally new optimization family or an
ACL-ready empirical paper is made.

\section{An operational acquisition policy}
\label{sec:online}
The exact certificate problem is directly useful after the signed witness map
is available. It is not, by itself, an online retriever: obtaining that map may
already require reading and verifying all candidate evidence. We specify the
following implementable research policy, called \emph{certificate-directed
acquisition}, to make the intended empirical method concrete. Its planning
predictions have no authority to certify an answer.

\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item \textbf{Declare the task.} Parse all required slots, versions and relation
types; retain alternative material query readings. Build typed complete joint
proposals from observed source occurrences and permitted structural
descriptors. Initialize the coverage sentinel to open. A changed question,
candidate pruning, or an expanded source domain requires reassessing the
coverage assertion rather than reusing it silently.
\item \textbf{Expose candidate actions.} Offer whole source neighborhoods such
as a row with its possible caption, a continuation identity, or a definition
footnote. Record their source identifiers, preview access, estimated cost and
actual acquisition cost. Reading a preview is information access and is
charged. Reference annotations are inaccessible to this stage.
\item \textbf{Construct prospective obligations.} For each possible target,
list missing positive claims and each rival requiring refutation. A planner
may predict which complete witness premises could discharge them. Use a
bounded exact solution of this \emph{predicted} map, or a declared heuristic,
to propose a complete acquisition plan. Prefer plans for which all required
dependencies can fit; this allows an initially zero-gain action that completes
a later conjunction. Predictions and their uncertainty remain separate from
the observed judgment ledger.
While the sentinel is open, this is diagnostic optimization of the structural
support and exclusion obligations only; a hypothetical plan does not clear
the sentinel or authorize an answer.
\item \textbf{Acquire, then verify.} Acquire a whole action from the chosen
plan and charge it immediately. Validate received anchors. Only after all
required premises are present may a source-conditioned verifier add support
or contradiction to the observed ledger. An unresolved judgment adds neither.
Retain adverse results and conflicts; replan when a predicted witness fails.
If no predicted plan is available, apply a frozen exploration rule or stop.
This choice, tie breaking, shortlist size and compute limit are fixed on
development data, not selected after held-out outcomes.
\item \textbf{Check and render.} Apply the certificate gate to observed
judgments and the current coverage assessment, with an exact final rendered
cost check. If it passes, render the complete typed answer and its source
derivations. Otherwise return insufficient evidence, a request to clarify
the unresolved query distinction, or an explicitly scored partial answer.
Stop at the declared acquisition or compute limit.
\end{enumerate}

There are two ledgers. The acquisition ledger retains every source read,
proposal and verifier call, including failed plans and evidence later omitted
from the reader input. The bounded reader pack carries every premise used by
the returned certificate. Shared spans are charged once within a union but
are never free. Source acquisition, final prompt tokens and latency are
reported in their own units; a scalar objective requires an explicit conversion.
Known active contradictions cannot be hidden by dropping their spans from a
later pack. A conservative implementation checks the full observed ledger for
conflicts in addition to the selected pack. The v21 signed ledger therefore
abstains on an observed conflict: adding another judgment cannot erase two
already active signs. Adjudication or rule supersession would need a separately
versioned, justified protocol that preserves the original judgments, outside
the present guarantee.

\begin{proposition}[Policy-independent conditional acceptance]
If every emitted answer satisfies the sound certificate contract, adaptive
planning, predicted-witness ranking and stopping do not change deterministic
source-relative soundness. They may change answer coverage and cost.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Apply the source-relative soundness theorem to the final observed certificate
on each emitted run. Its premises concern the final evidence, actual judgments,
coverage assertion and renderer, not the path that proposed an action. A
prediction affects the path only. It cannot replace a premise of that theorem.
For a stochastic learned verifier, satisfying its error assumptions after
adaptive selection remains necessary; the selective-risk analysis already
accounts for that distinction.
\end{proof}

This policy is a specified research method, not a completed automatic pipeline
in the current repository. There is no cost-competitive online theorem for
it. In particular, a surrogate plan may be wrong, a source may not contain a
needed witness, and a finite proposal beam may miss a valid answer class.
The exact reference solver diagnoses finite packing; it cannot remove these
semantic and discovery errors.

\section{Empirical contract and falsifiable claims}
\label{sec:empirical}
The local study must test the semantic premises and practical value separately.
Use immutable source snapshots and source-family-disjoint development and test
splits. Create references before method outputs with independent source review
and adjudication. Familiar histories and authored mathematical controls remain
development material. Multiple variants of one history are dependent samples.

The primary endpoint should be the rate of complete, jointly correct and
source-supported answers under a prespecified final-input budget and charged
controller budget. Also report answer coverage, conditional error among emitted
answers, wrong emitted answers per all questions, clarification and abstention,
and every timeout or schema failure. For a question with genuinely multiple
admissible readings, the reference must permit clarification or a complete
scoped multi-answer contract; an arbitrary single reference cannot establish
unique resolution.

Measure candidate coverage on valid representatives, false support, false
elimination of valid rivals, premise completeness, output faithfulness and
actual cost. Mere action-label recall does not validate candidate coverage.
Claimwise verifier accuracy does not replace runwise or adaptively selected
error analysis. The theoretical error terms are population quantities; no
numerical bound follows until those quantities are estimated on appropriate
independent data.

The baseline ladder is: a strong reader with structure-preserving closure;
typed scope/version filtering; relation-aware coverage selection; a strong
reranker; a gap/sufficiency controller; and an ambiguity-aware controller.
Give every method the same candidate source pool, available structure, reader,
abstention policy and charged access budget. Add compute-matched semantic
reranking and set-level complementary selection. Separate a supplied-pool
packing diagnostic from source-only discovery. Compare the proposed policy to
the closest available mechanisms, not only to a word-slot coverage strawman.

Fix the primary comparison, practically meaningful effect, sample size method
and decision rule before held-out predictions. Use a separate pilot to estimate
history-level variation and justify power; a recruitment target alone is not
a power calculation. Use history/source-family clustered uncertainty for paired
method differences. Report the tradeoff across budgets and answer coverage,
not a selected best operating point. Preserve failed runs and all amendments.

The paper's positive method claim requires a useful improvement over the
strongest matched baseline with uncertainty that supports the prespecified
effect, without concealing unsupported answers or extra compute. If structural
closure or generic sufficiency solves the same cases at the same cost, the
specialized method claim must be narrowed or abandoned. A formal certificate
and successful authored controls alone do not meet this gate.

\section{Artifact scope and limitations}
The released versioned module \texttt{certificate\_v21.py} evaluates a supplied
atomic witness circuit and searches its finite action subsets. It is not a
source extractor, natural-language entailment model, candidate-coverage oracle,
or completed local model host. Atomic coverage must be derived and validated
upstream; identifiers do not authenticate source meaning. The MILP and restricted
approximation are mathematical specifications; their implementation status is
listed separately from the executable exhaustive solver.

The accompanying \texttt{local\_empirical\_contract\_v21.txt} lists commands,
required adapters, metrics and launch gates. The proof review is a separate
model-role review, not independent human peer review. Automated checks use
authored finite cases and establish implementation behavior only. No native
reader or natural QA experiment is run for this theory checkpoint. Earlier
negative reader and retrieval results remain in the repository unchanged.

The main mathematical limitation is semantic: a finite checker cannot prove
that unrestricted natural-language proposals cover every admissible class,
that source assertions entail a supplied judgment, or that a source is true
in the world. The exact optimizer is exponential. The restricted approximation
requires a complete macro representation, conflict freedom and a declared
additive resource model. Exact tokenizer cost does not generally satisfy that
model. The online policy has no general approximation guarantee. These
limitations define measurable failure modes for the local study rather than
unreported exceptions to a broad guarantee.
