\section{Development source audit and limits}
We recovered twelve previously inspected filings with exact byte identities.
Reconstructing the development census yielded 41 selected same-binding probes
and 87 full-binding groups with different normalized values. All 87 groups had
overlapping signed intervals at their reported precision, with no unequal values
at equal accuracy. Their most precise values were unique. These observations
are consistent with ordinary rounded duplicates; they do not establish a new
conflict-resolution task. Precision-aware duplicate handling is part of the
baseline~\cite{xbrl2025duplicates}. The audit is not full taxonomy validation.

In a separately fixed six-history probe, questions asking for a period's revenue
as displayed in two successive filings were resolved through typed facts and
assistant-reviewed primary-statement structure. Two pairs differed. A further
two-question explanatory probe found an ordinary footnote route for one pair;
the other pair's specific cause remained unproven within the reviewed passages.
The latter does not establish corpus-wide absence of evidence. These are
assistant-reviewed development observations, not independently adjudicated gold
or automatic reader predictions. No retrieval advantage follows.

\section{Prospective evaluation gates}
We prepared 100 complete and 100 deliberately insufficient typed-record controls
with frozen model, prompt, decoding and arithmetic choices. These authored
controls screen scoped extraction and calculation; they cannot establish
competence on natural text and tables. A task-matched natural-evidence reader
set, reviewed references and executed reader gate remain necessary. No new
reader, proposer or verifier performance is reported.

The semantic interface separates observed proposal and verification evidence
from an external coverage audit. Its implemented human-gated arm is a diagnostic;
it supplies neither autonomous coverage discovery nor authenticated semantic
completeness. Software tests cannot discharge those assumptions.

The prospective confirmatory design specifies 200 independent issuer histories,
six scheduled questions per history, a minimum observed five-percentage-point
supported-answer improvement, a positive one-sided paired-history confidence
lower bound, and a five-percent upper limit on history-level unsupported-emission
risk in both primary arms. The effect interval is approximate; the practical
threshold is not a five-point confidence lower bound. Cohort enrollment,
independent references and model execution have not occurred.

Cross-filing statement reconstruction already has direct prior
art~\cite{martin2026reclassified}. The present development evidence does not
establish methodological novelty, empirical superiority or submission readiness.
