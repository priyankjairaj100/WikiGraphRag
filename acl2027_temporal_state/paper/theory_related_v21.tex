\paragraph{Decision classes and adaptive tests.}
EC$^2$ already selects costly tests to distinguish equivalence classes, rather
than every latent hypothesis \citep{golovin2010ec2}. Its formulation can represent
correlated noise through expanded realizations. ECED uses a root-cause model
with specified test likelihoods \citep{chen2017eced}. A learned score over
source-binding proposals does not by itself supply these probability models.
Our joint answer map specializes the class definition; it is not a new
equivalence-class objective.

The expected-cover proof underlying the original adaptive-submodularity
analysis was corrected in the authors' version 5, with a weaker bound under
additional assumptions \citep{golovin2011adaptive}. Later cover results have
their own models and parameters
\citep{esfandiari2021adaptivity,hellerstein2021tight}. We do not transfer the
original EC$^2$ constant to this problem or assert that EC$^2$ itself has been
disproved. More fundamentally, conjunctive source rules need not have the
diminishing returns required by the relevant guarantees.

\paragraph{Logical certificates and finite optimization.}
Test cover reduces relevant distinctions to a pair universe
\citep{debontridder2003testcover}. Abduction-based explanations combine
consistency and entailment and can be computed with exact hitting-set methods
\citep{ignatiev2019abduction}; explanation/counterexample duality is also
established \citep{ignatiev2019duality}. Boolean-function evaluation separates
a certificate's cost from the adaptive cost of finding it
\citep{deshpande2014sbfe}. These precedents rule out claims that minimum
certificates, positive evidence plus exclusion, or offline/online distinctions
are new general principles. Our finite encoding specifies how source anchors,
complete joint answers, conjunctive premises and conflicts enter that contract.

\paragraph{Sufficiency and complementary evidence.}
Sufficient Context considers ambiguity and selective answering
\citep{joren2025sufficient}. S2G-RAG's structured gaps include relations and
evidence spans; its sentence-pointer mechanism retains disambiguating context
\citep{li2026s2g}. RINSE evaluates evidence adequacy before answer generation
\citep{chen2026rinse}. Therefore a generic sufficiency baseline can request the
same table, definition or scope witness as our proposed controller.

The Missing Complement explicitly represents alternatives within evidence
requirements and conjunction across requirements, and distinguishes selection
from source discovery \citep{feng2026missing}. Its coding-agent setting differs
from source-scoped factual answers, but conjunctive evidence recovery and
set-level complement acquisition are direct prior mechanisms. The intended
empirical distinction here is whether explicit competing joint answers and
verified rival exclusion improve the allocation of the same evidence and
controller budget.

\paragraph{Ambiguous and conflicting answers.}
Tree of Clarifications, REFUEL and MADAM-RAG already explore interpretations
or resolve conflicting evidence
\citep{kim2023clarifications,gao2021refuel,wang2025madam}. Candidate generation,
pruning and disagreement alone cannot carry our novelty claim. Their
mechanisms should inform matched ambiguity-aware baselines, including cases
where clarification is the appropriate action.

\paragraph{Unresolved related-work scope.}
The indexed primary abstract of ROSE-RAG describes robust sequential evidence
acquisition under source-model ambiguity \citep{mokhtari2026roserag}. Its full
theorem assumptions and proofs were unavailable in this audit. We therefore
record a potential direct overlap rather than claim a verified distinction.
The audit is current to 5 October 2026 and is targeted, not an exhaustive
novelty certification. It must be refreshed before submission.
