\begin{abstract}

We evaluate reader and retrieval changes for versioned-document QA on eight reused questions from two histories. A newer quantized 4B reader passes eight short authored controls, yet ordinary, paired-version, parent-expansion and source-context-closure conditions yield respectively 1, 0, 1 and 1 jointly content-correct, scope-correct and citation-grounded answers out of eight. On sixteen byte-identical contexts, the newer configuration yields one joint success versus two previously. A separate source-only dense/RRF follow-up yields one complete context per eight questions in every new condition, versus two or three for BM25 controls. These negative development results establish neither a reader upgrade nor a retrieval advance. A supplied-judgment witness-selection scaffold and a twelve-object structured-source inventory remain separate feasibility work without natural-data method validation.

\end{abstract}

\section*{1  Scope of this extension}

The preceding v0.13 study did not establish a paired-retrieval advantage. Its primary ordinary and paired conditions each produced one jointly content-correct, scope-correct and citation-grounded answer out of eight. The completed extension tests conventional reader and retrieval changes before pursuing a new method. Its results are negative: the selected reader configuration and the tested dense retrieval do not improve their respective development endpoints.


Document version and assertion scope remain distinct. A later source can revise a result, retain it or change the population and procedure that the result describes. Receiving two values does not by itself justify attributing their difference to a correction. The present task asks for source-grounded reporting without recomputation or outside knowledge.


The corpus and questions remain development material, with earlier outcomes known. Question-to-history membership and within-history version order are supplied. This study therefore evaluates bounded evidence selection and reading within a known history, not document-lineage discovery or open-corpus routing.

\section*{2  Reader selection and the competence gate}

The selected configuration is Qwen3.5-4B in the unsloth Q5\_K\_M distribution, pinned to an immutable repository revision and verified asset hash. Selection preceded v14 answers and reflected reported capability plus CPU and memory feasibility. It remains a quantized four-billion-parameter reader; the newer checkpoint and technical preflight do not establish general reader strength.


The local asset was obtained and hash-verified on a recorded second download attempt after a disk-capacity failure. A native tokenization preflight passed without completion calls. These are execution facts, not semantic competence results. The first failed attempt and cache reconciliation remain in provenance.

\section*{4  Source audits and completed reader outcomes}

The purposive structural audit covers 34 probes on 13 pages and reviews all 72 emitted attachments on those pages. Required relations are complete for 7/17 and missing for 10/17, with no partial cases. No forbidden association is accepted on 15 probes; both locally unresolved probes appropriately remain unresolved.


Of 50 accepted links reviewed, 42 are correct as source context, seven attach excess footer context and one contains mixed scope. None is false under the frozen context-only contract, which does not certify governing semantic bindings. This samples 50 of 692 corpus-wide accepted links. Another 22 links remain unresolved: 17 headers and five continuations. One false table-region candidate has its proposed header withheld. These results permit a bounded context-expansion comparison, not reliable semantic closure.

\section*{5  Dense evidence audit and method boundary}

A separately frozen follow-up evaluates the pinned BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5 encoder and equal-weight BM25/dense reciprocal-rank fusion (k=60). The compact 2023-era encoder is an established baseline, not a current strongest retriever. Each ranker takes three seeds per version, with paired or the unchanged closure representation and the same packing limits. The 48 condition-context cases comprise 16 exact reused BM25 controls and 32 newly reviewed dense/RRF contexts. No natural answer generation was performed.


CLS pooling, L2 normalization, a query-only prefix and 512-token encoder truncation were fixed before retrieval. Four of 2,306 passage encodings truncate; no query or delivered source evidence is truncated. Local timing is descriptive, not a speed comparison. The external dense audit passes 983 checks, including final token revalidation.

\section*{6  Novelty boundary and discriminating baselines}

The bounded primary-source audit establishes substantial overlap with prior work. Table/header preservation, version filters, structured gaps, source pointers, sufficiency judgments, multiple interpretations and abstention are not claimed as inventions. The closest inspected additional mechanisms are summarized below; full citation metadata and search provenance accompany the project.


HC-RAG (Chen et al., 2026) organizes financial filings as document, section, text, table and metadata nodes; company/year matches and structural paths guide retrieval. Its offline table alignment supports cached online units carrying row and column headers, values and units. A query-intent classifier routes text versus tables, and comparison prompts align entities or fiscal periods. The selected-item budget B does not establish exact native-token accounting. These mechanisms directly precede typed evidence graphs, structured numeric units and comparison-aware routing. Sections 3.2-3.6 do not explicitly specify competing attachment hypotheses or separating-witness acquisition; this bounded observation cannot establish novelty. A future comparator must retain its structural and intent-aware mechanisms under our shared resource limits.

Withholding all answers is not success; unsupported-claim rate must be considered together with supported-answer coverage.


If a competent reader with ordinary structure resolves the failures, stop. Also reject gains that vanish against generic sufficiency or ambiguity baselines, matched resources, or removal of gold attachments. HC-RAG is now verified; earlier failed opens remain recorded. \section*{7  Limits, source feasibility and research decision}

The main and dense studies reuse two familiar histories and eight questions with model-assisted references and grading. The structural audit is purposive. Synthetic controls are substantially shorter than natural contexts and do not certify reader competence. Unknown pretraining exposure, quantization, no-thinking execution and the no-recomputation task restrict interpretation. No independent human gold or held-out natural evaluation is established.


Equal caps do not equal equal evidence or compute. Parent expansion drops 19 seeds; closure also changes deduplication and source labels. Dense/RRF uses one compact encoder and one fusion setting. These results reject the tested upgrade routes on development endpoints, not entire method families. A stronger reader and stronger structural/dense comparators remain necessary before attributing residual failures to a new reasoning problem.


A separate source-feasibility cohort contains twelve reconciled HTML objects across six issuer pairs. Berkshire filenames labelled 2022/2023 contain fiscal years 2021/2022; the mismatch is retained. Only five pairs satisfy the intended fiscal-year window. Original SEC identity and historical availability remain unverified. This cohort is now development-exposed; benchmark admission was not assessed, and no new questions or references were created.


An Inline XBRL inventory records 23,651 nonfraction fact occurrences with locally resolvable context/unit links and verifies 32,051 fact/context/unit source anchors. These are not validated distinct quantities: no values were normalized, display fidelity and taxonomy semantics were not established, and hidden/repeated/nil cases remain. This motivates a standard source-structured baseline and display-versus-tag check before any new witness-method claim; issuer tags are not independent gold.
